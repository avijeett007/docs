## What this is for

The **Phone Numbers** section is where you manage the numbers your voice agents use. From here you can:

- Import phone numbers
- Assign a number to a customer
- Assign a number to a specific AI agent
- Control whether a number handles inbound or outbound calls

## Where to find it

Open **Phone Numbers** in the Partner Portal sidebar.

## Who owns a number

A number can belong to either:

- **You (the partner)** — your agency owns it, and you can assign it out to customers.
- **A customer** — it belongs to that customer's account.

Ownership affects billing, permissions, and what you're able to do with the number.

## Import phone numbers

1. Open **Phone Numbers**.
2. Click **Import Numbers**.
3. Follow the steps and confirm.

Once it's done, the number shows up in your list.

## Assign a number to a customer

1. Find the number in the list.
2. Open its actions menu.
3. Choose **Assign to Customer**.
4. Pick the customer and confirm.

## Assign a number to an agent

To route a number to a specific agent:

1. Find the number.
2. Open its actions menu.
3. Choose **Assign Agent**.
4. Select the provider (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, and so on), the agent, and whether it's for inbound or outbound calls.
5. Save.

## Find numbers quickly

When you're managing a lot of numbers, the filters and search make it easy to narrow things down — by number, name, or customer, and by provider, status, ownership, or whether a number is assigned yet.

## What the list shows

Each number in the list shows its details at a glance: the number itself with a friendly name and country flag, the customer it belongs to, the agent it's assigned to (or an **Assign Agent** button if there isn't one yet), whether it handles inbound or outbound calls, its provider, its status, and what it can do (voice, SMS, MMS).

The top of the page also gives you a quick summary — how many numbers you own, how many belong to customers, how many are unassigned, and how many are ready to take calls — along with buttons to import numbers, export your list, and manage number pools.

## Outbound calling with platform numbers

Platform numbers (numbers provisioned through Knotie AI Pro, rather than brought via a BYO SIP trunk) now support outbound calling through the **shared platform SIP trunk**.

When an outbound call is made using a platform number, Connect Hub automatically registers that number as the caller ID on the shared trunk immediately before dialling. Inbound routing is not affected — the shared trunk carries no inbound numbers.

### What this means for you

- You do **not** need to configure SIP trunks manually for your platform numbers.
- Outbound calls via Knova agents now work end-to-end for platform numbers without extra setup in Twilio.
- BYO trunk and Hexai trunk numbers are unaffected and continue to route through their own trunks.

### Required Connect Hub configuration (self-hosted / Coolify installs)

If you run your own Connect Hub instance, set this environment variable and redeploy:

```env
LIVEKIT_SHARED_SIP_OUTBOUND_TRUNK_ID=<your-shared-trunk-id>
```

For **production Connect Hub on Coolify**, use the trunk ID provided during platform setup. Contact support if you have not received one.

## Troubleshooting

- **Calls aren't reaching the agent**
  - Check the number is assigned to the right customer.
  - Check the agent assignment exists and is active.
  - Check the inbound/outbound settings.
- **A number won't delete**
  - Unassign it from any customers and agents first, then try again.
- **Outbound calls from a platform number fail immediately**
  - Confirm `LIVEKIT_SHARED_SIP_OUTBOUND_TRUNK_ID` is set in your Connect Hub environment and the service has been redeployed.
  - If calls fail with a Twilio error 21216 or 32203, the destination number may be fraud-blocked by Twilio. This cannot be resolved from the platform — open a support ticket with Twilio to unblock the affected destination.
- **Outbound calls fail on BYO trunk numbers**
  - BYO trunk numbers do not use the shared trunk; verify your SIP trunk credentials and Twilio termination settings are correct.
