## What Phone Numbers is for

The **Phone Numbers** section helps you manage numbers used by voice agents.

You can:

- Import phone numbers
- Assign a number to a customer
- Assign a number to a specific AI agent
- Control inbound/outbound behavior (based on your provider setup)

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar, open **Phone Numbers**.

## Understand ownership (partner vs customer)

A phone number can be owned by:

- **Partner**: the number belongs to you (the agency) and can be assigned to customers.
- **Customer**: the number belongs to a customer account.

This matters for billing, permissions, and what actions you can take.

## Import phone numbers

1. Go to **Phone Numbers**.
2. Click **Import** (or **Import Numbers**).
3. Follow the import flow and confirm.

After import, you’ll see the number in your list.

## Assign a phone number to a customer

1. Find the number in the list.
2. Open the actions menu.
3. Choose **Assign to Customer**.
4. Pick the customer and confirm.

## Assign a phone number to an agent

If you want a number to route to a specific agent:

1. Find the number.
2. Open the actions menu.
3. Choose **Assign Agent**.
4. Select:
   - Provider (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, etc.)
   - The agent
   - Inbound/outbound preferences (if shown)
5. Save.

## Filter and search

The Phone Numbers page supports filters such as:

- **Search**: number, friendly name, or customer
- **Provider**
- **Status**
- **Assigned vs unassigned**
- **Ownership**: partner vs customer
- **Customer filter**

These filters help when you manage large volumes.

## Troubleshooting

- **Calls aren’t reaching the agent**
  - Confirm the number is assigned to the correct customer.
  - Confirm the agent assignment exists and is active.
  - Check inbound/outbound settings.
- **A number can’t be deleted**
  - Unassign it from customers/agents first, then try again.
