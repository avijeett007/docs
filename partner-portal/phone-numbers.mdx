## Open Phone Numbers

Select **Phone Numbers** in the Partner Portal sidebar. The page shows ownership, customer, billing, assignment, provider, status, and capability filters.

The summary cards show how many numbers are partner-owned, customer-owned, assigned, unassigned, ready for inbound calls, and imported.

## Buy a number

1. Select **Buy Number**.
2. Choose the provider account you want to use.
3. Follow the prompts to connect the account and search for an available number.
4. Review the number and any provider requirements shown in the purchase form.
5. Confirm only when you are ready to buy.

![Provider selection in the Buy a New Number dialog](/images/screenshots/phone-number-buy-modal-2026-09-14.png)

The provider charges your connected account for the purchase. Review the price and recurring cost shown before confirming.

<Note>
Some countries or number types require a registered address or supporting documents. If the purchase form requests them, complete those steps before retrying.
</Note>

## Import existing numbers

1. Select **Import Numbers**.
2. Choose Twilio or Telnyx.
3. Enter the requested account details.
4. Select the numbers to import and confirm.

The imported numbers then appear in the main list. See [Import Telnyx numbers](/agents/knova/telnyx-byo-numbers) for the Telnyx flow.

## Assign a number to a customer

1. Find the number in the list.
2. Open its actions menu.
3. Select the customer assignment action.
4. Choose the customer and save.

## Assign a number to an agent

1. Open the number's actions menu and select **Assign Agent**.
2. Choose the AI agent.
3. Select the available inbound or outbound options.
4. Save.

The list displays the assigned customer and agent, provider, call directions, status, and capabilities.

## Activate phone service

If the page shows **Activate Phone Service**, select **Get Started** and complete the requested business information, documents, and review steps.

See [Activate phone service](/partner-portal/phone-numbers/phone-activation) for the full flow.

## Number pools and exports

- Select **Number Pool** to group numbers for campaign routing.
- Select **Export** to download the number list using the options shown in the portal.

## Troubleshooting

- **A search returns no results:** broaden the country, type, area-code, or digit filters and try again.
- **A purchase cannot continue:** check the provider balance, credentials, and any address or document request shown in the form.
- **Calls do not reach the agent:** confirm the customer and agent assignments and the selected call direction.
- **A number cannot be removed:** unassign it from customers and agents first.
