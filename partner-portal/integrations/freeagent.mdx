## What FreeAgent is

**FreeAgent** is a cloud accounting platform built for freelancers and small businesses. It covers invoicing, expense tracking, bank reconciliation, tax returns, and more. When a customer connects FreeAgent through their portal, their AI agent gains access to 76 accounting tools — for example, listing invoices, creating expense claims, or querying contacts — directly during a conversation.

<Note>
FreeAgent is currently in **beta**. The OAuth connection flow is fully wired and the tools are available, but end-to-end live testing is ongoing. Enable it for customers you can monitor during their first few sessions.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- The customer must have a **FreeAgent account** (paid plan or trial at freeagent.com).
- The partner must have **FreeAgent enabled** in the customer's Allowed Apps list (see below).

---

## Enabling FreeAgent for a customer

FreeAgent is a **Premium** integration. Enable it the same way as any other app:

1. Go to **Customers** → select the customer → **Manage**.
2. Open the **Features & Add-ons** tab.
3. Make sure **Show Integration** is toggled ON (under **Menu Visibility**).
4. Click **Select Apps** next to **Allowed Apps**.
5. In the modal, find **FreeAgent** under **Premium Apps** and check the box.
6. Click **Save**.

## How the customer connects FreeAgent

Once you've enabled FreeAgent for a customer, they connect it themselves from their portal:

1. The customer opens **Integration** in their portal.
2. They find the **FreeAgent** card and click **Connect**.
3. They are redirected to the FreeAgent login page to authorize access.
4. After authorizing, they are returned to the portal and the card shows **Connected**.

The connection is OAuth2-managed by Knotie's Connect Hub — the customer does not need to copy an API key.

<Note>
If the FreeAgent OAuth page doesn't appear, ask the customer to clear their browser cookies for freeagent.com and try again.
</Note>

## Available tools

FreeAgent exposes **76 tools** through the Composio integration layer. These cover:

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Invoices | List invoices, create invoice, update invoice status |
| Expenses | Create expense, list expenses, categorize expense |
| Contacts | List contacts, get contact, create contact |
| Bank transactions | List transactions, reconcile transaction |
| Timeslips | Create timeslip, list timeslips |
| Projects | List projects, get project details |

The full tool list is resolved automatically when the connection is active — no manual configuration is needed.

## Agent usage

Once the customer's FreeAgent account is connected, their AI agent can call FreeAgent tools during conversations without any extra setup. For example, a customer could ask their agent:

> "Show me all unpaid invoices"  
> "Log a £45 meal expense for today"  
> "Create a new invoice for £500 to Acme Ltd"

The agent handles the tool call and returns the result as part of the conversation.

---

## Disconnecting FreeAgent

If a customer wants to remove the FreeAgent connection:

1. The customer opens **Integration** in their portal.
2. They click the **FreeAgent** card (showing **Connected**).
3. They click **Disconnect** and confirm.

The OAuth authorization is revoked and the tools are no longer available to the agent.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| FreeAgent card not visible in the portal | App not enabled for this customer | Add FreeAgent to the customer's Allowed Apps list |
| OAuth redirect fails or shows an error | Browser cookies or redirect mismatch | Ask the customer to clear freeagent.com cookies and try a private window |
| "Connected" but tools don't appear in agent calls | Connection may be stale | Ask the customer to disconnect and reconnect; then run a test call |
| Agent calls the wrong FreeAgent tool | Prompt needs more specificity | Refine the agent prompt to name the exact action (e.g., "list unpaid invoices" rather than "check invoices") |
