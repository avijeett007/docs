## What audiences are

An audience is a saved list of customers or companies you want to review or contact. The list can come from a discovery engine or from a finding in Insights Chat.

![Audiences screen in the development portal](/images/screenshots/customer-insights-audiences-dev.png)

When no lists have been saved, the page shows: **Select an audience to view its members.**

## Creating an audience

1. Open a completed discovery run or an Insights Chat finding.
2. Choose **Save as Audience**.
3. Enter a name.
4. Save the list.

## Viewing an audience

Open **Customer Insights → Audiences**, then select a saved list to view its members and source.

## Exporting a list

If the list includes an **Export CSV** action, use it to download the audience for review in your email or customer-management tool. Review any generated message before sending it.

Audiences are saved snapshots. They do not update automatically when customer data changes.
