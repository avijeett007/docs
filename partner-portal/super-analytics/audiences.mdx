## What audiences are

An **Audience** is a saved list of customers, partners, or companies that you want to target. You create audiences from:

- An **Engine run** result (e.g. the Churn Risk engine flagged 12 customers — save them as "July At-Risk List")
- A **Chat finding** (e.g. the Chat surfaced 5 companies visiting your pricing page — save them as "Hot Prospects Week 28")

Each audience member gets a **reason** (why they were included) and, where the engine wrote one, a **draft outreach message** personalised to their situation.

## Creating an audience

### From an engine run

1. Go to **Super Analytics → Engines**.
2. Run an engine (or open a previous run).
3. In the run results, click **Save as Audience**.
4. Give the audience a name (e.g. "Churn Risk — July 2026").
5. Click **Save**. The audience appears in the **Audiences** tab.

### From a Chat finding

1. Go to **Super Analytics → Chat**.
2. Ask a question that returns a list of customers or companies.
3. If the AI's answer includes a list, click **Save as Audience** below the response.
4. Give the audience a name and click **Save**.

---

## Viewing an audience

1. Go to **Super Analytics → Audiences**.
2. Click any audience to see:
   - The list of members (customer name, company, the reason they were included)
   - Draft outreach messages for each member (where the engine generated one)
   - When the audience was created and from which source

---

## Exporting to CSV

1. Open an audience.
2. Click **Export CSV**.
3. The downloaded file includes: member name, email (where available), company, reason, and draft outreach message.

Use the CSV with any email tool, CRM, or ad platform that accepts a contact list upload.

<Warning>
  **No auto-send in v1.** Super Analytics exports lists but does not send messages automatically. Always review the draft messages before using them — they are AI-generated starting points, not final copy. Automated sending (via bulk email or CRM sequences) is a planned follow-up feature.
</Warning>

---

## Audience limits

There is no hard limit on the number of audiences you can save. However, audiences are point-in-time snapshots — they don't update automatically as your customer data changes. Re-run the relevant engine and save a new audience when you want a fresh list.
