  **Super Analytics is a Level 6 feature.** Reach Level 6 in the Partner Level System to unlock the partner-facing view. Platform admins can also enable it per partner directly from Mission Control.
</Warning>

## What Super Analytics is

**Super Analytics** is a customer discovery and intelligence engine built into Knotie AI Pro. It runs structured analytics playbooks — called **Engines** — over your real usage data, lets you ask free-form questions in natural language through a built-in **Chat** interface, and lets you save any finding as a named **Audience** (retargeting list) you can export to CSV.

Think of it as an analytics brain that knows your customer data and can answer questions like:

- *"Which of my customers are at risk of churning?"*
- *"Which companies have multiple users — and aren't on a team plan yet?"*
- *"Which websites visited my pricing pages this week?"*
- *"Who are my power users, and which ones should I reach out to first?"*

There are two versions of Super Analytics:

| View | Who sees it | Where |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Mission Control** | Platform owner (admin) | `/mission-control/super-analytics` — analytics across ALL partners |
| **Partner view** | Partner (when enabled) | Partner Portal → **Super Analytics** — analytics scoped to that partner's own customers |

## Access and availability

### For the platform owner (Mission Control)

The **Super Analytics** entry appears in the Mission Control sidebar automatically. No level requirement, no toggle needed. Use the **Scope picker** at the top of the page to view analytics platform-wide or drill into a single partner.

You can also reach a partner-scoped view directly from any partner's detail page in Mission Control — click the **Super Analytics** button at the top of the page.

### For partners

Partners see Super Analytics in their sidebar only after **both** conditions are met:

1. They have reached **Level 6** in the Partner Level System, or
2. An admin has enabled `superAnalyticsEnabled` for their account from Mission Control → Partner → toggle.

When enabled, the partner's Super Analytics is hard-scoped to their own customers and data — they cannot see other partners' data.

<Note>
  `superAnalyticsEnabled` defaults to **OFF**. No partner sees Super Analytics until explicitly unlocked, either by reaching Level 6 or by admin toggle.
</Note>

## What's in Super Analytics

Super Analytics has five tabs:

| Tab | What it does |
|-----|-------------|
| **Overview** | Summary cards: last run per engine, audience count, recent chat sessions |
| **Engines** | Run any of the 6 analytics engines; review saved runs and their AI-written narratives |
| **Audiences** | View and export saved audience lists created from engine runs or chat findings |
| **Deep Research** | AI-powered research reports on specific subjects (a customer, a domain, a company) |
| **Chat** | Ask free-form questions about your data; the AI answers using real-time queries against your database, never hallucinated SQL |

---

## Privacy and data handling

- **Visitor IP addresses are never stored raw.** They are hashed with a stable secret salt (`TRACKING_IP_SALT`) before being written to the database.
- **DNT (Do Not Track) is respected** — visitors who set the DNT browser header are not tracked.
- **Tenant isolation is enforced at the query layer**, not the prompt layer. Every tool the Chat AI can call is scoped to the active partner — no prompt can expand the scope.

## Configuration (self-hosted / advanced)

The following environment variables control Super Analytics behaviour. None of them are required to run the feature — the defaults are production-safe.

| Variable | Default | Description |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| `SUPER_ANALYTICS_TRACKING_ENABLED` | `true` | Global kill-switch for the public `/api/public/track` ingest endpoint. Set to `"false"` to disable all visitor tracking instantly without a deploy. |
| `SUPER_ANALYTICS_PLATFORM_TRACKING` | `false` | Whether to record visitor events on the platform's own domains (not whitelabelled partners). Off by default — opt in explicitly. |
| `TRACKING_IP_SALT` | _(empty)_ | Salt for IP hashing. Set a stable random secret. Rotating it invalidates historical IP hashes (visitors are still tracked; IP correlation across periods is just broken). |
| `SUPER_ANALYTICS_MODELS` | `claude-sonnet-5,gpt-5.1` | Comma-separated ordered fallback list of LLM model IDs for engine narration and chat. First model that succeeds wins. |
| `SUPER_ANALYTICS_INPROCESS_MAX` | `2` | Max concurrent in-process Deep Research runs. If KnotieManager is down, overflow is deferred to the scheduled queue rather than blocking the main app. Min: 1. |
