## What Customer Insights does

Customer Insights helps you find useful patterns in your customer data. It brings together repeatable discovery tools, saved customer lists, and a question-and-answer workspace.

The partner view is limited to your own customers and workspace data.

![Customer Insights engines screen in the development portal](/images/screenshots/customer-insights-engines-dev.png)

## Available sections

| Section | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Overview** | Shows visitor-intent activity, daily usage or spend, and recent runs. |
| **Engines** | Runs six ready-made discovery checks. |
| **Runs** | Keeps completed checks so you can open them again. |
| **Audiences** | Shows saved customer lists. |
| **Chat** | Lets you ask questions about your customer data. |
| **Deep Research** | Builds a research report about one of your customers. |

## Getting access

Customer Insights appears in the Partner Portal after you reach Level 6, or after your account manager enables it for your workspace. If it is not enabled, the page explains how to unlock it.
