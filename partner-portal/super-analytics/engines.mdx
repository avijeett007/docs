## What discovery engines are

Discovery engines are ready-made checks over your customer data. Each card explains what it finds and includes a **Run engine** button.

![Discovery engine cards in the development portal](/images/screenshots/customer-insights-engines-dev.png)

## The six engines

### Power Users

Finds the customers using the platform most actively and highlights recent champions.

### Account Mapping

Groups users by company domain to help you spot businesses with several separate accounts.

### Churn Risk

Highlights customers showing signs of reduced activity, falling usage, or low credit balance.

### Upsell Opportunities

Finds customers whose current usage suggests a useful next feature or plan.

### Visitor Intent

Shows which companies are visiting your tracked pages, which pages they view, and whether they return.

### Revenue Trends

Shows usage and spending movement over time, split into growth and decline.

## Running an engine

1. Open **Customer Insights → Engines**.
2. Choose an engine.
3. Select the **Partner** view when it is available.
4. Click **Run engine**.
5. Open the saved run when it finishes.

Runs are kept in the **Runs** section so you can review them later.
