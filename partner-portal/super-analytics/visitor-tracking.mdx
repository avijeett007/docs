## What Visitor Intent does

Visitor Intent is one of the discovery engines. It highlights companies visiting your tracked pages, the pages they view, and whether they return.

The engine needs visitor activity before it can show useful results. If there is no activity yet, the overview page asks you to run it to populate the cards.

## Using Visitor Intent

1. Open **Customer Insights → Engines**.
2. Choose **Visitor Intent**.
3. Select the **Partner** view.
4. Click **Run engine**.
5. Review the companies, pages, and repeat visits in the result.

Visitor Intent is a signal for prioritising follow-up. It is not a replacement for confirming a customer’s interest directly.
