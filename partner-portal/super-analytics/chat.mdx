## What Insights Chat does

Insights Chat lets you ask questions about your customer data in plain language.

Examples:

- *Which customers had the most calls last month?*
- *Which customers signed up recently but have not made a call?*
- *Which customers may need a check-in?*
- *Who are my top customers by credit spend?*

![Insights Chat screen in the development portal](/images/screenshots/customer-insights-chat-dev.png)

The page includes **Sessions** for past conversations and a **New** action to start again. When there are no past conversations, it shows **No past sessions.**

## Saving a finding

When a response contains a useful customer list, use **Save as Audience** if it is available. Give the list a name so you can find it in **Audiences** later.

## Limits

Chat can answer questions only from the customer data available in your workspace. It cannot look up information from an outside customer-management tool unless that information is already connected to the workspace.
