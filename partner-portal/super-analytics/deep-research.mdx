## What Deep Research does

Deep Research creates an account-intelligence report for one of your customers. It is useful when you want more context before an outreach or account review.

![Deep Research screen in the development portal](/images/screenshots/customer-insights-deep-research-dev.png)

The page currently provides:

- **New research**
- **Researching one of your customers**
- A **Pick a customer…** selector
- **Run Deep Research** for an immediate report
- An option to **schedule for overnight batch**
- **Recent reports** for previously created reports

## Starting a report

1. Open **Customer Insights → Deep Research**.
2. Select a customer from **Pick a customer…**.
3. Choose **Run Deep Research** or schedule it for the overnight batch.
4. Return to **Recent reports** to open the result.

Reports may take time to appear. Leave the page and return later if a report is still being prepared.
