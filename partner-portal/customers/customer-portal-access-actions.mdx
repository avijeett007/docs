## Open the access controls

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Select **Manage** for the customer.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.
4. Scroll to **Customer Portal Access**.

The production panel shows **Preview Portal**, **Reset Password**, **Block Access**, and **Delete**.

## Preview the portal

Select **Preview Portal** to open the customer experience in a new window. The confirmation message tells you when the temporary preview expires.

Allow pop-ups for your portal if the new window does not open. Preview mode is for checking the customer experience; password changes are not available from this temporary session.

## Help a customer with their password

Customers can change their own password from their portal settings. The signed-in flow asks for the current password before accepting a new one.

If a customer cannot sign in, ask them to use the password-reset link on the sign-in page. You can also select **Reset Password** in the customer's access controls.

<Note>
A first sign-in may require the customer to replace a temporary password before continuing.
</Note>

## Block or remove access

- **Block Access** prevents the customer from signing in until access is restored.
- **Delete** removes the customer's portal access. Use it only when the account should no longer be available.

## Sign-in troubleshooting

If the customer signs in and immediately returns to the sign-in page:

1. Confirm **Enable Customer Portal** is on for that customer.
2. Confirm the branded portal and its domain are enabled under **Settings → White-Label Portal**.
3. Open the secure portal address, beginning with https://, in a fresh browser window and try again.
4. Clear saved site data for the portal or try a private window.
5. If the issue continues, select **Get Support** and include the portal address and the time of the attempt. Never send the customer's password.
