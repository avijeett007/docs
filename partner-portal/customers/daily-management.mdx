## What Daily Management is for

The **Daily Management** tab helps you keep track of day-to-day operations for each customer. Use it to monitor activity, track tasks, and stay on top of customer needs.

## Where to find it

<Warning>
The Customer Manage modal lists 9 tabs — Customer Info, Deal Management, Features & Add-ons, Billing, Phone Numbers, Credits, Embed URLs, Deployment, Reports. **"Daily Management" is not one of them in the current build.** The closest matches are **Customer Info** (recent activity / notes) and **Deployment** (status timeline). If you relied on a Daily Management tab, look for its fields under Customer Info or Deployment instead.
</Warning>

1. Open **Customers** (sidebar — expands to Active Customers + Prospects).
2. Select a customer.
3. Click **Manage**.
4. Use the **Customer Info** tab for activity/notes; **Deployment** for the milestone timeline.

## What you can track

The Daily Management tab provides visibility into:

- **Recent activity** – See what's happened with this customer recently.
- **Task tracking** – Monitor ongoing tasks and their status.
- **Notes and updates** – Keep a log of interactions and important information.
- **Alerts and notifications** – View any warnings or action items.

## Common use cases

### Morning check-ins

Start your day by reviewing Daily Management for your active customers:

1. Open each customer's Daily Management tab.
2. Check for any alerts or issues.
3. Review recent activity.
4. Plan your follow-ups.

### Customer support

When a customer reaches out with an issue:

1. Open their Daily Management tab.
2. Review recent activity for context.
3. Add notes about the support interaction.
4. Track resolution status.

### Team handoffs

When handing off a customer to a teammate:

1. Review the Daily Management tab together.
2. Point out any ongoing issues or tasks.
3. Add handoff notes for context.

## Best practices

- **Check daily** – Make Daily Management part of your morning routine.
- **Add notes** – Document important interactions and decisions.
- **Set alerts** – Configure notifications for critical thresholds.
- **Review trends** – Look for patterns in customer activity.
