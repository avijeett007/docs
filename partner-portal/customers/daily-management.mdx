## Keeping track day to day

There isn't a single "daily management" screen. Instead, the two places you'll check most often to stay on top of a customer are the **Customer Info** tab and the **Deployment** tab, both inside a customer's Manage window.

- **Customer Info** — recent activity, contact details, and the notes you've kept about your interactions with them.
- **Deployment** — the setup status and a timeline of milestones, so you can see where they are in getting up and running.

## Where to find them

1. Open **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Select a customer.
3. Click **Manage**.
4. Use **Customer Info** for activity and notes, and **Deployment** for setup progress.

## A simple daily routine

- **Start with a quick review** — open your active customers and glance at recent activity and setup status.
- **Add notes as you go** — after a call or an email, jot down what happened in Customer Info so nothing gets lost.
- **Hand off cleanly** — when a teammate takes over a customer, your notes give them the context they need.
