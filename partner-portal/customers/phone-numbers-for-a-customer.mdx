title: "Phone numbers for a customer"
description: "Assign and manage phone numbers for a specific customer (requires customer portal enabled)."
---

## Where to find it

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Select a customer.
3. Open **Manage**.
4. Go to **Phone Numbers**.

## Customer portal requirement

The Phone Numbers tab requires the customer portal to be enabled.

If portal access is not enabled, the UI shows a message like:

- **Customer Portal Required**

and provides an **Enable Customer Portal** button.

## What you can do

Inside the Phone Numbers tab you can:

- View customer-assigned numbers
- Assign a number to the customer
- Assign a number to an agent (via selection modals)
