## Overview

When you run the **Resell Voice AI** experience, your customers use your platform like a SaaS product — they build their own agents, hold phone numbers, and review campaign results. **Customer campaign authoring** takes that one step further: customers can create, start, and pause their own outbound campaigns without you doing it for them.

This is separate from the standard `showCampaigns` grant (which gives read-only access). Campaign authoring is an explicit, opt-in capability you grant per customer.

## Prerequisites

Before enabling campaign authoring for a customer:

1. Your partner account must have the **Resell Voice AI** experience active.
2. The customer must have **Campaigns** enabled (`showCampaigns`). Authoring requires campaigns to be visible first.
3. The customer must have at least one **active phone number** assigned to them. A customer with no numbers cannot create a campaign — there is nothing to dial from.
4. The customer must have at least one **outbound AI agent** configured. Only outbound agents can run campaigns.

---

## Enabling campaign authoring for a customer

1. Open **Partner Portal → Customers**.
2. Select the customer.
3. Click **Manage**.
4. Go to **Features & Add-ons**.
5. Under **Outbound Campaigns**, enable the **Allow Campaign Authoring** toggle.
6. Save.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the "Allow Campaign Authoring" toggle in the customer's Features & Add-ons modal._

Once enabled, the customer's portal gains **Create Campaign** access. They can create, start, and pause their own campaigns. Cancel and delete remain partner-only actions in this version.

<Note>
  The **Allow Campaign Authoring** toggle is only shown when the customer's **Campaigns** (`showCampaigns`) feature is already on. Enable that first if the toggle is not visible.
</Note>

## Creating a campaign (customer view)

The customer's campaign creation wizard has four steps:

1. **Basics** — Campaign name and description.
2. **Agent** — Pick from their own outbound agents. Inbound agents are not shown.
3. **Prospects** — Upload a CSV or add contacts by hand. Per-call variables (e.g., `offer_name`, `renewal_date`) can be set here for each prospect.
4. **Schedule** — Timezone, calling window, pace (calls per hour). If you have set a concurrency ceiling, it is shown inline next to the pace field.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the four-step campaign creation wizard in the customer portal._

Pool selection is automatic — the system uses a number pool you assigned to the customer, or falls back to their active phone numbers. Customers are not asked to choose.

## Partner-set ceilings

To protect your trunk capacity and caller-ID reputation, you can cap how aggressively a customer's campaign can run.

| Setting | Where to configure | Default |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| Max concurrent calls | Partner Portal → Customers → Manage → Campaign Settings | None (no cap) |
| Max calls per day | Partner Portal → Customers → Manage → Campaign Settings | None (no cap) |

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the customer campaign ceiling settings in the partner portal._

When a customer tries to set a pace above your ceiling, the campaign is **refused** with a clear message naming the limit — they are not silently slowed down. The ceiling is shown inline in the wizard's Schedule step so customers know the limit before they reach it.

<Warning>
  Ceilings only apply to customer-authored campaigns. Your own partner-run campaigns are never constrained by these values.
</Warning>

## Phone number resolution

A customer-authored campaign dials from:

1. A **Number Pool** you have assigned to that customer (if one exists), or
2. The customer's own **active phone numbers** (used as an implicit pool).

If the customer has neither, the campaign create request is refused with an error: _"Buy or assign a phone number before creating a campaign."_ Customers are linked to the Numbers page directly.

---

## Tenant isolation

Customer campaigns are scoped strictly to that customer:

- A customer can only see, start, and pause campaigns they created.
- Attempting to access another customer's campaign returns `404` — not `403`. Campaign existence is not disclosed across customer boundaries.
- Partner credentials are never reused for customer operations. Each customer acts on their own session only.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| "Allow Campaign Authoring" toggle not visible | `showCampaigns` is off for this customer | Enable Campaigns first under Features & Add-ons |
| Customer cannot create a campaign after toggle is on | No active phone numbers or no outbound agents | Assign a phone number or create an outbound agent |
| Create request fails with "ceiling exceeded" | Customer's concurrency or daily-calls setting exceeds the partner ceiling | Lower the value or raise the partner ceiling |
| Campaign created but nothing dials | No numbers resolved at dial time | Check the customer has active numbers, not just assigned |
