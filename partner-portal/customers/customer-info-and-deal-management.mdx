## Where to find it

1. Open **Customers** in the Partner Portal sidebar (expands to **Active Customers** + **Prospects** — the page most actions live on is **Active Customers**).
2. Click **Manage** on a customer's card.
3. A modal opens with **9 tabs**: Customer Info, Deal Management, Features & Add-ons, Billing, Phone Numbers, Credits, Embed URLs, Deployment, and Reports.
4. Navigate between tabs to access different settings.

## Customer Info tab

Use **Customer Info** to update customer data like:

- First name, last name, email
- Company name
- Business phone
- Business details (monthly call volume, primary use case, CRM system, phone system)
- **Call Complexity** (Simple / Medium / Complex) — a dropdown not previously documented
- Deployment timeline

## Account management fields

The modal includes fields such as:

- **Processing Status**
  - Submitted
  - Processing
  - Additional Info Requested
  - Under Review
  - Regulatory approval submitted
  - Regulatory approval completed
  - Business Agreement Established
  - Under Development
  - System under review
  - Voice AI Agent Live
- **Custom Automation**
  - A notes field for custom automation requirements.

## Pricing breakdown (read-only)

The UI shows a **Pricing Breakdown** panel including:

- Total Estimated Cost
- Platform Fee
- Voice AI Cost
- Telephony Cost
- Email Cost
- SMS Cost

This is useful for internal estimation and margin planning. All values are computed, not editable.

## Deal Management tab

Use **Deal Management** to control sales-stage fields like:

- **Deal Status**
  - Pending
  - Agreed
  - Failed
- **Billing Type**
  - Monthly Recurring
  - One-time Payment
- **Agreed Price** — your signed pricing field
- **Estimated Price** — read-only field showing computed $0

Notes:

- “Deal Notes” is currently marked as **Coming soon**.

## Best practices

- Treat “Agreed Price” as your signed pricing field.
- Keep “Processing Status” updated so your team knows where the customer is in the delivery pipeline.
- Use the full list of 10 Processing Status options for precise workflow automation.
