title: "Choose customer integrations"

---

## Open the app selector

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Find the customer and click **Manage**.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.
4. Turn on **Integration** under **Menu Visibility Options**.
5. Click **Edit Apps**.

## Choose apps

The selector groups apps as **Free**, **Premium**, and **Standard**. Some apps also carry **Beta** or **Alpha** labels so you can set expectations with the customer.

![Customer app selector showing Free and Premium groups, selection controls, and status labels](/images/screenshots/integration-app-selection-production-2026-09-12.png)

Use **Select All**, **Select All Free**, **Select Premium**, or **Clear All** to make a broad change. You can also select individual apps.

Click **Save Selection** when the list is ready. The number beside **Integration** shows how many apps the customer can access.

<Note>
Turning on the Integration menu and choosing apps are separate steps. Complete both so the customer can find the integrations you selected.
</Note>

## Change access later

Return to **Features & Add-ons → Edit Apps**, update the selection, and save again. Removing an app from the allowed list removes it from the customer's available choices.

## Microsoft Outlook

**Outlook** is in the Premium Apps list with a **Beta** label. It lets agents send and manage email and calendar events in Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com accounts.

Select Outlook and save the app list to make it available. The customer then connects their Microsoft account from their portal, using Microsoft's own sign-in page.

<Note>
Outlook and Gmail are separate apps. Customers on Microsoft 365 should connect Outlook; customers on Google Workspace should connect Gmail. You can offer both. For other email providers, offer [Email (any mailbox)](/agents/agent-mailbox).
</Note>

## FreeAgent

**FreeAgent** appears in the Premium Apps list with a **Beta** label. It is described in the portal as accounting, invoicing, and expense support for freelancers and small businesses.

![FreeAgent in the customer integration app picker](/images/screenshots/customer-app-picker-freeagent-production-2026-09-14.png)

Select FreeAgent and save the app list to make it available to the customer. The customer completes the connection from their branded portal using FreeAgent's secure sign-in page.

<Note>
Selecting an app controls availability. The customer must still connect their own account before an agent can use it.
</Note>

## Give many customers the same apps

Instead of choosing apps for each customer, you can set one list for all of them in **Settings → Integration Pricing → Apps for your customers**. See [Apps for your customers](/partner-portal/settings/integration-pricing#apps-for-your-customers).

A list you choose here for one customer always takes priority over that shared list.

## What customers see with no apps

If Integration is on for a customer but no apps have been chosen for them, their Integration page says **No apps are enabled for your account yet**, with a **Request an app** button. Requests are emailed to you — see [Integration app requests](/partner-portal/customers/integration-app-requests).

![The customer Integration page with no apps enabled, showing the Request an app button](/images/screenshots/customer-integration-no-apps-2026-09-29.png)

## Integration works in every dashboard mode

You can offer Integration whichever dashboard mode your portal uses. In [Platform configuration](/partner-portal/settings/platform-configuration), **Integration** and **MCP Access & Approvals** have their own **Integrations & Approvals** group in the default menu.

## Free Forever plan

Partners on the Free Forever plan can only offer free apps. Other apps show as not available on the plan until the partner upgrades.

## If a customer cannot find an app

- Confirm **Integration** is turned on for that customer.
- Confirm the app is selected in **Edit Apps**, or is on your [Apps for your customers](/partner-portal/settings/integration-pricing#apps-for-your-customers) list.
- Check the app's plan group and its Alpha or Beta label.
- Ask the customer to refresh their portal after you save.
