## Where to find it

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Select a customer.
3. Open **Manage**.
4. Use:
   - **Credits** tab (credit management)
   - **Features & Add-ons** (AI credit toggle + alerts)

## Credits tab

The **Credits** tab provides customer-level credit management.

Use it to:

- View customer credit status
- Apply credits or adjustments (as supported)

## AI Credits Enabled (feature toggle)

In **Features & Add-ons**, you can enable:

- **AI Credits Enabled**

When enabled:

- The UI describes this as “Charge customer using AI Credits instead of dollar pricing.”

## Low credit notifications

When AI credits are enabled, you can configure:

- **Enable Notifications**
- **Low Credit Threshold**

This sends whitelabel email alerts when credits reach the threshold.

## Important interaction: pricing visibility

If AI credits are enabled, pricing display may be disabled.

The UI indicates:

- “Show Pricing Information” is disabled when AI credits are enabled.
