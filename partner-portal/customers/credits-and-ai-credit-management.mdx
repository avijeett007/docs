## Where to find it

1. Open **Customers** (sidebar — expands to Active Customers + Prospects).
2. Select a customer.
3. Click **Manage**.
4. Select the **Credits** tab.
5. Open **Manage** to configure customer credit settings.

## Credits tab

The **Credits** tab provides customer-level credit management.

Use it to:

- View customer credit status
- Apply credits or adjustments (where supported)

## AI Credits Enabled (feature toggle)

In **Features & Add-ons**, you can enable **AI Credits**.

When enabled:

- The UI displays: _“Charge customer using AI Credits instead of dollar pricing.”_
- Low-credit notifications can be configured.

When AI credits are enabled, you can configure:

- **Enable Notifications**
- **Low Credit Threshold**

This sends whitelabel email alerts when credits reach the threshold.

## Important interaction: pricing visibility

When AI credits are enabled, pricing display may be disabled.

The UI indicates that **“Show Pricing Information”** is disabled while AI credits are enabled.
