## What customer management is

In Knotie AI PRO, **Customer Management** is everything you can control for a specific customer after they’ve been created.

Most partner actions live in the **Customer List** page under a customer’s **Manage** modal.

## Customer List: Two tabs

The **Customer List** page has two main tabs:

- **Customers** – Active customers who have completed onboarding
- **Prospects** – Leads from the AI Receptionist who are still in the onboarding process (Enterprise feature)

## Understanding the onboarding flow

### How prospects become customers

1. **Prospect creation** – When someone interacts with your AI Receptionist, they are automatically added as a Prospect.
2. **Onboarding journey begins** – The prospect starts the 10-step onboarding process.
3. **Conversion at Step 3** – Once a prospect completes **Step 3** of the onboarding journey, they are converted from a **Prospect** into a **Customer**.
4. **Payment at Step 9** – The customer reviews the plan and pays via Stripe Checkout (subscription, fixed price, or pay-as-you-go). Free Forever and pay-as-you-go portals skip checkout.
5. **Full activation at Step 10** – Once payment has cleared, the customer clicks **Deploy** on Step 10. The AI Agent/Receptionist is **automatically turned on** and ready to handle calls.

### The 10 onboarding steps

> This 10-step flow is available only when your portal is in **SaaS mode**.

1. Business Info
2. Website Verification
3. Contact Details
4. Service Categories
5. Knowledge Base
6. Greeting Setup
7. Information Collection
8. Communication Settings
9. Pricing & Payment
10. Deploy Agent

![Onboarding Steps Progress](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-200344.png)

> Step 10 is gated by `/api/whitelabel/check-onboarding-payment`. If the customer arrives at Step 10 without an `active` credential, they're sent back to Step 9 to complete payment.

## Where to find customer actions

You’ll typically use one of these locations:

- **Customer List** →**Active Customers** → **Manage**
  - This opens a multi-tab management modal.
- **Customers → Customer Portal Management**
  - This is for creating customer login credentials and sending invitation emails.

## What you can manage per customer

The customer management modal is organized into tabs:

- **Customer Info**
- **Deal Management**
- **Features & Add-ons**
- **Billing**
- **Phone Numbers**
- **Credits**
- [Embed URLs](/partner-portal/customers/embed-urls) – Generate customer-scoped tokens and URLs for widgets, iframes, and embedded experiences
- **Tool Call Quota** (only appears if **Integration** is enabled)
- **Deployment**

## Common prerequisites

Some features require setup elsewhere:

- **Customer Portal actions** require your **Whitelabel portal** to be configured.
- **Billing** inside the customer modal requires **Stripe Connect**.
- **API Keys menu visibility** requires **API Access** to be enabled for the customer.
- [Embed URLs](/partner-portal/customers/embed-urls) are typically configured after customer portal access is enabled and the customer is ready for embedded experiences.
