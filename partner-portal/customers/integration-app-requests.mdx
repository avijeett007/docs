## What app requests are

When a customer opens the Integrations page and finds an app they want but cannot access — either because it isn't in their allowed list or their plan doesn't include it — they can now click **Request an app** and tell you what they need.

Each request:

- Generates a reference code the customer can quote when following up (e.g., `APP-7K3Q9D`)
- Sends three emails: a confirmation to the customer, a notification to you (the partner), and an alert to Knotie support
- Is stored so you can review it at any time

---

## Where customers see the request button

The **Request an app** button appears in two places on the customer's Integrations page:

1. **Empty state** — when the customer has no available apps at all, a prompt appears beside **Refresh** inviting them to request one.
2. **"Not on your plan" notice** — when an app is blocked by the plan, the notice includes a direct request link with that app pre-selected.

## What the request form offers

The form lists only the apps this customer **cannot currently use** — resolved through the same access check the portal enforces for connections. It never advertises an app the customer could already connect.

Apps the customer could pay to unlock on their own are omitted (they can just buy access). The customer can also choose **Something else…** and describe what they need in their own words.

## The three emails

| Recipient | Sent from | What it says |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Customer** | Your agency's branded email (your domain and SMTP/SES settings) | Confirmation, their reference code (`APP-XXXXXX`), what they asked for |
| **You (partner)** | Knotie platform email | Who asked, which app, their note, and a link to the customer record; if the app is blocked by your own plan, an upgrade suggestion |
| **Knotie support** | Knotie platform email | Customer, partner, app, details, reference |

Only the customer email uses your branding and is billed at the usual credit rate for partner-attributed sends. The other two use Knotie's sender identity.

<Note>
If one email fails to send, the request is still stored and the customer still receives their reference code. A send failure doesn't block the request.
</Note>

---

## Rate limit

Each customer can submit up to **six requests per hour**. Each request triggers three emails, so this limit protects your sending quota.

---

## Reviewing requests

Request storage is live; a partner-facing queue to browse and act on requests is a planned follow-up. For now, the notification email to you contains everything you need to take action: the customer's name, what they asked for, their note, and a direct link to their customer record.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer request an app they could just buy?">
    No. Apps the customer can unlock by purchasing are omitted from the request form. Those apps show an unlock button instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my customers know which plan they are on?">
    No. The customer-facing confirmation email does not mention your plan or Knotie's plan tiers. If an app is plan-blocked, the customer sees a generic "not on your plan" message that names your agency, not us.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the free text the customer types safe to include in emails?">
    Yes. All customer-typed content (app description, free-text note) is escaped in both HTML email builders before sending.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
