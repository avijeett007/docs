## Open the controls

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Select **Manage** for a customer.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.

The first switch, **Enable Customer Portal**, controls access to the customer's branded portal.

## Add-on features

The current production panel includes controls for advanced call analysis, API access, and premium add-ons. **Knowledge Base Processing** is visible but marked **Coming soon**.

Some customer controls depend on a partner-level setting. If a switch is unavailable, enable the related service in your partner settings first.

## Credits and pricing

Use **AI Credits Enabled** to charge the customer with credits. The same section can control integration charges, low-credit notifications, and which credit plans the customer sees.

When credit billing is enabled, pricing display and AI Gateway availability follow the options shown in the panel.

**Charge for integrations** can inherit the partner setting or be set to **On** or **Off** for this customer. It applies only while AI credit billing is enabled. Configure the partner default under **Settings → Integration Pricing**.

## Customer portal sections

The menu controls currently include:

- Knowledge Base, integrations, Docs & Media, and Social Planner
- Phone Numbers, Campaigns, API Keys, and AI Gateway
- AI Memory, MCP Access, and curated marketplace solutions
- Media Studio, Website Studio, and AI Agents
- browser access to a customer's server

![Customer controls for Media Studio, Website Studio, and self-service AI Agents](/images/screenshots/customer-media-website-vps-controls-production-2026-09-14.png)

### Media Studio

Turn on **Media Studio** to let the customer create images and videos. Upload access is confirmed automatically when the customer first uploads a file.

### Website Studio

Turn on **Website Studio** to let the customer submit a website brief and follow the build in their portal. The main Website Studio experience must also be enabled.

### AI Agents

Turn on **AI Agents** to let the customer use the self-service Voice AI Platform. Use **Agent slots** to limit how many agents they can create; `0` means view and test only.

### Campaigns

Turn on **Outbound Campaigns** to show the campaign area where the customer can upload prospects and review results. The partner defaults under **Settings → Platform Configuration** also include **Create & Run Campaigns** for customers who should build, start, and pause their own campaigns.

When creating a campaign in the Partner Portal, choose a customer in the first step to show the campaign in that customer's portal. The same step lets you allow that customer to upload prospects.

## Customer VPS controls

The same panel can:

- allow a customer to buy managed servers
- set a customer-specific margin
- inherit, show, or hide the One-Click Apps section
- allow browser-based server access

The global VPS settings remain the default unless you choose a customer-specific override.

## Team and portal access

The lower part of the panel controls team-member access, portal preview, password reset, and account access. Save after making changes.
