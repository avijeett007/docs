## Notification bells

The Partner Portal has two notification areas in the top navigation:

- **Agent health notifications** — alerts about your AI agents (errors, connectivity issues, provider outages).
- **Partner notifications** — general platform notifications (billing alerts, credit thresholds, team updates).

### How polling works

The notification bells check for new notifications on a 5-minute interval. To save bandwidth, polling **pauses automatically when the browser tab is hidden** (for example, when you switch to another tab or minimise the window) and resumes when you return to the portal tab.

You can dismiss individual notifications or mark all as read from the bell dropdown.

## Phone activation status

When you have phone numbers going through carrier activation (for example, after importing Telnyx numbers or purchasing Twilio numbers), the phone numbers page shows a live activation status panel.

The panel polls for updates every **30 seconds**. It automatically stops polling once all numbers in the list have reached a final state:

- **Active** — the number is ready to use.
- **Rejected** — the number could not be activated; see the status detail for the reason.

Numbers in intermediate states (being reviewed, waiting for more information, or in an AI review) continue to poll until they resolve.

If you submit a new number for activation, polling resumes automatically.

## Live platform stats

The home page and certain dashboard widgets show live platform stats — total partners, customers, agents, and calls. This counter updates every **5 minutes** and only fetches when the browser tab is active.

On first page load, the counter is visible immediately rather than showing a loading state.

## Onboarding progress bar

The onboarding progress bar (shown until your partner setup is complete) refreshes once per minute. Only the floating onboarding widget drives the refresh timer — if you have the progress bar visible elsewhere (for example, on the dashboard), it reads from the same shared data and does not add extra network requests.

Once all onboarding steps are complete, the bar is no longer shown and polling stops.
