## What the App Marketplace is

The **App Marketplace** is a partner-to-partner commerce layer inside Knotie AI Pro. Partners who build AI solutions — agents, workflow templates, integrations — can publish them as listings. Other partners can browse those listings, purchase them, and immediately deploy them as white-label "New Solutions" in their own portal.

Think of it as a curated app store where buyers and sellers are all Knotie AI Pro partners.

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar, look for **Marketplace** (or navigate to `/partner/marketplace`).

## Browsing listings

The marketplace shows listings from other partners with:

- **Name and description** — what the solution does
- **Pricing** — Free, one-time purchase, or monthly/annual subscription
- **Media** — product images, logo, and optional video demo
- **Ratings** — average star rating and review count
- **Category** — filter by type (AI agents, integrations, templates, etc.)

## Pricing types

| Pricing type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Free** | No charge — install immediately |
| **One-time** | Single payment; access is permanent |
| **Recurring** | Monthly or annual subscription; access continues while subscription is active |

Payments for priced listings run through your connected Stripe account on the seller's payment infrastructure. **You cannot purchase your own listings.**

## Purchasing a listing

1. Open the listing detail page.
2. Review the description, pricing, and terms.
3. Click **Purchase** (or **Add for free** for free listings).
4. Complete the Stripe checkout (priced listings only).
5. Once payment is confirmed, you receive **entitlement** to use the listing.

### What "entitlement" means

After a successful purchase:
- **Free listings** — permanent access
- **One-time listings** — permanent access
- **Recurring subscriptions** — access while your subscription is active; if you cancel the subscription, the New Solution is automatically disabled and cannot be re-enabled until you resubscribe

## Adding a purchased listing to your portal

Once you have entitlement, you can add the listing as a **New Solution** — a white-label offering in your own partner portal for customers.

1. Go to your purchased listing (or find it in **My Purchases**).
2. Click **Add as New Solution**.
3. The solution appears in your portal under the configured name and branding.

## Leaving a review

After purchasing a listing, you can rate and review it:

1. Go to the listing detail page.
2. Click **Write a Review**.
3. Select a star rating and enter your review text.
4. Submit.

Reviews are public and visible to all partners. You cannot review a listing you haven't purchased, and you cannot review your own listings.

## Managing your purchases

Go to **Marketplace → My Purchases** to see:
- All active entitlements
- Subscription status (active / cancelled)
- Renewal dates for recurring subscriptions

To cancel a recurring subscription, use the cancel option on the purchase detail. Access continues until the end of the current billing period, then the linked New Solution is disabled.

## Terms of service

All marketplace transactions must run through the Knotie AI Pro platform. By making a purchase or publishing a listing, you agree to the platform's Terms of Service (presented during the developer console agreement gate). The platform may update these terms periodically; a new agreement prompt appears when you next access the Developer Console.
