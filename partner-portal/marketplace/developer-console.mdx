## What the Developer Console is

The **Developer Console** is a standalone portal for partners who want to build and sell AI solutions to other Knotie AI Pro partners. It is separate from the main Partner Portal — it has its own navigation, terminal-style theme, and role: you are the developer/seller here, not the agency owner.

Access the Developer Console at `/developer` (or via the Partner Portal → **Developer Console** in the sidebar).

## Prerequisites

Before you can list anything, you must:

1. **Connect Stripe** — Go to [Partner Settings → Stripe Connect](/partner-portal/settings/stripe-connect) and connect your Stripe account. This is required to receive payments for priced listings.
2. **Accept the Developer Agreement** — The first time you open the Developer Console, a full-screen agreement gate presents the platform terms (including the shared-responsibility model and the requirement that all payments run through the Knotie platform). You must accept to proceed. If the terms are updated, you'll be prompted to re-accept.

## Creating a listing

1. In the Developer Console sidebar, go to **Listings → New Listing**.
2. Fill in the listing details:
   - **Name** — Public-facing title of your solution
   - **Description** — Markdown-supported description of what it does and why partners should use it
   - **Category** — Helps buyers find your listing
3. **Save as draft** before setting a price (required).

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the listing editor showing the name, description, and category fields._

### Setting a price

After saving a draft, the **Pricing** section unlocks (only if your Stripe account has charges enabled):

| Pricing type | How to set it |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Select Free — no Stripe setup required |
| **One-time** | Enter a price; a Stripe Product + Price is created on your connected account |
| **Recurring** | Enter a price and select monthly or annual; a Stripe Subscription Price is created |

If your Stripe account does not have charges enabled, the pricing controls are hidden and the listing will be free until you complete Stripe setup.

### Adding media

Make your listing stand out with rich media:

- **Product images** — Up to multiple images (partner-scoped, hosted on Knotie's storage)
- **Logo** — A square logo that appears on listing cards in the marketplace
- **Video** — Paste a YouTube or Loom URL, or upload a short video clip directly

All media is stored securely and scoped to your partner account. Customers browsing the marketplace see a developer-stripped view — your internal pricing context and account details are never exposed.

### Publishing a listing

Once your listing is complete, click **Publish**. Published listings appear in the public marketplace immediately. You can unpublish at any time to take a listing offline.

## Managing your listings

In **Listings**, your dashboard shows:
- Total active listings
- Total revenue earned
- Average rating
- Per-listing analytics (views, purchases, conversion)

Click any listing to open the **listing detail** panel, which has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Edit listing details, pricing, and media |
| **Analytics** | Views, purchases, conversion rate over time |
| **Requests** | Questions and support requests from buyers |
| **Reviews** | Buyer ratings and comments |

## Handling buyer requests

When a buyer submits a request or question about your listing, it appears in:
- The **Requests** tab of that listing
- The **Requests Inbox** in the Developer Console sidebar (shows all pending requests across all listings)

Reply directly from the Requests inbox. Unanswered requests are flagged to prompt timely responses.

## Earnings

Go to **Earnings** in the Developer Console sidebar to see:
- Total lifetime revenue
- Revenue per listing
- Recent transactions

Payouts follow your Stripe Connect payout schedule. All revenue from marketplace sales is paid directly to your connected Stripe account.

## Developer Console settings

In **Settings** (inside the Developer Console), you can:
- Update your developer display name and bio
- Review the accepted Terms of Service version
- Re-accept terms if prompted after an update

## Privacy and buyer data

The Developer Console is designed to protect end-customer privacy:
- No developer-facing screen exposes the identity of end-customers.
- Buyer interactions (reviews, requests, purchases) are **partner-to-partner** — you see the buying partner, not their customers.
- The customer-facing view of any listing is always developer-stripped (no pricing, no developer account details).

## Troubleshooting

**Pricing controls are greyed out.**  
Your Stripe account does not have charges enabled. Complete Stripe onboarding at [Partner Settings → Stripe Connect](/partner-portal/settings/stripe-connect).

**I can't access the Developer Console.**  
Make sure you have accepted the Developer Agreement. If the page redirects you back to the agreement gate, read and accept the terms to proceed.

**I updated terms but the agreement gate keeps reappearing.**  
The platform bumped the terms version. You must re-accept the new version before continuing.

**My listing isn't appearing in the marketplace.**  
Check that the listing status is **Published** (not Draft or Unpublished) in the Listings panel.
