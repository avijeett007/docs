## Overview

Every Knotie AI partner account includes a base storage allowance shared across all files on the platform:

- Knowledge base files
- Media library uploads
- Artifacts and generated files
- Website assets
- Regulatory / compliance documents

When you reach your limit, uploads stop. The **Storage Add-on** lets you subscribe to additional storage tiers so you never hit a wall mid-project.

## Where to find it

Open the Partner Portal sidebar → **Credits accordion** → **Storage**.

You'll land on `/partner/storage`.

## The storage page

The `/partner/storage` page has three main sections.

### Storage usage card

Shows your current allocation and how much you've used:

- **Total storage** — base allowance plus any purchased add-ons.
- **Used** — sum of all files currently stored under your account.
- **Usage bar** — fills as you approach the limit.

**Warning banners:**
- At **75 % used** — a yellow caution banner prompts you to consider adding storage before uploads are affected.
- At **90 % used** — an orange urgent banner warns that uploads may start failing soon.

## Subscribing to a storage plan

> 🎬 **Video walkthrough:** _Record a short clip walking through the full storage purchase flow — opening the storage page, clicking Subscribe on a plan, completing the Stripe checkout, and seeing the "Current Plan" badge appear._

1. Open the **Storage** page from the sidebar.
2. Find the plan that fits your needs and click **Subscribe**.
3. A confirmation modal opens showing the plan you selected and the price. Plans you already subscribe to appear as **Current Plan** and cannot be re-selected.
4. Click **Confirm** — you're redirected to Stripe Checkout.
5. Enter your payment details and complete the checkout (card only).
6. You're returned to `/partner/storage?success=true`. Your storage total updates immediately.

> **Tip:** Your extra storage is live the moment the checkout completes — you don't need to wait for an email confirmation.

### After a successful purchase

The page detects the `?success=true` redirect and immediately activates your new subscription server-side. The plan card for the tier you just bought switches to **Current Plan** and your usage bar reflects the higher total.

### If you cancel at checkout

If you close the Stripe Checkout window or click **Back**, you return to the Storage page with a `?canceled=true` flag. Nothing is charged and nothing changes.

## Cancelling a storage subscription

Storage subscriptions are Stripe subscriptions. To cancel:

1. Log in to the payment portal via **Settings → Plans & Billing → Manage Billing**.
2. Locate your Storage add-on subscription and cancel it.

After cancellation you have a **30-day grace window** — your total storage allocation stays the same for 30 days so you can download or clean up files before the limit drops.

## For Mission Control admins: managing storage plans

> 🎬 **Video walkthrough:** _Record a short clip of the Mission Control flow — creating a new storage plan, syncing it to Stripe, enabling it, and confirming it appears on the partner-facing storage page._

Admins define sellable storage tiers in **Mission Control → Storage Plans** (`/mission-control/storage-plans`).

### Creating a plan

1. Go to **Mission Control → Storage Plans → New Plan**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Key** — unique identifier, e.g. `storage_50gb` (cannot be changed after creation).
   - **Name** — display name shown to partners, e.g. "50 GB Storage".
   - **Description** — optional marketing copy.
   - **Storage (GB)** — positive number.
   - **Price (cents)** — e.g. `999` for $9.99.
   - **Billing interval** — Monthly or Annual.
   - **Interval count** — e.g. `1` for every billing period.
   - **Sort order** — controls display order on the partner page.
3. Save. The plan is created with **Inactive** status.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the new storage plan form in Mission Control._

### Syncing to Stripe

A plan must be synced to Stripe before it can be activated.

1. Open the plan from the list.
2. Click **Sync to Stripe** (or **Re-sync Price** if the plan was already synced but the price changed).
3. Knotie creates a Stripe Product and a recurring Price for the plan and stores the Stripe price ID. If you change the price later and re-sync, the old Stripe price is archived and a new one is created (Stripe prices are immutable).

### Activating / deactivating a plan

- **Activate** — toggle the plan to Active. Only available after a Stripe price has been synced.
- **Deactivate** — hides the plan from the partner-facing storage page. Partners with existing active subscriptions are unaffected; the plan just won't be offered to new subscribers.

### Deleting a plan

A plan with one or more active subscriptions cannot be deleted (the API returns 409). Deactivate the plan first, then ask subscribers to cancel their subscriptions, before attempting a delete.

## Common questions

**Can I subscribe to more than one storage plan?**
Yes — each subscription is independent. Your purchased total is the sum of all active subscriptions.

**What counts toward my storage?**
Knowledge base files, media library uploads, artifacts, website assets, and regulatory documents are all counted. Usage is recalculated on every upload so the bar stays accurate.

**What happens when the grace window expires after cancellation?**
Knotie reduces your purchased storage allocation. Files aren't deleted automatically, but new uploads may be blocked if your used storage exceeds the new total. Clean up files before the 30-day grace window closes.

**Will I be double-charged if a Stripe event fires more than once?**
No. The storage activation logic is idempotent — a replayed `checkout.session.completed` event for an already-active subscription is detected and ignored.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Uploads fail with a storage-limit error | Used ≥ total storage | Subscribe to a storage plan or delete old files |
| Storage page shows "Current Plan" on a plan I didn't buy | A Stripe webhook replayed an old checkout session | Contact support — this is blocked by a server-side guard but log the session ID |
| Plan doesn't appear after subscribing | Client-side activation failed | Refresh the page; the async Stripe webhook will activate within seconds |
| Mission Control plan can't be activated | No Stripe price synced yet | Click "Sync to Stripe" on the plan before toggling it active |
| Mission Control plan delete returns 409 | Active subscribers exist | Deactivate the plan, wait for subscribers to cancel, then delete |
