## Open Storage

Expand **Credits** in the Partner Portal sidebar and select **Storage**. You can also use **Add Storage** in the account overview.

![Storage page with current usage and available plans](/images/screenshots/storage-overview-2026-09-14.png)

## Check usage

The **Current Usage** card shows:

- storage used and total storage available;
- the free allowance included with your account;
- storage added through active plans; and
- your remaining available storage.

Storage is shared across your customers. If you approach the limit, remove files you no longer need or add a storage plan before uploading more.

## Add storage

1. Open **Storage**.
2. Review the capacity and price shown on each available plan.
3. Select **Subscribe** on the plan you want.
4. Review the checkout details and complete payment.
5. Return to the Storage page and confirm that the new allowance appears.

Use **Show History** to review previous storage purchases and their status.

<Note>
The portal shows the current plans, prices, and cancellation terms. Review those details before subscribing because available offers can change.
</Note>

## If uploads are paused

Compare **Current Usage** with your total allowance. Delete files you no longer need or add more storage. If the displayed allowance does not update after checkout, refresh the page before contacting support.
