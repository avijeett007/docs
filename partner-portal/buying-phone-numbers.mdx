## What this is for

You can buy a phone number from Twilio without leaving Knotie AI Pro. The platform searches available numbers, handles Twilio's address and compliance bundle requirements automatically, and shows you a clear message if anything in your Twilio account still needs attention.

## Where to find it

Open **Phone Numbers** in the Partner Portal sidebar, then click **Buy Number** and select **Twilio** as your provider.

## How to buy a number

1. Open **Phone Numbers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Buy Number**.
3. Choose **Twilio** as the provider.
4. Select the country, number type (local, mobile, or toll-free), and optionally an area code.
5. Pick a number from the search results.
6. Confirm the purchase.

## Regulatory requirements

Twilio requires an **address** and/or an approved **regulatory compliance bundle** for many number types — especially:

- Numbers outside the US and Canada (UK, Germany, Australia, etc.)
- Mobile numbers in regulated markets
- Local numbers in countries with identity verification laws

Knotie AI Pro handles these requirements automatically during purchase.

### Address required

When Twilio requires a verified address for the number type and country:

1. The platform checks your Twilio account for a saved address in that country.
2. If one is found, it is used automatically and the purchase proceeds.
3. If none is found, an address form appears in the modal. Fill it in and submit — the address is saved to your Twilio account and the purchase is retried immediately.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the inline address form that appears inside the buy modal when an address is required._

### Compliance bundle required

When Twilio requires an approved regulatory bundle:

1. The platform checks your Twilio account for an approved bundle matching that country and number type.
2. If an approved bundle is found, it is used automatically.
3. If none is found, the modal shows a guidance panel explaining which country and number type needs a bundle, with a link to the Twilio console where you can create and approve one.

Once the bundle is approved in Twilio, return to Knotie AI Pro and try the purchase again.

## Error messages

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Address required** | Twilio needs a registered address for this number type and country, and no address was found in your account | Fill in the address form that appears in the modal |
| **Bundle required — [country] [type]** | Twilio requires an approved regulatory compliance bundle that does not yet exist in your Twilio account | Create and approve a bundle in the [Twilio Regulatory Compliance console](https://console.twilio.com/us1/develop/phone-numbers/regulatory-compliance/bundles) for the listed country and number type, then try again |
| **Purchase failed** (with a carrier message) | The carrier rejected the purchase for a specific reason — the reason is shown verbatim | Read the message. It usually names the exact missing requirement. Contact Twilio support if it is unclear |

## Troubleshooting

- **No numbers in search results.** Try a different area code, region, or number type. Availability varies by country and number type.
- **Mobile number purchase fails with a validation error.** Mobile numbers sometimes have no locality or region data. This is handled automatically — if you see this error on an older version, update to the latest release.
- **UK local number shows a "Bad gateway" error.** Carrier rejection errors were previously hidden by Cloudflare. They are now shown as plain error messages in the modal.
- **Purchase succeeds but the number does not appear in the list.** Refresh the Phone Numbers page. If it still does not appear after a minute, check the **Imports** tab and contact support.
