## What the tour is

When you sign up and open the Partner Dashboard for the first time, a **guided tour** appears automatically. It highlights two controls to help you get oriented:

1. **Step 1 — Start here (video):** Points to the floating video button, which plays a short introduction to the platform.
2. **Step 2 — Start your journey (level system):** Points to the level-progress widget, which shows how to unlock features and grow your agency tier.

The tour runs once. After you close it (or complete both steps), it never appears again for your account.

## How the tour appears

The tour waits for the page to go quiet before starting. A "quiet page" means:

- The dashboard has finished loading
- No sign-up modals, welcome videos, preferences pickers, MFA warnings, or offers pop-ups are on screen
- No full-screen overlay is active

This prevents the tour from appearing on top of other important first-time flows (like the welcome video or the partner preferences picker). Once those clear, the tour begins automatically — usually within 2 seconds of the page going quiet.

<Tip>
If it takes a while to clear modals on your first login (welcome video, preferences, MFA setup), that's normal. The tour waits up to **10 minutes** for the page to go quiet before giving up and saving the tour for your next login instead.
</Tip>

## How the tour handles interruptions

The tour is designed to stay out of the way while other things happen on the screen. It behaves this way after it starts:

- If a modal appears (for example, you open a dialog or the platform shows a notification), **the tour pauses** — the overlay disappears from the screen.
- When the modal clears, **the tour resumes on the same step** — it never resets to the beginning.

This means interruptions during the tour (including opening the video the tour is pointing at) won't break your progress.

### Clicking the spotlit button

In Step 1, the tour points to the floating video button. Clicking that button **automatically advances the tour to Step 2** — because doing what the step asked *is* completing the step. After the video plays and you close it, the tour resumes on Step 2.

---

## Closing the tour early

You can close the tour at any time:

- Click the **×** button in the tour tooltip.
- Press **Escape** on your keyboard.

Closing the tour marks it as seen. It will not appear again on your next login.

---

## If the tour doesn't appear

In some cases the tour may not appear on your first login:

| Reason | What happens |
|---|---|
| The page never went quiet within 10 minutes | The tour is saved for your next login |
| The video button is not configured | Step 1 is skipped; the tour still runs showing Step 2 only |
| Your account already has the tour marked as seen | The tour does not run again |

If you'd like to see the tour again for testing, a team member with database access can clear the `dashboard_tour_seen_at` column for your partner row.
