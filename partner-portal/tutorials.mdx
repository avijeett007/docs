## What Tutorials is for

The **Tutorials** section is a centralized place for training videos.

Use it when you want a guided walkthrough of:

- Onboarding customers
- Setting up agents
- Managing credits
- Understanding analytics

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar, open **Tutorials**.

## The fastest way to learn

Combine Tutorials with:

- The **floating video help** button (page-specific videos)
- The **onboarding progress** checklist

This helps new team members get productive quickly.

## Troubleshooting

- **Tutorial videos don’t load**
  - Refresh the page.
  - Try a different browser.
  - Confirm you’re logged in.
