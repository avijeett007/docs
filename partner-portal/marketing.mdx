## What Marketing is for

The **Marketing** section gives you ready-to-use assets you can share with prospects.

It's designed for agencies that want to:

- Explain voice AI clearly
- Show examples quickly
- Reduce time spent creating sales collateral

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar, open **Marketing**.

## Marketing Videos

Use **Marketing Videos** to:

- Preview short videos by category (Sales, Healthcare, Case Study, etc.)
- Search by title
- Filter by category

![Partner Portal — Marketing Videos gallery showing video tiles and category filter (reachable at /partner/marketing/videos)](/images/screenshots/partner-marketing-videos.png)

## Marketing Posts

Use **Marketing Posts** to:

- Copy pre-written posts for platforms like Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and Blog
- Filter by category and platform
- Customize copy for your niche

Tip: Replace generic phrases with your customer's industry for better conversion.

![Partner Portal — Marketing Posts section with sample post cards and copy buttons (reachable at /partner/marketing/posts)](/images/screenshots/partner-marketing-posts.png)

## Voice-Enabled Website

Use **Voice-Enabled Website** to:

- Preview a demo site layout
- Copy an "integration code" snippet that embeds a voice agent on any website

## Demo for Customers

Use **Demo for Customers** to:

- Deploy demos that help you showcase voice AI in a live call
- Run "test" experiences during sales calls

This page requires a **Retell API key** configured in Settings before you can deploy a demo.

![Partner Portal — Demo for Customers page showing available demo options and the deploy button (reachable at /partner/marketing/demo)](/images/screenshots/partner-marketing-demo.png)

## Theme-aware interface

All Marketing pages respect your partner portal's **whitelabel theme**. If you have configured custom brand colors in **Settings → Whitelabel**, the Marketing pages will render with those colors automatically — no extra configuration needed.

This means Marketing screenshots will look different on partners with custom themes. When capturing screenshots for your own documentation or customer guides, take them from the same partner account you're documenting.
