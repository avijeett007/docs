## What partner offers are

The platform can surface **targeted marketing offers** to partners based on their level, subscription tier, and current stage. When an offer is available for your account, it appears as a pop-up or a banner in your Partner Portal.

Offers can give you:

| Reward type | What you receive |
|---|---|
| **Credits** | Credits added to your balance immediately (never clawed back) |
| **Feature unlock** | Access to a gated feature (Customer Credit Plans, Subscription Plans, and so on) |
| **Agent slots** | Extra agent-creation capacity for a set period |
| **Free usage (Boost)** | Selected charges waived — voice AI, AI memory, knowledge-base processing, or email — for a fixed number of days |
| **Tier discount** | A single-use promo code for your tier-upgrade checkout |
| **Stage discount** | A single-use promo code for a Customer Expansion Plan checkout |

---

## Where offers appear

Offers are shown in three places:

- **Dashboard pop-up** — appears a few seconds after you load the Partner Portal dashboard, or when you reach a new level or stage.
- **Dashboard rail card** — a persistent card in the dashboard sidebar for active, unclaimed offers.
- **Offers ticker** — a slim banner at the top of the portal for time-sensitive offers.

## Claiming a free offer

1. When the offer pop-up appears, read the headline and reward summary.
2. Click the **Claim** button (the label varies — "Claim offer", "Claim my credits", etc.).
3. The reward is applied immediately. A confirmation screen shows what you received.
4. Close the confirmation. Your updated credit balance or unlocked feature is live right away.

## Claiming a paid offer

Paid offers require a card payment before the reward is granted.

1. Click the **CTA button** on the offer pop-up or rail card.
2. You are taken to a Stripe Checkout page. Only card payments are accepted.
3. Complete payment. Stripe confirms the payment to the platform.
4. Your reward is granted automatically once payment is confirmed.

---

## Claiming a discount offer

A discount offer mints a single-use promo code and redirects you to the relevant checkout.

1. Click the CTA button on the offer.
2. The platform creates a one-time promo code scoped to your account.
3. You are taken to the upgrade checkout (tier upgrade or Customer Expansion Plan) with the discount pre-applied.
4. Complete the checkout to claim the discounted plan.

> **Note:** The promo code is single-use and bound to your account. It expires after the validity window shown in the offer.

---

## Dismissing an offer

If you don't want to claim an offer, click **Maybe later** (or the offer's custom dismiss label). The offer hides for the current session. Depending on the offer's frequency settings, it may reappear on your next visit.

Dismissing an offer does **not** remove it from your account — you can still claim it if it's still active and the claim cap hasn't been reached.

---

## Viewing your claimed rewards

After claiming an offer, your rewards appear in:

- **Credits**: the balance shown in the Partner Portal header.
- **Feature unlocks & agent slots**: reflected immediately in the relevant sections of the portal.
- **Free usage (Boost)**: usage for the covered types shows as $0.00 for the boost period.
- **Tier / stage discount**: the promo code is single-use; once applied at checkout it is consumed.

## Offer tokens in headlines

Offer copy may include dynamic tokens that resolve to your account data:

| Token shown in offer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| your partner name | Your business name as set in Partner Settings |
| your level number | Your current Partner Level (1–10+) |
| credit amount | The credits offered |
| days left | Days remaining before the offer expires |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I claim the same offer more than once?**
No. Each offer may be claimed at most once per account (subject to the total claim cap set by the platform admin).

**What happens if the offer expires before I claim it?**
The offer disappears from your dashboard and can no longer be claimed. No partial reward is granted.

**Do credit rewards expire?**
Credits added by an offer follow the same expiry rules as any other credits on your account — check your balance details for any expiry date.

**Can I refund a paid offer?**
Paid offers are non-refundable once the reward has been granted. Contact support if you believe a payment was processed in error.
