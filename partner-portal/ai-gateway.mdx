## What AI Gateway is

**AI Gateway** is the Partner Portal's top-level menu for creating metered LLM API keys.

It is designed as an **OpenAI-compatible** gateway, so partners can use the standard OpenAI SDK pattern and swap the `base_url` to `https://api.knotie.ai`.

Use AI Gateway when you want to:

- Create partner-owned virtual LLM keys
- Restrict each key to specific models
- Pre-fund usage from **Knotie Credits**
- Assign a key to a customer and bill usage back to that customer's **AI Credits**
- Let customers create their own AI Gateway keys from the whitelabel portal

> The page header shows two badges: **OpenAI Compatible** and **🧪 Beta**.

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar:

1. Open **AI Gateway**.
2. Use the top section for the SDK quick start.
3. Use **AI Gateway Keys** to create and manage keys.
4. Use **Customer Self-Service API Keys** to control client-portal access.

## Create a partner AI Gateway key

Click **Create Key** to open the partner key modal.

### Key Type

The modal starts with a **Key Type** radio group:

- **👥 For my customer** (default): Assign this key to one of your customers. Their AI Credits are charged per usage with your profit markup; the partner-side reserve is a fixed $10 deposit.
- **🛠️ For my internal use**: No customer attached. All usage is deducted from your Knotie Credits balance, after the standard $10 per-key deposit is consumed.

### Key details

- **Key Name**
- **Description**
- **Allowed Models** — select one or more from the tier-gated list

### Reserved Deposit (fixed)

Instead of a configurable budget, the modal shows a read-only **Reserved Deposit** section:

> "$10 (1,000 Knotie Credits) reserved from your balance per key. Once that deposit is consumed, usage continues to draw from your main Knotie Credits balance automatically — there's no per-key spending cap. The key is blocked the moment your Knotie Credits balance reaches zero. To avoid interruption, set up auto-recharge under Settings → Billing."

- `100 credits = $1.00 USD`.
- If your tier has an admin/platform fee, the modal shows the total credits required before creation.

![Partner AI Gateway create key modal](/images/screenshots/partner-ai-gateway-create-key.png)

### Model access and plan limits

Available models are filtered by the partner's subscription tier.

The UI groups models into categories such as:

- **budget** — e.g., Gpt 4.1 Mini, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite, MiniMax M2.7 Free
- **mid tier** — e.g., Grok 4, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, Claude Haiku 4 5, Claude Opus 4 6, Claude Sonnet 4 6, Gpt 5.1 Codex Max
- **premium** — tier-gated; may be empty depending on your plan

You can select one or more models per key. If a model is not available for your plan tier, it is not offered for selection.

### Customer billing (appears after picking a customer)

When you assign a key to a customer, the **Customer Billing** section expands to show:

- **Assign to Customer**
- **Profit Multiplier**
- **Price per AI Credit (cents)**
- **Min Cost Floor per 1M Tokens** (optional)
- **Billing Preview** — live-calculated preview showing your cost, your total cost, and customer charged

> **Note:** The Profit Multiplier, Price per AI Credit, Min Cost Floor, and Billing Preview fields only appear **after** you select a customer from the combobox. Until then, the Customer Billing section shows only "Assign to Customer."

This is used when you want the partner to create and own the key, but charge usage back to a specific customer's **AI Credits** with markup.

![Partner AI Gateway customer billing](/images/screenshots/partner-ai-gateway-customer-billing.png)

### Allowed Domains (optional)

Under **Advanced Options**, you can restrict the key to approved domains (comma-separated).

## Managing existing keys

The **AI Gateway Keys** table shows:

- Name
- Key prefix
- Budget vs spend
- Allowed models
- Status
- Last used

Available actions for active keys:

- **Top Up**
- **Regenerate**
- **Revoke**

Important behaviors:

- Regenerating a key immediately invalidates the old key.
- Revoking a key cannot be undone.
- Revoking refunds the unused reserve back to the partner balance according to the key type.
- You can optionally show **revoked** keys in the table.

Expanded rows also show more detail, including:

- Credits reserved
- Credits used
- Credits remaining
- **Admin Fee** (e.g., 0%)
- Profit multiplier
- Credit price
- Rate limits (Daily, Monthly)
- Expiry
- **Created** timestamp
- **Updated** timestamp
- **Last Synced** timestamp

## Virtual key handling

When a new key is created or regenerated, the virtual key is shown once in a success banner.

Copy it immediately. The app warns that it will not be shown again.

## Customer Self-Service API Keys

The lower section of the page controls whether customers can create their own AI Gateway keys from the whitelabel portal.

The section includes:

- **Master enable/disable** toggle

> When the master toggle is **disabled**, additional controls such as **Apply to New Customers**, **Apply to Existing Customers**, **Default Profit Multiplier**, and **AI Credit → USD Cents Rate** are hidden. Enable the master toggle to reveal these settings.

![Partner AI Gateway self-service settings](/images/screenshots/partner-ai-gateway-self-service-settings.png)

### Deposit and suspension behavior

When customer self-service is enabled:

- Each customer-created AI Gateway key reserves **1,000 Knotie Credits** from the partner balance as a deposit.
- That reserve is approximately **$10**.
- If the partner balance drops below **1,000 credits**, customer-created keys can be suspended until the partner tops up again.

### What customers also need

Turning on the master self-service setting is not enough by itself.

For a customer to actually see **AI Gateway** in their portal, all of these must be true:

- The partner-level **Customer Self-Service API Keys** master toggle is enabled
- The customer has **AI Credits Enabled**
- The customer's **AI Gateway** menu visibility toggle is enabled in **Features & Add-ons**

See [Features, add-ons, and menu visibility](/partner-portal/customers/features-addons-and-menu-visibility) for the per-customer toggle.

## Accuracy notes

Important distinctions:

- **Partner-created keys** use a fixed $10 reserved deposit (not a configurable budget). Once consumed, usage draws from the partner's main Knotie Credits balance automatically.
- **Customer-created keys** are **pay-as-you-go** from the customer's AI Credits and do not ask the customer to enter a budget.
- **API Keys** in the customer portal are different from **AI Gateway**:
  - **API Keys** depend on **API Access**
  - **AI Gateway** depends on **AI Credits** plus the partner self-service settings
