## What the Dashboard shows

When you log in, the Partner Dashboard is the first page you see. It summarises your agency's health at a glance and gives you quick access to the most common actions.

![Partner Dashboard — full-page view showing metrics cards, analytics chart, and grouped sidebar layout](/images/screenshots/partner-dashboard-full-2026-07-03.png)

## Metrics cards

The top row of the dashboard displays key numbers:

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Opportunity Value** | Combined estimated value of all customers (sum of their `estimatedPrice` fields). |
| **Total MRR** | Your agency's total monthly recurring revenue across active customers. |
| **Total Users** | All registered customers in your account. |
| **Live Users** | Customers who currently have active portal access. |
| **Portal Access** | Count of customers with a live portal login. |
| **Phone Numbers** | Total phone numbers provisioned in your account. |

## Analytics charts

Below the metrics cards, two charts show activity over time:

- **User Growth (Area chart)** — Total registered vs. live users over time.
- **Call Analytics (Bar chart)** — Total calls, failed calls, success rate, and average duration using your connected AI agents.

The date range and breakdown can be adjusted using the controls above each chart.

![Partner Dashboard — analytics charts section showing Customer Growth Timeline area chart with sample data over the last 30 days](/images/screenshots/partner-dashboard-analytics-2026-07-03.png)

## Credit balance display

Your current **AI Credits** and **Telephony Credits** balances are visible in the dashboard. A low balance will show a warning indicator — top up from **Credits** in the sidebar before calls start failing.

If you are eligible for a **free credit claim** (e.g., during an onboarding promotion), a **Claim Credits** button will appear.

## Quick actions

### Add Customer

Click the **+ Add Customer** (or **Quick Onboard**) button to open the Quick Onboarding modal. This walks you through creating a new customer record, setting up portal access, and assigning an agent — all in one flow.

### Onboarding progress bar

If you haven't completed initial setup yet, an **Onboarding Progress Bar** appears below the header. Each step (add a customer, connect an agent, assign a phone number, etc.) is checked off as you complete it. The bar disappears once setup is done.

### Daily Challenge widget

The **Daily Challenge** widget surfaces one quick action each day to help you grow your agency — for example, reviewing a customer's usage, updating their plan, or following up on a prospect. Completing challenges earns credit rewards.

![Partner Dashboard — Daily Challenge widget showing the empty state when no challenge is active ("No Challenge Today — Check back tomorrow for a new daily challenge!")](/images/screenshots/partner-dashboard-daily-challenge-2026-07-03.png)

### Notification Bell

The **Notification Bell** (top-right of the dashboard header) shows real-time alerts:

- Low credit balance warnings
- Agent errors or call failures
- System announcements

Click the bell to expand the notification panel.

### Passkey prompt

If you haven't set up a passkey yet, a prompt appears on the dashboard encouraging you to do so. Passkeys provide stronger account security than a password alone. See [Passkeys](/account/passkeys) for setup instructions.

## AI Builder popup

If you haven't created an AI agent yet, an **AI Builder** popup may appear guiding you to create your first agent. This can be dismissed once you're familiar with agent creation.

## Customer quick view

The dashboard includes a customer search and quick-view panel. Clicking a customer name opens a compact summary of their plan, portal status, and recent activity without leaving the Dashboard.

![Partner Portal — a customer quick-view panel opened by clicking a customer name on the Customers page. Shows tabbed view (Customer Info, Deal Management, Phone Numbers, etc.) of plan, portal status, and recent activity.](/images/screenshots/customer-info-tab-2026-07-03.png)

## Tips

- The Dashboard is the fastest way to check your credit balances and MRR without navigating to individual pages.
- Use **Pricing Calculator** (accessible from the Dashboard) to estimate costs before quoting a new customer.
- The Onboarding Wizard (**Get Started** button, if visible) walks first-time partners through the complete setup sequence.
