## Overview

The **Partner Level System** is a progression framework built into the Knotie Partner Portal. As you use the platform, complete onboarding tasks, and grow your agency, you earn experience through **challenges** that raise your partner **level**.

Higher levels unlock:
- Additional AI credits granted automatically
- Platform features that are not available at lower tiers
- Recognition on the live **Engagement Leaderboard**

The system is designed to guide new partners through the most valuable parts of the platform while rewarding experienced partners for continued growth.

## Levels

Partners start at **Level 1** and progress through levels by completing challenges. Each level requires a certain amount of experience points (XP) accumulated from challenge completions.

The current level structure and XP thresholds are managed by the Knotie platform team. Your current level and progress are always visible on the partner dashboard.

### What levels unlock

Each level can grant:
- An automatic **AI credit top-up** deposited to your account
- One or more **platform features** enabled for your portal (e.g., advanced integrations, higher customer limits)
- Eligibility for higher-tier challenges

## Challenges

Challenges are structured tasks that guide you through platform capabilities. They appear in your **challenge queue** — typically one active challenge at a time, with the next queued up once you complete the current one.

### Viewing your challenges

Open the **Challenges** section from the partner portal sidebar (or the dashboard card) to see:
- Your **current challenge** with its steps
- The **challenge queue** showing what comes next
- Your progress through completed challenges

### Challenge structure

Each challenge has:
- A **title** and **description** — what you will do and why it matters
- One or more **steps** — discrete actions with instructions and a "Mark complete" button
- An **XP reward** — earned when all steps are marked complete
- An optional **track** — challenges may be scoped to a specific focus area (e.g., Phone, CRM, VPS) based on the preferences you selected during onboarding

### Completing a challenge step

1. Open your active challenge from the sidebar or dashboard.
2. Read the step instructions and complete the described action in the portal.
3. Click **Mark step complete** (or the equivalent button for that step type). Some steps — such as watching a tutorial video — complete automatically.
4. When all steps are done, the challenge closes and your XP is credited automatically.

<Note>
Some challenges may include an embedded tutorial video. The video plays directly in the portal — no new window or tab required.
</Note>

### Credit claim challenges

Some challenges grant **AI credits** as a reward in addition to XP. When you complete such a challenge, the credit balance in your partner account is topped up automatically. The credit amount is shown on the challenge card before you start.

## Leaderboard

The **Engagement Leaderboard** shows the top partners by total XP earned. It updates in real time and is visible within the partner portal.

## Opting in and out

The level system is automatically active for all new partners. If you joined the platform before the level system launched, you can opt in from the **Settings** page or from the onboarding prompt in the partner portal.

To opt out, contact Knotie support. Opting out removes you from the leaderboard and stops the challenge queue, but does not remove credits or features you have already unlocked.

## Feature flags (for operators)

If you are running a self-hosted deployment, the level system is controlled by these environment variables:

| Variable | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_LEVEL_SYSTEM` | Enabled | Set to `false` to disable the level system for all partners |
| `FEATURE_LEVEL_SYSTEM_AB_HOLDOUT_PCT` | `0` | Percentage (0–100) of partners randomly excluded for A/B testing |
| `FEATURE_LEVEL_SYSTEM_PROOF_GATE` | `false` | Set to `true` to require admin review before proof-based challenges grant credits |

### Operator setup steps

Before the level system is usable after a fresh deploy, run these in order:

1. **Apply schema changes**: `npm run db:push` — adds the level-system tables and the `redesign_intro_seen_at` / `selected_track_keys` columns on `Partner`.
2. **Set the cutover date**: `PROGRESSIVE_ONBOARDING_CUTOVER` — ISO-8601 datetime that separates "new" partners (joined on/after this date, see curated feature set only) from "existing" partners (joined before, see all active features). Example: `2026-06-27T00:00:00Z`.
3. **Curate the new-partner feature set**: In Mission Control → **What to Sell**, toggle **Show to New Partners** for the features you want visible in the first-login picker.
4. **Unlock existing partners**: Use the admin **Open all features** button (per-partner, or in bulk) to grant existing partners access to the full active feature set.
5. **Seed challenges** (optional): Run `node scripts/levelSystem/seedChallenges.js` to load the default challenge catalogue.
