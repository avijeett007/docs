## What Prospects is for

**Prospects** shows people who started your **SaaS portal onboarding** flow but haven’t become full customers yet.

This is useful if you’re running a “productized” offer (agency-as-a-SaaS) and want to:

- See who started onboarding
- See where they dropped off
- Follow up and convert them

## Plan requirement

Prospects is an **Enterprise** feature.

If you’re not on an Enterprise tier, the page shows an upgrade message.

## Where to find it

In the Partner Portal sidebar:

- **Customers**
  - **Prospects** (Enterprise)

## What you’ll see

On the Prospects page, you’ll typically see:

- Prospect business name
- Contact details (email/phone)
- Current onboarding step
- Status (in progress vs completed)

## Understanding the onboarding step

The live UI walks prospects through 9 steps (a previous 10-step flow was collapsed in 2026):

- Step 1/9 — Business Info
- Step 2/9 — Website Verification
- Step 3/9 — Contact Details
- Step 4/9 — Service Categories
- Step 5/9 — Knowledge Base
- Step 6/9 — Greeting Setup
- Step 7/9 — Information Collection
- Step 8/9 — Communication Settings
- Step 9/9 — **Summary & Deploy**

<Note>
  Steps 4–8 (Service Categories, Knowledge Base, Greeting Setup, Information Collection, Communication Settings) are now **summary cards inside Step 9 (Summary & Deploy)** rather than standalone steps in the left rail. The right-rail step indicator only shows 1–9.
</Note>

Use the step name to decide the right follow-up message. A prospect parked on **Summary & Deploy** has filled in everything but hasn't clicked Deploy — usually the highest-value follow-up.

## How to use Prospects (simple workflow)

1. Open **Prospects**.
2. Sort by most recent.
3. Click **View** to open details.
4. Follow up with:
   - A short help message (if they’re stuck)
   - A quick call invite (if they’re close to completion)

## Viewing business details

When you click **View** on a prospect, you can see their complete business information:

- Business name and contact details
- Website URL
- Current onboarding step
- Service categories selected
- Any notes or custom fields they've filled in

## Free AI Credits allocation

You can allocate **Free AI Credits** to prospects to help them get started without any upfront cost:

1. Open the prospect's details.
2. Find the **Free AI Credits** section.
3. Set the amount of credits you want to allocate.
4. Save changes.

This is useful for:
- **Trial periods** – Let prospects test the AI before committing.
- **Onboarding incentives** – Reduce friction during signup.
- **Demo accounts** – Give sales demos without affecting your main credits.

## Committed Memberships

**Committed Memberships** offer a reduced price rate for prospects who commit to longer-term plans.

If you're interested in offering Committed Memberships to your prospects, please [contact us](mailto:support@knotie-ai.pro) for pricing and setup details.

## Tips

- Treat prospects like "warm leads"—they already showed intent.
- Keep follow-ups helpful and specific ("Need help connecting your knowledge base?").
- Use Free AI Credits strategically to convert high-value prospects.
