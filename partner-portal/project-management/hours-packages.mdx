## Overview

You can create **hours packages** that your clients purchase directly in their portal.
Payment is processed through your Stripe Connect account — the funds go to you, and Knotie
adds the purchased hours to the client's balance automatically.

You can also set up **monthly hour grants** so clients receive a fixed allowance each
billing cycle, with optional rollover of unused hours.

## Creating an hours package

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open customer settings">
    In the partner sidebar, click **Customers**, select a customer, click **Manage**, and
    open the **Projects & Hours** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a package">
    Under **Hours packages**, click **Add package**. Fill in:
    - **Name** — shown to the client in the portal.
    - **Hours** — number of hours included.
    - **Price** — in your account currency. A Stripe product and price are created on your
      connected account automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which customers can see it">
    By default the package is visible to all customers with project management enabled.
    You can restrict visibility to specific customers in the package settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once a client has purchased a package you cannot delete it — you can only make it
  invisible to new customers. This protects purchase history.
</Warning>

## Client purchase flow

Clients with project management enabled see a **Buy hours** button in their portal's Hours
tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Client selects a package">
    The portal shows available packages with name, hours, and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stripe Checkout">
    Clicking a package launches a Stripe Checkout session on your connected account. The
    client pays with any card Stripe supports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hours added automatically">
    After a successful payment, Knotie adds the hours to the client's balance immediately
    on the success page. If the page is closed before it loads, a daily reconciliation
    job ensures the hours are always added for completed payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Impersonation and embedded portal sessions cannot purchase hours. Only the customer's
  main account holder can complete a checkout.
</Note>

## Monthly hour grants

You can automatically add hours to a customer's balance on a fixed monthly schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's Hours settings">
    Customer → Manage → **Projects & Hours** → Hours settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the monthly amount">
    Enter the number of hours to grant each month (e.g. `10` for 10 hours/month).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the start date">
    Pick the day of the month for the first grant (1–28). Subsequent grants fire on the
    same day each month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure rollover">
    - **Rollover on** — unused granted hours carry forward indefinitely.
    - **Rollover off** (default) — hours granted this month expire at the next grant date
      if unused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Hours expiry

Granted hours can expire if rollover is off, or if you set an explicit expiry when
granting hours manually.

**Expiry rules:**

- Reserved (held) hours are backed by expiring lots first — a purchase can never cause
  reserved hours to expire.
- Expired hours appear in the transaction history with type **Expired**.
- Purchased hours do not expire unless the partner explicitly sets an expiry.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Client does not see the Buy hours button">
    Check that: (1) project management is enabled for this customer, (2) at least one
    hours package is set to visible for this customer, and (3) the client is signed in as
    the main account holder (not via embed or as a team member).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment succeeded but hours were not added">
    The daily reconciliation job (runs at 05:30 UTC) will add missing hours for all
    confirmed payments. If it's urgent, contact your platform administrator — they can run
    the `grant-hours-purchase.ts` admin script for the specific purchase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot delete a package">
    Packages that have been purchased cannot be deleted. Hide them from new customers
    instead by unchecking their visibility in the package settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monthly grant did not fire">
    The grant cron runs at 05:00 UTC. If the first grant date has not arrived yet, nothing
    is sent. Check the **Transaction history** in the customer's Hours tab — a successful
    grant appears as type **Grant**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
