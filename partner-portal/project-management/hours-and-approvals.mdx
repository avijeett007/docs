<Note>
  Hours & Approvals require Project & Hours Management to be enabled on your Knotie instance
  and for the specific customer. See the [Project & Hours Management overview](/partner-portal/project-management/overview).
</Note>

## Overview

Every customer with project management enabled gets an **hours balance** — a running
ledger of approved, reserved, and available hours. Clients see their balance and pending
approvals in their portal; you see the same view from the partner side.

**How the lifecycle works:**

1. You **allocate hours** to a project (optional, for large scopes).
2. You or Plane set an **estimate** on a task.
3. Knotie sends the client an **approval request** for those hours.
4. The client **approves, rejects, or makes a counter-offer**.
5. When a task is marked **Done in Plane**, the approved hours are automatically
   **charged** from the balance.
6. If a task is **reopened**, the charge is refunded and re-held for re-approval.

## Hours balance

The **hours balance** is per customer. It starts at zero and increases when you:
- **Grant hours** manually (admin action on the customer's hours settings).
- A client **purchases an hours package** (see [Hours Packages](/partner-portal/project-management/hours-packages)).
- A **monthly grant** fires on schedule (if configured).

The balance decreases as hours are approved and tasks complete.

## Project allotments

For large projects you can **allot a block of hours** up front. The allotment draws from
the customer's balance and reserves those hours for the project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    In the partner sidebar, click **Projects** and select the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an allotment">
    Click **Allot hours** on the project page and enter the number of hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client approves the allotment">
    The client receives an approval request. Until they approve, task-level approvals
    cannot exceed the allotment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Task hour approvals

When a task has an hour estimate (from a Plane `Nh` label), Knotie creates an approval
request automatically.

**Approval states:**

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for the client |
| **Approved** | Client accepted; hours are reserved |
| **Rejected** | Client declined; you can revise and re-propose |
| **Counter** | Client proposed a different number; you can accept or decline |
| **Charged** | Task done in Plane; hours deducted from balance |

### Changing hours on an approved task

If the scope of a task grows, you can propose a new estimate:
1. Open the task drawer.
2. Click **Change hours** and enter the revised amount.
3. The client receives a new approval request for the difference.

### Closing a project

You can close a project once all client decisions are resolved (no pending charges). Click
**Close project** on the project page. Remaining allotment hours are released back to the
balance.

## Two-way comments

Comments marked `#client` in Plane appear in the client's portal task drawer. The client
can reply from the portal; their reply is posted back to Plane as `[Client] <text>`.

<Note>
  Impersonation and embedded portal sessions can read comments but cannot post replies.
  Only the customer's main account holder can comment.
</Note>

---

## Hours history

Both you and the client can view a full transaction history:

- Partner: open the customer's **Projects & Hours** tab → **Hours history**.
- Client: portal → **Hours** tab → **Transaction history**.

Each row shows the date, type (grant / approval / charge / refund / expiry), amount,
running balance, and the linked project and task where applicable.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A task was done in Plane but the balance did not change">
    The charge runs during the next sync. Click **Refresh** on the project page to force
    an immediate sync. If the balance still doesn't update, check that the task has an
    approved hours estimate — unapproved tasks are charged but flagged for client review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Client cannot approve — the button is disabled">
    Only the customer's main account holder can approve hours. Team members and sessions
    entered via embed URL cannot approve.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Approval request is not arriving for a task">
    Check that the task has an `Nh` label in Plane. If the label format is not recognised
    (e.g. `2 hours` instead of `2h`), no estimate is read and no approval is created.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot close the project — getting an error">
    The project can only be closed when there are no unresolved client decisions. Open the
    task list and check for tasks with **Pending** or **Counter** approval status and
    resolve them first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
