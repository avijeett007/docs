## Overview

Project & Hours Management lets agencies manage client deliverables and billable hours
without leaving Knotie AI Pro. Your existing Plane workspace is the source of truth — Knotie
keeps a synchronised copy that powers both your partner view and your clients' white-label
portal.

**What you can do:**

- **Create or link Plane projects** to customers in one step.
- **See every task, estimate, and status** without opening Plane.
- **Show clients their project progress** in the portal, with only client-visible comments
  displayed.
- **Track and approve hours** per project and task (see [Hours & Approvals](/partner-portal/project-management/hours-and-approvals)).
- **Let clients buy hours** via Stripe (see [Hours Packages](/partner-portal/project-management/hours-packages)).
- **Send branded project emails** and downloadable CSV statements (see [Email Notifications & Reports](/partner-portal/project-management/email-notifications)).

## Prerequisites

Before you can use Project & Hours Management:

1. **Feature flag enabled** — your platform administrator must have set both
   `PROJECT_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED=true` (server) and
   `NEXT_PUBLIC_PROJECT_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED=true` (build-time) in Coolify.
2. **Plane connection configured** — the admin must have run
   `scripts/project-management/set-connection.ts` with a valid Plane API key for your
   workspace (or a default connection exists). You will see a "connected ✓" indicator on the
   Projects page once this is in place.
3. **Customer has project management enabled** — each customer must have the toggle turned
   on individually (see [Enabling for a customer](#enabling-for-a-customer)).

## Enabling for a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer">
    In the Partner Portal sidebar, click **Customers** and select the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Customer Management">
    Click **Manage** to open the customer management modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable project management">
    On the **Projects & Hours** tab, toggle **Enable project management** on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Customer Management modal with the
> "Projects & Hours" tab selected and the project management toggle highlighted._

Once enabled, **Projects** appears in your partner sidebar under this customer's section,
and the portal shows a **Projects** menu item for the client.

## Creating a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    Click **Projects** in the partner sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create new">
    Click **New Project**. Enter a name and choose the customer it belongs to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Project is created in Plane">
    Knotie creates the project in your connected Plane workspace and links it to the
    customer. Labels `0.5h`–`40h` are created automatically for time estimates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Alternatively, if the project already exists in Plane, click **Link Existing** and select
it from the list.

## Working with tasks

The **Task Table** on the project page shows every task from Plane:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Task title from Plane |
| **Status** | Current Plane state (Todo / In Progress / Done / …) |
| **Hours** | Estimated hours (from the `Nh` label in Plane) |
| **Approval** | Hours approval status (see [Hours & Approvals](/partner-portal/project-management/hours-and-approvals)) |

Click a row to open the **Task Drawer** with full details, client-visible comments, and
(where hours are configured) an approval action.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Task Table with the Task Drawer open
> on the right._

### Keeping tasks in sync

Knotie syncs with Plane automatically every few minutes. To trigger an immediate refresh,
click **Refresh** on the project page. A full reconciliation runs weekly.

## Client portal view

Clients see a **Projects** section in their white-label portal. For each project they can
see:

- Project status and task list.
- Hours estimates for each task.
- Comments marked `#client` in Plane (internal comments are never shown).
- Their hours balance and pending approvals (if hours are configured).

Client comments posted in the portal are sent back to Plane automatically, prefixed with
`[Client] `.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Projects does not appear in the sidebar">
    Check that `NEXT_PUBLIC_PROJECT_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED` is set at build time (not just at
    runtime). If you recently set it, you may need to trigger a redeployment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tasks are not updating">
    Click **Refresh** on the project page. If tasks still don't appear, ask your administrator
    to verify the Plane connection and check the connection status indicator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No hour estimate on a task">
    Hours are read from a Plane label in the format `Nh` or `N.5h` (e.g. `2h`, `1.5h`).
    Add or update the label directly in Plane and the next sync will pick it up. If two
    hour labels exist on the same task, it appears as an estimate conflict — resolve it in
    Plane.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A client comment is not appearing in Plane">
    The portal sends comments as the connection's service account. Check that the Plane API
    key still has write access and that the project has not been archived.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
