## Overview

Knotie can send your clients **partner-branded project emails** — from your name, through
your domain — and let both you and the client download a **CSV statement** of all hours
activity.

**Email types available:**

| Email | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| **Approval needed** | Batched per customer when new approval requests are waiting |
| **Low hours alert** | Once when the client's balance drops below your threshold |
| **Project summary** | Weekly (Mondays) or monthly (1st of month), or on demand |

## Configuring email notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open customer notification settings">
    Customer → Manage → **Projects & Hours** → Notification settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the low-hours threshold">
    Enter the number of minutes (e.g. `120` for 2 hours) at which to send a low-hours
    alert. Leave blank to disable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a summary frequency">
    Select **Weekly** (Mondays at 07:00 UTC), **Monthly** (1st of month at 07:00 UTC), or
    **Off** (default). The first summary fires at the next scheduled time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a summary now (optional)">
    Click **Send summary now** to send an immediate summary to the client outside the
    schedule. This is rate-limited to one send per 10 minutes per customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approval-needed emails

When one or more tasks are waiting for client approval, Knotie batches them into a single
email and sends it every 10 minutes (if new approvals have come in since the last send).
No configuration is needed — this email fires automatically once project management is
enabled.

The email includes:
- A list of tasks awaiting approval with their hour estimates.
- A direct link to the client's portal approvals panel.
- Your partner brand name and logo.

---

## Low-hours alerts

A low-hours alert is sent **once** when the balance drops below the threshold you set. It
re-arms automatically when the balance recovers (e.g. after a purchase or grant), so the
client can receive another alert the next time hours run low.

<Note>
  The alert is sent to the customer's main account holder only. Team members do not
  receive it.
</Note>

---

## Project summaries

A project summary email gives the client a snapshot of their projects and hours for the
period: tasks completed, hours charged, and current balance.

Summaries are sent on a **per-partner** basis — only the partner that manages a customer's
hours account sends summaries. If a customer has no hours account, no summary is sent.

If a scheduled run is missed (e.g. the cron was restarted), the next run catches up and
sends the summary for the missed period.

---

## Hours statement (CSV)

Both partners and clients can download a **CSV statement** of all hours transactions.

**How to download:**
- **Partner**: Customer → Manage → Projects & Hours → **Download statement**.
- **Client portal**: Hours tab → **Download statement**. Quick presets (last 30 days, last
  90 days, custom range) are available.

**CSV columns:**

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Date (UTC) | Transaction date and time |
| Type | grant / approval / charge / refund / expiry / purchase |
| Hours | Amount (positive = added, negative = deducted) |
| Balance after | Running balance after this row |
| Project | Project name (if applicable) |
| Task | Task title (if applicable) |
| Note | Description or reference |

The default range is 90 days. The maximum range is 366 days.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Emails are not arriving">
    Check that your email domain is verified in Settings → Email Settings. The partner's
    SES domain is tried first; if it fails, the partner's SMTP config is tried; then the
    platform default. If none are configured, emails cannot be sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Client is not receiving the approval-needed email">
    Verify that the customer has at least one pending approval and that the cron
    (`*/10 * * * *` for `pm-notifications`) is running. Check that the customer's main
    account holder has a valid email address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Low-hours alert fired but the client is not getting it again">
    The alert re-arms only when the balance recovers above the threshold. Check the
    customer's current balance — if it has not recovered, no second alert is sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CSV is missing recent transactions">
    The statement is generated on demand from the ledger. If a very recent transaction
    (seconds ago) does not appear, wait a moment and re-download.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Summary says 'Send now' is unavailable">
    "Send summary now" is rate-limited to one send per 10 minutes per customer. Wait a few
    minutes and try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
