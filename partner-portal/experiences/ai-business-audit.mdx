## What it is

The **AI Business Audit** is a One-Click Experience that lets you offer an automated business-health analysis as a lead-generation or paid service product.

A prospect visits your branded landing page, enters their business name, and within minutes receives a structured audit report covering areas like online presence, local SEO, and recommended next steps. The report can optionally include a menu of your service packages — each purchasable via Stripe Checkout.

The entire flow runs under your white-label domain with no Knotie branding visible to the end customer.

## Enabling the AI Business Audit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Experiences">
    In the Partner Portal sidebar, click **One-Click Experiences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select AI Business Audit">
    Find the **AI Business Audit** card and click **Enable**. The system automatically creates a dedicated AI Gateway key for this experience — you will see a status badge change from *Pending* to *Active* once it provisions.

    > 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the AI Business Audit experience card in the Experiences list, showing the Enable button and the "AI Gateway key: Active" status badge._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an alias">
    Give your experience a URL alias (e.g., `audit`). Prospects will reach it at `https://your-domain.com/experience/audit`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The experience is now live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuring the experience

### Branding

Under the **Landing Page** tab you can override the default white-label branding specifically for this experience:

| Field | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Business name** | Shown in the page header and email confirmations |
| **Assistant name** | The "character" name used in the report narrative (e.g., "Aria") |
| **Logo** | Replaces the default white-label logo |
| **Primary colour** | Accent colour for buttons and highlights |
| **Hero title / subtitle** | The headline copy on the prospect landing page |
| **Trust pills** | Short social-proof badges shown below the hero (e.g., "500+ audits delivered") |
| **Form disclaimer** | Legal/marketing text shown below the business search form |

### Narrative model

By default the audit narrative is generated with **Claude Sonnet 4.6**. To use a different model:

1. Go to the **Settings** tab for this experience.
2. Enter a model ID in the **Narrative model** field (must be allowed by your AI Gateway).

<Note>Only models enabled in your partner AI Gateway are valid. Using a disallowed model ID will cause the audit job to fall back to the default.</Note>

### Writing audits to Customer KB

When **Write audits to Customer KB** is enabled, completed audit reports are stored as Knowledge Base files on the converted customer's account. Downstream agents can then reference these findings during calls.

## Service catalog: services and packages

The service catalog lets you define what you offer and present purchasable packages at the bottom of the audit report.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Service Catalog configuration page in the Partner Portal, showing the services list and the packages panel._

### Adding services

In the Partner Portal, go to **One-Click Experiences → AI Business Audit → Services**.

Click **Add service** and fill in:
- **Title** — Short name (e.g., "Google Business Profile Optimisation")
- **Description** — One or two sentences for the prospect
- **Deliverables** — Bullet list of what's included

Services are the atomic building blocks. They do not have prices on their own.

### Creating packages

Go to **One-Click Experiences → AI Business Audit → Packages**.

Click **Add package** and configure:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Package display name (e.g., "Growth Package") |
| **Description** | Short selling pitch |
| **Services included** | Select which services from your catalog are bundled |
| **Pricing type** | `One-time` or `Recurring` |
| **Amount** | Price in the smallest currency unit (e.g., 19900 = £199.00) |
| **Currency** | `gbp`, `usd`, etc. |
| **Highlighted** | Marks the package as "Most Popular" or similar |
| **CTA label** | Button text on the report page (e.g., "Get this package") |
| **Enabled** | Toggle visibility without deleting the package |

When you save a package, Knotie automatically creates the corresponding Stripe Product and Price via your Stripe Connect account.

<Warning>
Stripe Connect must be connected and active before saving packages. If the Stripe sync fails, you will see an error banner — fix the Stripe connection and re-save.
</Warning>

## The prospect journey

Once enabled, the experience runs through these pages automatically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Landing page">
    The prospect lands on `https://your-domain.com/experience/audit`. They see your branding, the hero copy, trust pills, and a business-name search field.

    > 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the landing page including the business search autocomplete field._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business lookup">
    As the prospect types, an autocomplete powered by Google Places suggests matching businesses. The prospect selects their business (or types a free-form name).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit running">
    The prospect sees a progress screen while the audit is being generated. The backend:
    1. Pulls business data from external sources (via Apify)
    2. Runs the LLM analysis pipeline
    3. Stores the structured results

    > 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the "Audit running" progress screen._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report page">
    When complete, the prospect is redirected to the full report page. The report includes:
    - An executive summary
    - Scored sections with findings and recommendations
    - Your service packages (if configured) with a Stripe Checkout button on each

    The prospect can also click **Download PDF** to save a branded PDF copy.

    > 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the full report page, showing scored sections and a package card with the CTA button._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Package checkout">
    Clicking a package CTA redirects to Stripe Checkout. On success, the prospect is converted to a customer in your Partner Portal and shown a thank-you page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## API reference (partner-facing endpoints)

The following REST endpoints power the AI Business Audit. All partner endpoints require your partner API key as a Bearer token.

### Experience management

```
GET  /api/partner/experiences
POST /api/partner/experiences/[id]/services
GET  /api/partner/experiences/[id]/packages
POST /api/partner/experiences/[id]/provision-gateway-key
```

### Whitelabel audit (prospect-facing)

These endpoints are called by the prospect-facing UI and do not require a partner key — they authenticate via the experience alias.

| Method | Path | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/generate` | Start an audit job for a prospect |
| `GET` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/status` | Poll audit job status |
| `GET` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]` | Fetch completed audit report |
| `GET` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/download-pdf` | Download branded PDF report |
| `POST` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/checkout/[packageId]` | Create a Stripe Checkout session for a package |
| `POST` | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/purchase` | Mark a package as purchased (after Stripe webhook) |

## Troubleshooting

**AI Gateway key is stuck on "Pending"**
Wait up to 60 seconds after enabling the experience and refresh the page. If it stays pending, disable and re-enable the experience to trigger a fresh key provision.

**Audit job never completes**
- Check that your AI Gateway has remaining budget (Partner Portal → AI Gateway).
- Verify the narrative model ID is valid and permitted on your gateway.
- View KnotieManager logs for the audit queue worker if you have self-hosted access.

**Stripe package button is greyed out**
The Stripe product/price was not synced. Check your Stripe Connect status at **Settings → Stripe Connect**, then re-save the package.

**PDF download is blank**
The PDF renderer requires a completed audit. Confirm `status: completed` by calling the status endpoint before directing the prospect to download.
