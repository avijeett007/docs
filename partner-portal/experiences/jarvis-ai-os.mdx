## What JARVIS AI OS is

**JARVIS AI OS** is a Partner Experience that lets you sell a branded AI business assistant to your clients under any name you choose. When a prospect signs up through your landing page, the platform automatically provisions their personal AI assistant — trained on their website and documents, connected to AI tools, and accessible from a voice ORB in their customer portal.

Use JARVIS AI OS when you want to:

- Give prospects a hands-on demo of their own branded AI assistant at the end of a short onboarding flow
- Convert demos to paying customers with a Stripe-linked free-trial or paid tier
- Offer a voice-first AI experience without building any backend yourself

<Warning>
  **Prototype — use for prospecting and demos, not for live sale yet.** JARVIS AI OS is in active development (Beta). The partner settings page shows this banner on every visit. Show it to prospects and let them experience the voice demo, but hold off on positioning it as a finished product until the full launch.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

Before enabling JARVIS AI OS:

1. **Knotie Credit balance must be positive.** The provisioning pipeline mints a Knova voice agent (Moderate tier — 7 credits/min). Top up at **Partner Portal → Credits** if needed.
2. **Knova voice agents must be available** in your partner tier. Contact support if you do not see Knova in your agent list.
3. **Twilio credentials** (optional — only needed if you want to give customers a phone number). Prefilled from your SaaS-mode white-label settings at **Partner Portal → Settings → White-label**. No extra configuration needed if your Twilio keys are already entered there.

---

## Enabling the experience

1. Go to **Partner Portal → Experiences**.
2. Find the **JARVIS AI OS** card (the One-Click Experiences catalog is itself a Beta area).
3. Click **Enable**.
4. The system registers the experience with the default alias `/ai-os` and takes you to the JARVIS AI OS settings page.

![One-Click Experiences catalog grid with the JARVIS AI OS card visible alongside other experiences, above the Coming Soon section](/images/screenshots/jarvis-ai-os-experiences-catalog-2026-06-30.png)

You can change the public alias at any time from **General Settings**. If your landing page lives at `yourdomain.com/ai-os`, that is what prospects will visit.

## Settings page overview

The JARVIS AI OS settings page lives at `/partner/experiences/jarvis_business_os`. It is a **static, purpose-built page** (not the generic experience settings), composed of these sections top-to-bottom:

## Phone provisioning

The **"Give customers a phone number"** toggle controls whether each converted customer's JARVIS agent is assigned a phone number (imported or purchased) so they can also call their assistant. When off, customers use the in-portal voice demo only.

| Toggle | Behaviour |
|--------|-----------|
| **Phone off** | Customers interact via the browser ORB only. No Twilio consumption at provisioning. |
| **Phone on** | Each converted customer gets a phone number. Twilio credentials are drawn from your SaaS white-label settings. |
| **Phone on + Skip phone for free-trial** | Free-trial customers stay browser-only. Paid customers receive a phone number. Recommended default to control cost during trials. |

To change the setting:

1. Open **JARVIS AI OS → Settings**.
2. Find the **Give customers a phone number** card.
3. Click the toggle. The change saves immediately.
4. If phone is on, a secondary toggle appears: **Skip phone for free-trial customers**. Toggle as needed.

## Free-trial credits

When you allow free trial access (configured in the **Customer Portal** section below), each trial customer receives a credit grant on signup. The default is **100 credits**.

To change the credit amount:

1. Scroll to the **Free-trial credits** card on the JARVIS AI OS settings page.
2. Enter the new credit value.
3. Click **Save**.

Credits are deducted from your partner credit pool at provisioning, so keep your balance topped up when running active campaigns.

## Customer Portal settings

The **Customer Portal** section controls how your customers see their portal after signing up:

- **Assistant name** — the persona name displayed in the portal (e.g. "ARIA", "NOVA", "JARVIS"). This name is used on the landing page, in the onboarding wizard, and inside the customer's AI OS dashboard.
- **Branding** — primary colour, logo, and headline copy shown in the customer portal.
- **Dashboard template** — defaults to the AI OS layout with the voice ORB. You can override this if needed.
- **Sidebar visibility** — which portal sections are shown to JARVIS AI OS customers.
- **Allow free trial** — when on, prospects can sign up without paying. They receive the credit amount configured in the Free-trial credits panel above.

When a prospect visits your landing page (e.g. `yourdomain.com/ai-os`), they see a **partner-branded page** positioned around your assistant name. The page is generated from your branding and assistant-name settings — no code changes required.

The page includes a prominent **"Activate [AssistantName]"** call-to-action that opens the 4-step onboarding wizard.

## 4-step onboarding wizard

After clicking the CTA, prospects complete a 4-step wizard before their account is created:

| Step | What is collected |
|------|-------------------|
| **1 — Business details** | Business name, website URL. Optional Google Business Profile lookup (if you enable business-lookup in settings). |
| **2 — Contact details** | First name, last name, email (required), phone (optional). This becomes the customer's portal login. |
| **3 — Train your assistant** | Confirms the website for automatic learning. The prospect can add extra notes (services, hours, anything the AI should know). Documents can be uploaded after login. |
| **4 — Urgent notifications** | Opt-in preference for proactive notifications when the assistant detects something urgent (stored for a future release). |

On completion the wizard submits a prospect record and triggers provisioning. The prospect sees a confirmation:

> *"You're all set — [AssistantName] is being prepared. Check your email for your login. Once your assistant is ready, open your portal and tap the orb to start talking."*

## What happens after signup — automatic provisioning

After a prospect converts (free-trial or paid via Stripe), the platform automatically provisions the customer's JARVIS assistant. This happens in the background and follows this ordered pipeline:

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **MCP token** | An all-tools MCP token is minted for the customer. Gives their JARVIS access to all connected tools. |
| **AI Mind** | A dedicated AI Memory bank is created. JARVIS uses this to remember things across conversations. |
| **AI Gateway key** | A customer-scoped AI Gateway key is created (reserved for future autonomous features). |
| **Knowledge Base** | A knowledge base is created. The customer's website is automatically ingested. Any documents they upload after login are added here. |
| **Knova agent** | A JARVIS Knova voice agent is created at **Moderate tier** (7 credits/min), with the MCP server and knowledge base linked. The assistant name and business context from onboarding are baked into the system prompt. |
| **Phone number** *(conditional)* | If you enabled phone provisioning and the customer is eligible (paid, or free-trial with phone allowed), a phone number is imported or purchased and assigned to the JARVIS agent. |
| **Ready notification** | The customer receives a "Your JARVIS is ready" email with a portal login link. |

The platform tracks provisioning status, which you can see in the customer's detail view in your partner portal.

<Note>
  If provisioning fails at any step, the pipeline retries automatically. If it continues to fail, check the customer's deployment status in **Partner Portal → Customers → [Customer] → Deployment Status**.
</Note>

## The AI OS dashboard — talking to JARVIS

Once provisioned, the customer logs in and lands on the **AI OS dashboard**. The centrepiece is a voice **ORB**. Tapping the ORB starts a live voice conversation with their JARVIS assistant over a browser-based audio connection (LiveKit).

The ORB moves through four states:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Provisioning** | JARVIS is still being set up. An animation plays and a status message is shown. |
| **Ready / Idle** | JARVIS is ready. Tap to start talking. |
| **Listening / Thinking / Speaking** | Animated in real-time as the assistant processes and responds. |
| **Error / Top-up needed** | Shown when there is a connection error or the customer's credit balance is too low. |

The assistant answers from the customer's knowledge base, uses tools via their MCP server, and draws on their AI Mind for persistent memory.

## Viewing prospects and customers

Prospects who complete the onboarding wizard (but have not yet converted) appear in **Partner Portal → Prospects** under the JARVIS AI OS experience type.

Converted customers appear in **Partner Portal → Customers** and show their JARVIS provisioning status in the **Deployment Status** column.

## Known limitations (Beta)

- **Prototype positioning.** The partner settings page permanently displays a warning banner. The banner will be removed when JARVIS AI OS reaches general availability.
- **Browser voice only (unless phone provisioning is on).** Customers can talk to their JARVIS from the portal browser. Phone access requires you to enable phone provisioning in settings and have Twilio credentials configured.
- **Website ingestion is asynchronous.** The knowledge base is created at provisioning, but the website crawl and indexing runs in the background. The JARVIS agent may not have full knowledge of the customer's website for a few minutes after the "ready" email arrives.
- **Urgent notifications are stored but not yet active.** The "notify me about urgent things" preference from onboarding step 4 is saved but not wired to outbound calls yet (Phase 2).
- **Autonomous task execution is not yet available.** JARVIS can answer questions, use tools interactively during a conversation, and remember things — but the autonomous background work queue is a Phase 2 feature.
