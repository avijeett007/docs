## What Resell Voice AI is

**Resell Voice AI** is a Partner Experience that turns your Knotie partner account into a fully white-labeled voice-AI platform. When you enable it, your customers arrive as end users of their own AI service — they are not managed clients. They:

- Build and configure their own AI voice agents
- Acquire and manage their own phone numbers
- Run their own outbound campaigns (if you grant the authoring permission)
- Sign up and onboard themselves, without any manual work from you

This is distinct from the default "managed" model, where you control what the customer does and run campaigns on their behalf.

## Prerequisites

1. Your partner account must be on a tier that supports the Resell Voice AI experience. Contact support if the card is not visible in **Partner Portal → Experiences**.
2. You need a configured white-label domain or subdomain for your customers to sign up through.
3. Stripe Connect is recommended so your customers can be billed for subscriptions.

---

## Enabling the experience

1. Go to **Partner Portal → Experiences**.
2. Find the **Resell Voice AI** card.
3. Click **Enable**.
4. Confirm any auto-enabled dependencies in the confirmation modal.

Once enabled, your platform URL displays a branded landing page where prospects can sign up directly.

## Customer onboarding email

When a customer signs up to your Resell Voice AI platform, they receive a **welcome email** confirming their account is ready. The email matches the self-serve nature of the platform:

- It confirms their account is set up and they can start immediately.
- It **does not promise** a setup call, calendar invite, or a team member reaching out — because on a self-serve platform, none of those happen.

<Note>
  Earlier platform versions sent a generic "You're booked in — what happens next" email to all new signups regardless of experience type. That email promised a setup call that was never going to be made. If your customers reported receiving a contradictory second email at signup, that issue is resolved.
</Note>

The welcome email is sent to the customer's registered address. The partner also receives their own separate lead notification email, which is unchanged.

## White-label branding

When customers use your platform, they should only see **your** brand — not Knotie's. The platform enforces this in the following places:

- **Connect Agents modal** and **Connect Studio modal** — show your business name. If branding is not yet configured, these fall back to your product name (e.g. "AI Agents" or "Studio"), not "Knotie".
- **AI Gateway landing page footer** — shows your copyright line only. The "Built on Knotie AI Gateway" attribution has been removed.

<Warning>
  If any Knotie branding appears on your customer-facing pages, check your white-label settings under **Partner Portal → Settings → Whitelabel** and ensure your business name and brand colors are configured.
</Warning>

## Configuring the experience

Click the **Settings** gear on the Resell Voice AI card to open the configuration page. From there you can:

- Set the platform URL alias
- Configure the landing page headline and description
- Set default features for new customer signups
- Configure campaign authoring ceilings (max concurrent calls, max calls per day)
