## Overview

When a customer submits a motion brief and the partner schedules it, an orchestrator pipeline automatically turns the brief into a polished video. This page explains the stages, quality checks, and outputs — useful context for partners who want to understand what happens between "Schedule" and delivery.

---

## The pipeline

Each job runs these stages in order:

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **1. Prepare** | The brief and any uploaded files are fetched. For Creator Recut, the clip is transcribed with Deepgram nova-3 (with a Whisper fallback). |
| **2. Concept** | An AI agent writes a `data.json` file that drives the template engine. A honesty gate checks that every visible string comes from the brief, transcript, or revision note — no invented facts, numbers, names, or quotes. |
| **3. Build** | The template engine assembles the video frames from `data.json`. |
| **4. Check** | HyperFrames linting and a bounds check confirm the layout is within safe zones. |
| **5. Render** | The video renders at 90 fps with a shutter blend. |
| **6. QA** | Six automated checks run (see below). |
| **7. Deliver** | The video is written to the private `motion-assets` bucket and the job moves to `needs_review`. |

## QA checks

The orchestrator runs these checks automatically on every render:

| Check | What it verifies |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Format** | Correct resolution and aspect ratio for each output (9:16, 16:9, 1:1). |
| **File size** | Each output file is within limits. |
| **Loudness** | Audio loudness meets broadcast norms (LUFS). If the loudness is wrong, a loudnorm repair pass runs automatically. |
| **Black frames** | No unexpected black frames at the start or end. |
| **Legibility sheets** | Per-frame screenshots showing whether text is readable at small sizes. |
| **Safe zones** | Text and key graphics stay inside the safe zone for each aspect ratio. |

If any check fails, the job is set to `needs_attention`. The partner can trigger one free retry. After that, the job moves to `needs_review` and the partner decides whether to approve with caveats or request changes.

## Output formats

Most templates produce three format variants. The orchestrator renders all variants in one job:

| Format | Pixel size | Typical use |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Instagram Reels, TikTok, Stories |
| 16:9 | 1920×1080 | YouTube, LinkedIn video, web embeds |
| 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Instagram feed, Facebook |

Invite Reveal and Creator Recut produce 9:16 and 16:9 only. See the [template catalog](/partner-portal/experiences/motion-studio-templates) for per-template format availability.

## Honesty gate

The concept stage enforces an honesty check: the AI agent cannot invent claims. Every visible piece of text in the final video must be traceable to:

- The customer's brief (what they typed in the wizard).
- The transcript of their uploaded clip (Creator Recut only).
- Their revision note (if they requested changes).

If the AI agent attempts to include a number, name, quote, or offer that is not in the source material, the stage fails and the job retries the concept pass.

This means the video the customer receives reflects what they submitted — not what the AI thought would sound good.

---

## Revision flow

After the partner reviews a delivered video, they can:

- **Approve** — the job is marked delivered and the customer can download.
- **Request changes** — the partner writes a revision note. One revision is included free; further revisions cost 800 credits each, up to 5 per project.

On a revision, the orchestrator runs the full pipeline again with the original brief plus the revision note.

If a revision render fails, the job returns to review with the previously delivered video intact. The free revision is not consumed.
