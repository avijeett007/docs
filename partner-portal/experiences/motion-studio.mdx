## Overview

Motion Studio is a second service inside **Website Studio** that lets partners sell short, AI-generated motion-graphics videos alongside websites. Customers submit a brief through their portal, an AI agent turns it into a polished video, and the partner reviews and approves it before delivery.

<Note>
Motion Studio is behind a feature flag. Set `MOTION_STUDIO_ENABLED=true` (server) and `NEXT_PUBLIC_MOTION_STUDIO=true` (Next.js) to activate it. Enable per partner during rollout.
</Note>

## How it works

1. A partner enables Motion Studio and picks which templates to offer.
2. A customer browses the template gallery, fills in the brief wizard, and submits.
3. The partner schedules the job, which charges credits and queues the render.
4. An AI agent produces the video (typically within a few minutes).
5. The partner reviews the result in the console and approves or requests changes.
6. On approval the customer sees a player and download links in their portal.

## Set up Motion Studio for your account

1. Open **One-Click Experiences → Website Studio** in the Partner Portal.
2. Scroll to the **Motion videos** section.
3. Turn on **Enable motion videos** and choose which templates to offer.
4. Save your settings.

## Give a customer access to Motion Studio

The global switch and per-customer access are independent.

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Click **Manage** for the customer.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.
4. Enable **Motion videos** under Menu Visibility Options.
5. Save.

## Customer experience

Once enabled, the customer sees a **Motion videos** card in their portal.

### Template gallery

The gallery lists every template the partner has activated, with a preview video for each. Hovering or tapping a tile plays the preview clip.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Motion videos template gallery in the customer portal._

### Brief wizard

After the customer picks a template, a six-step wizard collects the brief:

| Step | What the customer fills in |
|------|---------------------------|
| Template | Already selected; shown for confirmation. |
| Brief details | Template-specific fields — headlines, steps, stats, or a video clip depending on template. |
| Photos / logo | Upload photos, logos, or short clips (where the template needs them). |
| Style | Optional choices (paper look, colour emphasis, etc.) where offered by the template. |
| Review | Summary of all inputs before submitting. |
| Confirmation | Submission notice; the customer waits for the partner to schedule. |

Uploads are signed and stored privately. The wizard autosaves drafts. A customer can have up to five active drafts.

### Tracker and delivery

After submission the customer sees a tracker card. Once the partner approves, the card becomes a video player with download buttons.

## Partner console — review and approve

The **Motion videos** section in the Website Studio console shows the full job queue. For each job, the partner can:

| Action | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Schedule** | Charge credits and start the render. Deducts 2,500 credits. |
| **Approve** | Accept the rendered video and deliver it to the customer. |
| **Request changes** | Send a revision note; one revision is included free. Additional revisions cost 800 credits each (up to 5 revisions per project). |
| **Retry** | Restart a failed render (one free retry per job). |
| **Cancel** | Cancel a pending job (credits are refunded). |

The review panel includes:

- A player for each output format (9:16, 16:9, and 1:1 where available).
- **Proof sheets** — per-frame legibility screenshots.
- **QA checks** in plain words (format, loudness, contrast, safe zones, hook).

## Credits

| Action | Credit cost |
|--------|------------|
| Schedule a new video render | 2,500 credits |
| Revision (second and beyond) | 800 credits per revision |
| Refund on render failure | Full refund (once per job) |

<Warning>
Credit prices are marked **needs-founder** in the codebase and may change before general availability. Confirm the live prices in your platform settings before advertising them to customers.
</Warning>

## Maintenance cron

A cron job (`/api/cron/motion-maintenance`) runs every 15 minutes via `coolify.yaml`. It:

- Sweeps expired upload tickets.
- Reconciles any credit refunds that failed to record.

This runs automatically after deployment — no manual setup needed.

## Deploy checklist

Before enabling Motion Studio in production:

1. Run `npm run db:push` — the migration adds the `MotionProject` and `MotionRenderJob` tables.
2. Set the required environment variables:

| Variable | Required | Description |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| `MOTION_STUDIO_ENABLED` | Yes | Activates the feature server-side. |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_MOTION_STUDIO` | Yes | Activates the feature in the Next.js build. |
| `MOTION_INTERNAL_API_SECRET` | Yes | Shared secret between the app, KnotieManager and the orchestrator. |
| `MOTION_ASSETS_BUCKET` | No | R2 bucket name for motion assets. Defaults to `motion-assets`. |

3. Deploy KnotieManager **before** the orchestrator.
4. Enable Motion Studio per partner during initial rollout.
