## What Resell Voice AI Platform is

**Resell Voice AI Platform** is a Partner Experience that lets you operate your own white-label voice AI SaaS — your own version of Retell or VAPI, running on your domain, under your brand.

When this experience is enabled, your customers can:

- Visit a branded landing page and sign up without a payment barrier
- Access a self-serve **AI Agents** section inside their customer portal
- View, test, and manage AI voice agents you provision for them
- Connect external coding agents (Cursor, Claude Code, n8n) via MCP to build agents programmatically

Everything runs inside your existing white-label portal — no separate product to deploy.

<Note>
  Resell Voice AI Platform is in **Beta**. The master switch must be turned on in experience settings before any customer can see the AI Agents section.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- Your partner account must have the **Resell Voice AI Platform** card visible on the Experiences page. Contact support if it's not there.
- At least one **Knova agent** configured under **AI Agents → Knova Agents** in the Partner Portal — customers can only interact with agents you've provisioned.

No Stripe Connect requirement — customers sign up and log in for free by default; usage is metered per-minute against your credit pool like any other voice agent.

---

## Enabling the experience

1. Go to **Partner Portal → Experiences**.
2. Find the **Resell Voice AI Platform** card and click **Enable**.
3. The experience is created and you're taken to the settings page.

Once enabled, four feature pins are automatically seeded for this experience: **AI Agents**, **Integrations**, **Knowledge Base**, and **Phone Numbers**. You can adjust which features are visible to customers from the experience settings.

## Settings page overview

Open the settings page from **Experiences → Resell Voice AI Platform → Settings** (gear icon).

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Agent Builder** (master switch) | Must be ON for customers to see the AI Agents section in their portal |
| **Landing page** | Web address alias (e.g. `/voice-ai`) and branding overrides for the public-facing landing page |
| **Self-serve agent limit** | Maximum number of agents a single customer can create (default: configurable per customer via the partner portal) |
| **Feature visibility** | Control which sidebar items appear for customers in this experience |

## Customer experience

When a customer navigates to your white-label portal with this experience active:

### Sign-up and login

The sign-up flow is **one step** — customers enter their details and are immediately taken to their portal. There is no payment wall at sign-up; usage is billed per-minute as calls are made.

> 🎬 **Video walkthrough:** _Record a short clip of the customer sign-up flow — landing page → single-step sign-up form → customer portal AI Agents page._

### AI Agents page

After logging in, customers see an **AI Agents** section in their portal sidebar. This page shows:

- All Knova agents provisioned for their account
- Agent name, type, channel, and active status
- A **Web Test** button to test the agent directly in the browser (uses the existing web test modal)

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the customer-facing AI Agents page showing the agent list with the Web Test button._

<Note>
  The agent list uses a strict serializer — customers see only public fields (name, type, channel, status). Internal fields like system prompt, LLM config, webhook URLs, and pricing margins are never exposed.
</Note>

### Agent creation

The **Create Agent** button in the customer portal opens the agent creation modal with the **AI Builder** as the primary path. Customers describe the agent they want in plain language and the AI Builder generates a starter configuration.

The manual form is available as a secondary option for customers who prefer to configure details directly.

Self-serve creation counts against the per-customer agent limit set in the partner portal. Attempting to create beyond the limit returns a clear error rather than silently failing.

## Managing customer access

From the Partner Portal, open a customer's record and navigate to the **AI Agents** tab to:

- Enable or disable the AI Agents feature for that specific customer
- Set or override the self-serve agent limit for that customer
- View agents that customer has created

## Landing page

The experience ships with a configurable landing page (URL alias: `/voice-ai` by default). Customers arrive here and sign up directly — no redirect or additional payment step.

Three visual themes are available for the landing page; you can select them from the settings page:

- **Aurora** — gradient/ambient style
- **Concierge** — professional/enterprise style
- **Nova** — bold/modern style

## Provisioning agents for customers

Partners provision agents for customers the same way they do for any Knova deployment:

1. In the Partner Portal, open the customer's record.
2. Go to **AI Agents** and create or assign a Knova agent.
3. The agent immediately appears on the customer's AI Agents page.

Customers cannot provision their own servers or telephony — phone numbers and infrastructure are managed entirely by you.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---------|--------------|
| Customer doesn't see AI Agents in sidebar | The experience master switch is OFF, or `showAiAgents` is disabled for that customer |
| Customer gets "agent limit reached" error | The per-customer agent limit is set too low — adjust in the customer's portal record |
| Landing page not showing correct branding | Check the experience settings — hero text, features, and theme are configurable there |
| Customer login shows "complete payment" | This shouldn't happen — `RESELL_VOICE_AI` is free-to-enter by default. Contact support if this persists |
