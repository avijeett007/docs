## Overview

Motion Studio ships with nine templates across two release waves. Partners choose which templates to offer in their Website Studio settings. Each template tile in the customer portal shows a preview video so customers can see what they're getting before they submit.

<Note>
Template availability depends on your platform version. Wave 1 templates ship with the initial Motion Studio release; Wave 2 templates (app-promo-pop, saas-glow-launch, paper-collage-explainer, menu-carousel) became selectable in the portal after the KNOTI-1183 update.
</Note>

## Wave 1 templates

### Kinetic Hype

Best for product launches, hiring campaigns, and creator hype reels.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Main hook for the video. |
| Body copy | No | Supporting text. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Kinetic Hype template tile in the gallery and/or its brief wizard step._

### Photo Film

Best for product, food, and shop launches that lead with photography.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Main message. |
| Photos | Yes | 4–6 images. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

---

### Data Story

Best for annual results, year-in-numbers, or stat-driven marketing.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Framing line. |
| Stats | Yes | 2–5 data points, each with a label. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

---

### Invite Reveal

Best for grand openings, events, and weddings.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Title | Yes | Event or occasion name. |
| Date | Yes | The event date. |
| Venue | Yes | Location name. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9

---

### Creator Recut

Best for creators and brands that have a talking-head clip and want motion-graphics layered on top.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Video clip | Yes | Customer's own talking-head clip. |
| Optional overlay text | No | Extra text to add. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9

The AI transcribes the clip with Deepgram (with a Whisper fallback) and builds captions verbatim from the transcript.

## Wave 2 templates

These four templates became available for partners to offer after the KNOTI-1183 update and show preview videos in the gallery.

### App Promo Pop

Best for SaaS and mobile apps — showcases screenshots with animated feature chips.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Product tagline. |
| Screenshots | Yes | 1–4 product screenshots. |
| Feature chips | No | Short bullet labels for key features. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the App Promo Pop brief wizard showing the screenshot upload and feature-chips step._

---

### SaaS Glow Launch

Best for SaaS product announcements with an editorial, high-end feel.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Launch statement. |
| Steps | No | Up to 4 numbered steps. |
| Features | No | Feature list items. |
| Stat + series | No | A key number and its label. |
| CTA button text | No | Call-to-action label. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

---

### Paper Collage Explainer

Best for mission-led brands, non-profits, or indie products that want a crafted, tactile feel.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Opening statement. |
| Photos | Yes | 1–6 images with captions. |
| Steps or dated milestones | No | Choose between steps, a timeline, or photos only. |
| Paper look | No | Toggle for the torn-paper texture. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Paper Collage Explainer brief wizard showing the photo captions and paper-look toggle._

---

### Menu Carousel

Best for restaurants, cafés, and food brands.

| Field | Required | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Headline | Yes | Restaurant or campaign name. |
| Photos | Yes | 3–8 dish or product photos, each with a name and optional price. |

**Output formats:** 9:16, 16:9, 1:1

<Note>
Photos and menu items must match in count — each photo maps to exactly one item name. The platform enforces this in the brief wizard.
</Note>

## Template previews

Every Wave 2 template (and Wave 1 templates where a preview clip exists) shows a short preview video in the gallery. Previews play on hover or tap, and a poster image shows before playback starts.

Preview files are served from `public/motion-previews/` and are same-origin paths — they play without a CDN or external dependency.

## Partner template selection

Partners can offer any combination of available templates:

1. Open **One-Click Experiences → Website Studio** in the Partner Portal.
2. Under **Motion videos**, find the **Template selection** list.
3. Toggle each template on or off.
4. Save.

A partner who has never saved a template list automatically offers all available templates. An explicit saved list requires the partner to opt in to new templates as they arrive.
