## What are One-Click Experiences?

**One-Click Experiences** are fully-built service products that run under your white-label brand. Each experience comes with a landing page, a prospect journey, and optional paid packages — all customisable without writing code.

Available experiences:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI Business Audit" icon="magnifying-glass-chart" href="/partner-portal/experiences/ai-business-audit">
    Run automated business health audits for prospects. Deliver a branded PDF report and upsell your service packages via Stripe.
  </Card>
  <Card title="OpenClaw Whitelabel Service" icon="robot" href="/agents/openclaw-setup">
    Deploy production AI assistants on customer VPS instances, routed through your AI Gateway.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Availability

Experience availability depends on your Knotie plan. If the **One-Click Experiences** menu is not visible in your sidebar, contact support to check eligibility.
