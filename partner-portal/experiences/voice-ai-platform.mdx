## Overview

The **Voice AI Platform** is a resellable experience that lets your customers purchase and deploy an AI Receptionist directly from your branded customer portal. When a customer subscribes:

1. They choose an agent tier (Essentials, Moderate, or Premium).
2. They complete the onboarding wizard (persona, voice, KB, phone number).
3. The platform automatically deploys a live Knova agent connected to their phone number.

Partners set a **profit multiplier** (1.0×–5.0×) per tier to earn margin on each minute of call time. The customer sees "≈ N credits/min" at their tier — they never see your underlying cost rate.

## Enable the Voice AI Platform

1. Go to **Experiences** in the Partner Portal sidebar.
2. Click **Enable** on the **Voice AI Platform** card.
3. Complete your platform settings (see [Platform settings](#platform-settings) below).

---

## Platform settings

Open the settings by clicking the **gear icon** on the Voice AI Platform experience card.

### Customer pricing

Set a profit multiplier for each tier:

| Tier | Platform base rate | Your multiplier | Customer rate |
|------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Essentials | 5 credits/min | 1.5× | ~8 credits/min |
| Moderate | 7 credits/min | 1.5× | ~11 credits/min |
| Premium | 10 credits/min | 1.5× | 15 credits/min |

Rates are rounded up to whole credits per started minute.

Leave a tier's multiplier at `null` or unset to bill at the platform's standard rate.

### Curated voice shortlist

You can pin a shortlist of up to **5 voices** for each tier that your customers see during onboarding Step 3. Voices appear in the order you configure them.

- Click **Browse voices** in the settings to open the full voice catalogue.
- Drag voices into the shortlist for each tier.
- Customers see only the voices you've curated; they can still click **Browse all** to choose outside the shortlist.

### App allowlist

When a customer enables the Voice AI experience, a set of integrations (AI Memory, Knowledge Base, MCP token) is automatically granted. You can extend this list with additional apps from your marketplace.

## Customer onboarding flow

When a customer subscribes, they go through the same onboarding wizard as the AI Receptionist setup (see [Onboarding steps](/agents/knova/ai-receptionist#onboarding-steps)).

The flow is:
1. Business profile
2. Persona name
3. Voice selection (from your curated shortlist)
4. Tier selection
5. Knowledge base
6. Pricing review (customer sees credits/min at their chosen tier)
7. Phone number
8. Deploy
9. Post-deploy test call

## Credit billing for Voice AI customers

Voice AI credits are billed per **started minute** of call time. The platform uses the tier-aware billing logic:

- Credit rate = `ceil(partner_base_rate × multiplier)` whole credits per minute
- The deduction uses the customer's **experienceType** (`voice_ai_platform`) and the agent's tier to apply the multiplier; all other customers/providers/experiences use standard billing.
- Every deduction record includes `pricing_basis`, `agent_tier`, and `credit_rate_per_minute_decimal` for audit purposes.

<Note>
The analytics credit meter is idempotent: a webhook replay or duplicate delivery cannot debit the partner or customer twice. Each call's billing leg is protected by a transaction-scoped advisory lock.
</Note>

## Storefront and landing page

Each Voice AI Platform deployment has a branded storefront landing page:

- **Hero section** — introduces the persona by name and includes your partner tagline.
- **Feature strip** — wraps and centres correctly at all widths (375 px mobile through 2540 px ultra-wide).
- **Pricing section** — shows 1–4 tier cards in a grid that adapts to the number of tiers you offer (1 card / 2 cards / 3 cards with a featured highlight / 4 cards in a 2×2 grid).

The storefront is **server-rendered** and partner-branded — the partner's custom domain, logo, colours, and persona name are applied server-side.

## Call Consent and Recording

The platform supports **opt-in call recording** for compliance purposes:

- Recording does not start until the caller gives verbal consent.
- If the caller says no (or does not respond), recording is stopped and the clip is discarded immediately.
- Recording egress to storage is deferred until consent is confirmed.

This is configured per-agent. Check your local jurisdiction's requirements before enabling recording.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Customer portal shows all voices, not your curated 5 | `curatedVoiceKeys` not emitted by custom-domain branding route | Update to the latest app build |
| Storefront hero shows legal name, not persona | Pre-update deployment | Redeploy; branded name resolver now uses persona name in all hero contexts |
| Credit meter double-charges on webhook retry | Pre-update billing logic | Update to latest app build — idempotency lock is now enforced per call leg |
| Tier pricing preview doesn't match actual bill | Committed-partner base rate not reflected in preview | Update to latest app build — preview now reads the partner's committed rate |
