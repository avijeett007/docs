## Overview

AI Experiences are productized AI solutions that partners can enable for their customers with a single toggle. Each experience is a complete product with its own branded landing page, customer onboarding flow, and agent deployment pipeline.

![One-Click Experiences page — cards for AI Receptionist, AI Personal Assistant, OpenClaw Setup Service, AI Business Audit, and Resell AI Gateway with Enable / Disable toggles and per-experience stats](/images/screenshots/experiences-overview-2026-06-09.png)

Each card shows the experience's status badge (`Ready` / `Beta` / `Prototype`), category (`Support` / `Operations` / `Sales`), number of onboarding steps, the URL alias (e.g., `/audit`, `/assistant`, `/api-gateway`), live prospect / customer counts, and the **Enable** or **Disable** toggle.

## Experience types

| Name | Status | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| AI Receptionist | Ready | 24/7 inbound call handling with automatic appointment booking and language routing |
| AI Personal Assistant | Prototype | WhatsApp-native personal EA managing calendar, tasks, and knowledge base |
| OpenClaw Whitelabel | Beta | Full AI Org under the agency brand (marketing, sales, support, ops) |
| OpenClaw Auto-Install | Beta | One-click self-serve install via Telegram, auto-connects Gmail/Calendar/Notion |
| Hermes Auto-Install | Beta | One-click autonomous AI agent (Hermes) on a dedicated private server — chat from the portal |
| OpenClaw Setup Service | Beta | Paid consulting call booking for prospects who need guided setup |
| AI Business Audit | Beta | 60-second self-serve audit with branded report + checkout for packages |
| Resell AI Gateway | Beta | Branded API resell — partners sell access to Claude/GPT/Gemini with their own pricing |

<Info>
**OpenClaw Auto-Install** and **Hermes Auto-Install** are _auto-provisioning_ experiences: when a customer subscribes, the platform automatically provisions a VPS, deploys the agent, and funds AI credits — no manual steps needed. See [Auto-Install Experiences](/partner-portal/experiences/auto-install) for the full setup guide.
</Info>

## Enable an experience

1. Go to **Experiences** in the Partner Portal sidebar
2. Find the experience you want to enable
3. Click the **Enable** toggle on the experience card
4. For experiences that auto-provision dependencies (like **AI Business Audit** or **Resell AI Gateway**), a confirmation modal lists everything that will turn on — click the confirm button to proceed

### First-time enable

When you enable an experience for the first time:
- The experience is created and turned on
- Some experiences auto-enable related features and surface them in the confirmation modal before you commit

For **AI Business Audit**, the confirmation modal explains the two things that turn on:
- **Business Lookup** is enabled (powers the Google Business Profile search on the audit landing page)
- A dedicated **AI Gateway key** is created for the experience — every audit's LLM calls bill against your gateway budget at your default profit multiplier (`1.5×` by default, adjustable). Audits cost roughly **$0.03** each in raw model spend.

Both can be reviewed or changed later under **Settings → Whitelabel** and **AI Gateway**.

![Heads up confirmation modal that appears when enabling AI Business Audit, explaining the auto-enabled Business Lookup and AI Gateway key](/images/screenshots/experiences-autoenable-banner.png)

### Gateway key provisioning

If AI Gateway key provisioning fails, you'll see an error banner with a **Retry** button. Clicking it re-triggers the key creation.

## Configure experience settings

Click the **Settings** (gear) button on any enabled experience card to open the configuration page for that experience type.

For **OpenClaw Auto-Install** and **Hermes Auto-Install**, the settings page includes a **Pricing Tiers** editor where you define the VPS spec and AI credit allowance per subscription plan. See [Auto-Install Experiences](/partner-portal/experiences/auto-install) for details.

## View landing page

Click **View Landing Page** (the arrow icon next to the gear) to see the experience on your partner domain:
- Custom domain if configured
- Or `https://{subdomain}.knotie-ai.pro/{alias}`

### Example landing pages

Each enabled experience renders a public landing page on your domain, branded with your partner identity. Two examples below are from a partner's live whitelabel domain:

**Resell AI Gateway** — `/{your-domain}/api-gateway`. A branded API-resell storefront where end-users sign up for an API key. Customers self-serve, pick a subscription tier you've defined, get a key, and you keep the margin between their billed rate and your upstream cost.

![Resell AI Gateway landing page on partner domain — branded hero, model logos, and Get API access CTA](/images/screenshots/experiences-resell-gateway-landing.png)

**AI Business Audit** — `/{your-domain}/audit`. A self-serve Google Business Profile audit form: prospects search their business, drop name / email / mobile, and get a PDF scorecard emailed to them. Each completed audit produces a Knotie prospect for your funnel.

![AI Business Audit landing page on partner domain — Google Business Profile search and lead-capture form](/images/screenshots/experiences-business-audit-landing.png)

## View stats

Each experience shows:
- **Total Prospects** — number of prospects for this experience
- **Total Customers** — number of customers using this experience

## Pricing and costs

AI Business Audit calls cost approximately **$0.03** in raw model spend per audit. This is billed against your AI Gateway budget at your profit multiplier (default 1.5x).

For Auto-Install Experiences, you set the subscription price per tier. AI credits included in each tier are funded from your gateway budget when a customer subscribes and refilled monthly. See [Auto-Install Experiences → Ongoing billing](/partner-portal/experiences/auto-install#ongoing-billing).

## Set a default experience for your root domain

You can mark one enabled experience as the **default** for your portal's root domain. When a visitor goes to `yourdomain.com/` (or `yoursubdomain.knotie-ai.pro/`), they are taken directly to that experience's landing page instead of the standard SaaS portal.

This is useful when you want a single product — like AI Receptionist or AI Business Audit — to be the first thing prospects see when they land on your domain.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the One-Click Experiences page showing the "Make default" / "On root" toggle on an experience card._

### How to set a default experience

1. Go to **Experiences** in the Partner Portal sidebar.
2. Enable the experience you want to use as the default (the toggle must be on).
3. Click **Make default** on the experience card — the label changes to **On root** to confirm it is active.

**Rules:**
- Only **one** experience can be the root default at a time. Setting a new default automatically clears the previous one.
- The default applies to your root path (`/`) only — all other routes (`/login`, `/platform`, `/audit`, etc.) are unaffected.
- If you disable the currently-defaulted experience, its root-default status is automatically cleared so your portal falls back to the standard portal.
- You can revert at any time by clicking **On root** again — your experience settings are preserved.

<Info>
**Prerequisite:** your portal's **Enable Customer Portal** toggle must be on (in **Settings → Whitelabel → Domain Settings**) before the root-domain default takes effect. This is a one-time setup step for each partner account.
</Info>

### Post-domain-registration prompt

After you register a custom domain in **Settings → Whitelabel**, a **"Domain setup done!"** popup appears and points you to the Experiences page. This is the recommended next step — pick your default experience so visitors to your new domain see a branded product immediately.
