## Overview

One-Click Experiences are packaged offers you can publish under your brand. Open **One-Click Experiences** in the Partner Portal to see what is available and what is still coming soon.

Each card shows:

- the experience name and a short description
- its current readiness label, such as **Ready**, **Beta**, or **Experimental**
- its public page path
- available customer and prospect counts
- the actions currently available for that experience

<Warning>
Experimental experiences are still changing. Review their live page and settings before offering them to customers.
</Warning>

## Manage an experience

Depending on its state, a card can show these actions:

- **Enable** or **Disable** controls whether the experience is active.
- **Make default** places the experience on the root of your customer-facing domain.
- **Settings** opens its configuration page when one is available. Some Experimental experiences may not show setup controls yet.
- **View Landing Page** opens the public page on your branded domain.

Only change an experience's state when you are ready for it to appear or disappear on your public portal.

## Available experience types

The production catalog currently includes AI Receptionist, OpenClaw and Hermes setup experiences, AI Business Audit, Resell AI Gateway, Voice AI Platform, JARVIS AI OS, AI Media Studio, and Website Studio. Read the status shown on each card before you configure it.

The live catalog currently shows **Voice AI Platform**, **AI Media Studio**, and **Website Studio** alongside the other available experiences. AI Media Studio and Website Studio are marked **Experimental**, so their screens and options may change.

![Production experience catalog showing Voice AI Platform, AI Media Studio, Website Studio, and the current default experience](/images/screenshots/one-click-experiences-production-2026-09-14.png)

## Choose the root experience

The catalog marks the experience currently shown on your root customer-facing domain as **Default** or **On root**. Other eligible active experiences show **Make default**.

Review the public page before changing this setting. Only one experience is presented as the root experience at a time; the remaining experiences continue to use their listed paths.
