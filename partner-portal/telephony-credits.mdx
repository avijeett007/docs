## What Telephony Credits are for

**Telephony Credits** are used exclusively for phone and calling-related services, completely separate from AI Credits.

Telephony Credits cover:
- **Inbound call minutes** – Customers receiving calls
- **Outbound call minutes** – AI agents making calls
- **Phone number rentals** – Monthly fees for provisioned numbers
- **SMS messaging** – Carrier costs for text messages

## Key differences: Telephony vs AI Credits

| Feature | Telephony Credits | AI Credits |
|---------|------------------|------------|
| **Used for** | Calls, SMS, phone numbers | AI processing, platform features |
| **Pricing** | Cost-based (no markup) | Platform pricing with margin |
| **Expiration** | Never expires | May expire based on plan |
| **Balance location** | Separate balance | Separate balance |
| **Purchase** | Dedicated telephony packages | Standard credit packages |

## Where to find it

1. In the Partner Portal sidebar, go to **Credits**.
2. Click **Telephony Credits** in the submenu.
3. Or access directly from the sidebar under **Credits → Telephony Credits**.

## View telephony credit balance

On the Telephony Credits page, you'll see:

- **Current Balance** – Your telephony credit balance in dollars/cents
- **Low Credit Alert** – Warning if below threshold
- **Transaction History** – Recent purchases and usage
- **Credit Packages** – Available top-up options

## Purchase telephony credits

### Select a package

1. Go to **Telephony Credits**.
2. Browse available credit packages.
3. Packages typically include:
   - **Small** – $50 (5000 cents)
   - **Medium** – $100 (10000 cents)
   - **Large** – $250 (25000 cents)
   - **Custom** – Enter your own amount

### Complete purchase

1. Click **Purchase** on your chosen package.
2. Review the amount.
3. Complete Stripe checkout.
4. Credits are added immediately after payment confirmation.

**Redirect URL:** After purchase, you're redirected to a success page showing:
- Credits added
- Amount paid
- New balance

![Telephony Credit Purchase Modal](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-201349.png)

## Configure low credit alerts

Set up notifications before credits run out:

1. Go to **Telephony Credits**.
2. Click **Settings** (gear icon).
3. Configure:
   - **Alert Threshold** – Balance amount that triggers alert (e.g., $10 or 1000 cents)
   - **Email Notifications** – Receive email when threshold is reached
   - **Dashboard Alerts** – Show warning banner on dashboard

### Why set alerts

- **Prevent service interruptions** – Top up before calls fail
- **Plan ahead** – Budget for monthly usage
- **Avoid emergency top-ups** – No last-minute scrambles

## How telephony credits are consumed

### Call minutes

Every call consumes credits based on:
- **Duration** – Charged per minute
- **Direction** – Inbound vs outbound
- **Provider rates** – Twilio or other carrier costs

**Example:**
- 10-minute call at $0.02/min = $0.20 (20 cents) deducted

### Phone number rentals

Monthly phone number costs:
- **Local numbers** – ~$1/month
- **Toll-free numbers** – ~$2/month
- **International numbers** – Varies by country

Charged automatically at the beginning of each month.

### SMS messages

Per-message costs:
- **Outbound SMS** – $0.0075 per message (0.75 cents)
- **Inbound SMS** – Usually free or minimal cost
- **MMS** – Higher cost, varies by carrier

## Cost-based pricing explained

### No markup policy

Telephony credits are priced **at cost** – you pay exactly what Knotie AI pays providers (Twilio, etc.):

**Why?**
- **Transparent pricing** – No hidden fees
- **Competitive rates** – Pass savings to you
- **Predictable costs** – Same as provider rates

**Your markup:** You can mark up services to your customers in your own billing, while paying provider cost.

### Never expire

Unlike some credit systems:
- **No expiration date** – Buy once, use anytime
- **Roll over** – Unused credits carry forward
- **No waste** – Credits never lost to expiration

## Best practices

### Maintain buffer balance

- **Keep 2-3x monthly usage** – Avoid running out mid-month
- **Set alerts at 20-30%** – Early warning for top-ups
- **Review monthly patterns** – Adjust buffer based on growth

### Monitor usage trends

- **Track monthly consumption** – Identify spikes or drops
- **Seasonal planning** – Prepare for busy periods
- **Customer-level tracking** – See which customers use most credits

### Budget for growth

As you onboard more customers:
- **Project future usage** – Estimate credit needs
- **Bulk purchases** – Buy larger packages for better planning
- **Set up recurring** – Consider auto-top-up if available

## Telephony vs AI credit top-ups

### When to buy telephony credits

Buy telephony credits when:
- You're onboarding customers with phone agents
- Setting up new phone numbers
- Launching SMS campaigns
- Scaling call volume

### When to buy AI credits

Buy AI credits when:
- Enabling AI-powered features
- Scaling AI agent usage
- Using platform tools and integrations
- Adding knowledge base processing

**Both balances are independent** – Top up each separately based on your needs.

## Troubleshooting

### Purchase succeeded but balance not updated
- **Refresh page** – Click the refresh icon or reload browser
- **Wait 1-2 minutes** – Payment processing may be delayed
- **Check email** – Stripe sends confirmation to your email
- **Contact support** – If credits missing after 5 minutes

### Low credit alert not showing
- **Check settings** – Verify alert threshold is set
- **Email configuration** – Ensure email notifications enabled
- **Balance above threshold** – Alert only shows when below limit

### Unable to make calls despite credits
- **Check AI credits** – You need BOTH telephony AND AI credits for calls
- **Phone number status** – Verify number is active
- **Customer configuration** – Ensure customer agent is deployed

### Unexpected credit consumption
- **Review usage history** – Check what consumed credits
- **Failed calls** – Some failed calls may still consume minimal credits
- **Test calls** – Remember test calls consume real credits
- **Phone number rentals** – Monthly fees deducted automatically
