## Overview

**Settings → Approval Defaults** is where you set up and manage the phone-approval feature for your entire platform. This page lets you:

- **Provision your approval tenant** — a one-time step that connects your Knotie account to the approval server so phone approvals can work for your customers.
- **Set default rules** — choose which tools require approval by default for all new customers.
- **Lock rules** — prevent customers from changing specific approval settings.

<Info>
Phone Approvals must be enabled for your deployment (`APPROVALS_ENABLED`). If you don't see this page, contact your platform administrator.
</Info>

## Provisioning your tenant

Each partner account has its own isolated approval tenant on the approval server. Your customers' approval decisions and paired devices are completely separate from every other partner's.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Approval Defaults">
    Navigate to **Settings** in your partner portal and select **Approval Defaults** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Provision Approval Tenant">
    Click the **Provision Approval Tenant** button. Knotie registers a new tenant on the approval server and stores the credentials securely against your account. This takes a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the provisioned status">
    Once complete, the page shows a green status badge. Your tenant is now ready and phone approvals are available to your customers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Provisioning is idempotent — if you click the button more than once (for example after a failed attempt), Knotie claims the same tenant rather than creating a duplicate.

<Warning>
The approval-server credentials are stored encrypted and the plaintext key is never shown again. Back up your database's `partner_approval_tenants` table and your `ENCRYPTION_KEY`. If either is lost, you will need to re-provision and all customers will need to re-pair their phones.
</Warning>

## Setting default rules

Once provisioned, the **Default Rules** panel lists all tool categories and individual tools available through the Knotie integration gateway.

For each tool you can set the default for new customers:

| Default | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Off (allow freely)** | Tool calls run without an approval step. This is the default for all tools. |
| **On (require approval)** | New customers will have approval-on for this tool unless they change it. |

### Locking a rule

Toggle the **lock icon** next to any rule to prevent customers from changing it. A locked rule shows a padlock in the customer portal and cannot be modified by the customer.

<Note>
If you lock a rule that requires approval and a customer has no phone paired, that customer cannot use the tool. The Approval Defaults page shows a count of affected customers at the bottom of the panel so you can reach out before locking.
</Note>

## Branding on the phone

When your tenant is provisioned, the approval server is seeded with your `Business Name` from your branding settings. This name appears on the push notification the customer receives. If you update your branding later, the approval server is updated automatically.

## Environment variables

If you self-host Knotie, the following variables are required for phone approvals:

| Variable | Where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `APPROVALS_ENABLED` | Main app | Set to `true` to enable the feature. Off by default. |
| `APPROVALS_ADMIN_API_KEY` | Main app | Operator key for the approval server — used only to provision new partner tenants. |
| `APPROVALS_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` | Main app | Public base URL of your Knotie deployment, used to receive decision callbacks. |
| `MAIN_APP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` | Connect Hub | Must equal the main app's `ENCRYPTION_KEY` — used to decrypt partner tenant credentials at call time. |
