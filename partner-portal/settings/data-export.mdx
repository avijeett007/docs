## Overview

The **Data Export** feature lets you download a full archive of your Knotie AI Pro account data to satisfy GDPR data-portability requirements or for your own records. Because large accounts can contain thousands of records, the export runs as a **background job** rather than waiting for an immediate download.

## Start an export

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Settings → Danger Zone** (scroll to the bottom of the Settings page).
2. Click **Export My Data**.
3. Confirm the request in the dialog that appears.

The system queues a background export job and returns immediately. You do not need to keep the page open.

## Track progress

After you start the export, the **Danger Zone** section shows a progress indicator with one of these states:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | The job is waiting to start |
| **Running** | The export is in progress |
| **Ready** | The archive is ready to download |
| **Failed** | The job encountered an error — see troubleshooting below |

The page updates automatically while the job runs. You can safely navigate away and return later.

## Download the archive

When the status changes to **Ready**, a **Download** button appears in the Danger Zone section. Click it to save the archive to your device.

The download link is:
- **Signed** — valid only for your account session
- **Time-limited** — expires after a set retention period (you will see an expiry date next to the Download button)

You will also receive an **email notification** when the archive is ready, so you do not need to check the portal manually.

<Warning>
Download the archive before the expiry date. After it expires the file is automatically deleted and you will need to start a new export.
</Warning>

## What is included

The archive contains the exportable records associated with your partner account, including customers, agents, and configuration data. Sensitive secrets (API keys, webhook signing secrets) are not included in the export.

## Troubleshooting

**The export shows "Failed"**

Refresh the page. If the status is still Failed, click **Export My Data** again to queue a new job. If repeated attempts fail, contact support with the approximate time you started the export.

**I didn't receive the email notification**

Check your spam folder. Confirm that the email address on your account is correct under **Settings → Account**. The download button is also available in the Danger Zone section of the portal — you do not need the email to download.

**The Download button is gone**

The archive has expired. Start a new export from **Settings → Danger Zone → Export My Data**.
