## What this setting does

**Settings → Integration Pricing → Apps for your customers** lets you pick which integration apps your customers should have access to by default. Instead of opening every customer record and selecting apps one at a time, you configure it once and apply the list to any number of customers.

<Note>
This setting controls which apps a customer **can** access. The customer still connects their own account for each app before an agent can use it.
</Note>

---

## Open the app list

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Settings → Integration Pricing**.
2. Scroll to the **Apps for your customers** section.

You'll see the integration app catalog grouped by availability (free, premium, standard). New apps added since your last save are badged **NEW**.

## Choose apps

Use the bulk controls to make a broad change:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Select All** | Ticks every app in the catalog |
| **Select All Free** | Ticks only the free-tier apps |
| **Clear All** | Unticks all apps |

You can also tick or untick individual apps.

**Free Forever partners**: apps outside the free tier are locked and cannot be selected. They display an **Upgrade to offer** label.

When you're done, click **Save** to store your selection.

---

## Automatically add new apps

The **Automatically add new apps as we release them** switch, when on, adds newly released Knotie apps to your saved list each time we release one.

- **Snapshot-based**: turning the switch on saves a snapshot of the catalog at that moment. New apps are "anything released after that snapshot".
- If you have never saved a list, the auto-add switch has no reference point and cannot act on its own.
- The switch only ever **adds** — it never removes an app you previously selected.

---

## Apply the list to existing customers

Saving the list does not automatically update existing customers. To hand the list to existing customers:

1. Click **Give them this list** on the **Apps for your customers** page.
2. Review the confirmation — it shows how many customers will be updated, including any who had no apps before.
3. Confirm.

The update only sets the list. It never removes apps a customer already had from another source (a plan grant, a per-customer selection, an add-on).

<Warning>
"Give them this list" is a one-way action. Customers who receive the list are marked as following it. Clicking it a second time sends the current ticked apps — not the previously saved list — so make sure your selection is up to date before clicking.
</Warning>

---

## How the list reaches customers

A customer follows the partner's list only if they are **marked as a follower**. The mark is set when:

- A new customer is onboarded with no app list of their own
- You click **Give them this list** for existing customers

The mark is cleared when a customer gets their own app list from any other source (a plan, a per-customer selection, an add-on webhook). A customer with their own list always uses that list, not yours.

This means you can always give one customer a deliberate, individual list and it won't be overwritten by your partner-wide default.

---

## Free Forever plan restrictions

Free Forever partners can only offer **free-tier apps** to customers. Attempting to save a list containing paid apps returns an error naming the restricted apps.

The app catalog clearly shows which apps are free and which require a higher plan with an **Upgrade to offer** label.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will saving a new list change existing customers?">
    No. Saving the list only affects new customers onboarded after the save, and existing customers who specifically receive it via "Give them this list".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a customer has no apps and is not a follower?">
    They see an empty Integrations page with a "No apps are enabled for your account yet" message. Use "Give them this list" (or edit the customer directly) to give them access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I give individual customers a different list?">
    Yes. Open the customer record, go to Features & Add-ons, and click Edit Apps. Giving a customer their own list clears the follower mark — they will no longer track your partner-wide default.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
