## What Support Keywords are for

When you contact the Knotie support team — by voice, Discord, or any other channel — the team needs to confirm they are speaking with the legitimate account owner before discussing sensitive account details.

The **Support Keyword** system gives you a memorable phrase plus a 6-digit one-time code (TOTP, the same standard used by Google Authenticator and Authy). This pair proves your identity without sharing a password:

- The **keyword** (`swift-tiger-42` style) is short enough to say out loud or type in a chat message.
- The **6-digit code** changes every 30 seconds and can only be generated by your own authenticator app.

Neither piece is useful alone — a keyword without the live code cannot be used to impersonate you.

## Where to find it

1. In the Partner Portal sidebar, click **Get Support**.
2. In the modal that opens, switch to the **Support Keyword** tab.

## First-time setup

### Step 1 — Generate your keyword

Click **Generate** in the Support Keyword tab. The server creates:

- A unique human-readable keyword in `adjective-noun-number` format (e.g., `swift-tiger-42`).
- An encrypted TOTP secret stored on the server.
- A QR code you can scan immediately.

### Step 2 — Set up your authenticator app

Scan the QR code with any TOTP-compatible authenticator:

- **Google Authenticator** (Android / iOS)
- **Authy** (Android / iOS / Desktop)
- **1Password**, **Bitwarden**, **Microsoft Authenticator**, or any RFC 6238–compliant app

The app will start generating 6-digit codes that change every 30 seconds. Verify the setup by reading the current code before closing the modal.

If you cannot scan the QR code, use the **Reveal secret** option to copy the raw TOTP key and enter it manually in your authenticator app.

### Step 3 — Save your keyword somewhere accessible

Your keyword is always visible in the Support Keyword tab when you return. You do not need to write it down unless you prefer to, but make sure you have your authenticator app available whenever you plan to contact support.

## Using your keyword with the support team

When you open a support request:

1. Provide your **keyword** to the support agent (voice, chat, or form).
2. The agent will ask for your **6-digit TOTP code** from your authenticator app.
3. The agent's system verifies the pair. On success, your `partnerId` and business name are confirmed — no passwords exchanged.

The support team **will never ask for your password**, your Clerk login details, or your API keys. If someone asks for those, do not provide them.

## Auto-rotate

If you enable **Auto-rotate**, your keyword is automatically deactivated after the first successful verification. The next time you need support, return to the Support Keyword tab and click **Generate** again to get a fresh keyword and QR code.

This is useful if:
- You want maximum security and are happy to regenerate before each support session.
- You suspect your keyword may have been seen by someone else.

Auto-rotate is off by default — your keyword stays active until you explicitly regenerate or deactivate it.

## Regenerating or deactivating your keyword

- **Regenerate** — click **Generate** again at any time. A new keyword and TOTP secret replace the old ones. Your authenticator app's old entry will stop working; scan the new QR code.
- **Deactivate** — click **Deactivate** to disable the current keyword without creating a new one. The support team will be unable to verify your identity until you generate a fresh keyword.

After regenerating, remove the old entry from your authenticator app to avoid confusion.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| TOTP code rejected by support team | Clock drift on your phone | Re-sync your device clock; most authenticator apps handle small drift automatically, but large drift (>30 s) causes failures |
| "Keyword not found" error | Auto-rotate already fired after a previous session | Generate a new keyword in the Support Keyword tab |
| QR code won't scan | Lighting or camera focus | Use the **Reveal secret** option to copy the manual key instead |
| Modal shows no keyword | No keyword has been generated yet | Click **Generate** |

## Email magic-link verification (v2)

In addition to the TOTP-based keyword flow, the Knotie AI support agent (Katie) can also verify your identity via a **one-time email magic link**. This is the method used when you contact support through multi-channel surfaces such as Discord, Slack, or web chat — any channel where you cannot easily read a 6-digit code aloud.

### How it works

1. During a support conversation, Katie sends a verification request. Knotie emails you a **magic link** at the address registered to your account.
2. The email also displays a short **confirmation code** that Katie will tell you at the same time — cross-checking the two codes prevents relay attacks.
3. Click the link in the email. A browser page opens, displays the confirmation code, and asks you to confirm.
4. Click **Confirm identity**. The page posts the token server-side, which marks your support session as verified. Katie's next response will confirm she can now see your account context.

### Security properties

- The link token is a 256-bit cryptographically random value, stored as a SHA-256 hash. The raw token is never written to disk or logs.
- The token travels in the URL **fragment** (`#t=…`), not the query string or path. Fragments are never sent to the server by the browser, so the raw token cannot appear in edge/access logs or the `Referer` header.
- Tokens are single-use and expire after **15 minutes**.
- The verification endpoint is non-enumerating — it always returns the same shaped response whether the keyword is valid or not, preventing fishing attacks.

### When to use which method

| Situation | Recommended method |
|---|---|
| Phone / voice call with support | TOTP keyword (read the 6-digit code aloud) |
| Chat via Discord, Slack, or web widget (Katie) | Email magic link (click the link in your email) |
| Prefer not to install an authenticator app | Email magic link |

## Security notes

- TOTP secrets are stored **AES-256-GCM encrypted** on the Knotie servers — the raw secret is never written to disk in plain text.
- Keywords are globally unique across all partners — no two accounts share the same keyword.
- The verification endpoint is rate-limited and scoped to internal support tooling; it is not exposed as a public API.
- If you believe your keyword or authenticator entry has been compromised, regenerate immediately from the Support Keyword tab.
