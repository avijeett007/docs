## What redirect domains are

When a customer or prospect completes (or abandons) a Stripe Checkout session, Stripe redirects them to a URL you specify. For security, Stripe only allows redirects to domains you have pre-approved.

The **Payment redirect domains** widget in **Settings → Partner API Keys** lets you manage that allowlist directly from the Partner Portal without logging in to Stripe.

## Where to find it

1. In the Partner Portal sidebar, click **Settings**.
2. Open **Partner API Keys**.
3. Scroll to the **Payment redirect domains** section.

## Adding a domain

1. Click **Add domain** (or **+**) in the widget.
2. Enter the domain you want to allow — e.g. `youragency.com`.
3. Click **Save**.

Once saved, Stripe Checkout sessions on your account can redirect to any path under that domain (e.g. `https://youragency.com/thank-you`).

## Removing a domain

Click the **×** next to any listed domain to remove it. Save your changes.

<Warning>
Removing a domain that an active payment link uses will cause Stripe to reject the redirect after checkout. Always verify no live payment links point to a domain before removing it.
</Warning>

## Common issues

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Redirect goes to a Stripe error page after payment | The redirect URL's domain is not in the allowlist — add it here |
| Widget shows "Unauthorised" when loading or saving | Log out and back in to the Partner Portal to refresh your session token, then try again |
| Domain added but still rejected by Stripe | Allow a minute for propagation; Stripe sometimes caches the allowlist briefly |
