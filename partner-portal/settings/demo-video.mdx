## What this is for

When prospects visit your white-label platform's landing page, they see a **demo video section** with a 20-minute platform deep dive. This video plays directly on the page and is designed to convert visitors into signups.

You can configure which YouTube video is displayed by setting the `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_ID` environment variable.

> ℹ️ The **demo video** section renders on the customer's whitelabel portal landing page (above the pricing grid) — outside knotie-ai.pro. There is no UI toggle in the partner portal; the section is configured via the `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_ID` env var and rendered through the `DemoVideoSection` component. Capture from a deployed whitelabel portal where the demo video is configured.

## How it works

The landing page includes an animated section titled **"See How Easy It Is to Build Your Voice AI Empire"** that embeds a YouTube video. Visitors can watch it inline without leaving your page. The section also has a **Get Started** button that opens the signup modal.

The "See how it works" link in the platform hero section scrolls down to this video automatically.

---

## Configure the demo video

### Environment variable

Set this in your main app `.env`:

```env
# YouTube video ID for the platform demo video (from https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID)
NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_ID=1wgE_3YCCGY
```

The value is the **YouTube video ID** — the part after `youtu.be/` or `?v=` in a YouTube URL. For example, if your video URL is `https://youtu.be/ABC123xyz`, set:

```env
NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_ID=ABC123xyz
```

The default value (`1wgE_3YCCGY`) is the official Knotie AI platform overview video. Replace it with your own if you want a custom white-label demo.

### Finding the YouTube video ID

1. Open the YouTube video you want to use.
2. Copy the URL from the browser address bar.
3. Extract the ID:
   - From `https://youtu.be/ABC123xyz` → ID is `ABC123xyz`
   - From `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABC123xyz` → ID is `ABC123xyz`

---

## After changing the video

Because `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_VIDEO_ID` is a build-time variable (prefixed with `NEXT_PUBLIC_`), you need to **rebuild and redeploy** the main app for the change to take effect. A hot-reload or restart is not sufficient.

---

## Disabling the demo video section

There is no toggle to hide the demo video section from the UI. If you do not want the section to appear, either:

- Point the variable to an unlisted YouTube video that doesn't reveal internal details.
- Remove the `DemoVideoSection` component from `src/components/KnotieDashboard.tsx` (requires a custom code change).

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Video not showing up**
  - Confirm the variable is set and the app was rebuilt after the change.
  - Make sure the YouTube video ID is correct and the video is public (or unlisted).
- **Wrong video is showing**
  - Check that the new value was saved and the app was rebuilt. Old values can persist in the build cache.
- **"Video unavailable" inside the player**
  - The video owner may have disabled embedding. Open YouTube Studio and ensure **Allow embedding** is turned on for the video.
