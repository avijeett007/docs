## What this page covers

When you create or edit a **Subscription Plan**, the plan modal includes a **Features & Add-ons** section. This is where you configure which platform capabilities a customer automatically receives when they subscribe to that plan, so every new subscriber on a tier gets the right capabilities from day one — without you having to toggle them per customer after sign-up.

This page is the reference for that section. For the plan's basic fields (name, price, billing interval, landing-page visibility) and how to reach the modal, see [Subscription plans](/partner-portal/settings/subscription-plans).

## Where to find it

1. Open **Settings** in the Partner Portal sidebar.
2. Click **White-Label Portal**.
3. In the **Section Nav Rail**, click **Subscription Plans**.
4. Click **Create Plan** (or **Edit** on an existing plan).
5. Scroll down to the **Features & Add-ons** section, below **Features (Display Text)**.

## How it works

When a customer subscribes (or is assigned) to a plan, Knotie copies the plan's feature flags onto that customer's account. Updating a plan does **not** retroactively change existing customers — it only affects new subscribers from that point on.

You can always override individual flags later via the per-customer **Features & Add-ons** modal — see [Features & Add-ons per customer](/partner-portal/customers/features-addons-and-menu-visibility).

Every toggle starts **off** when you click **Create Plan**. When you edit an existing plan, its currently-saved values load into the modal — nothing is reset.

## Available toggles

The section is grouped by category. Each row is a single on/off toggle unless noted.

### Analytics Features

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **Advanced AI Analytics** | Turns on the advanced analytics dashboard. Gated — shows _"Disabled at partner level — enable AI Analytics in Partner Settings first"_ until you enable it at the partner level. |
| **Detailed Call Analysis** | In-depth call breakdowns and transcripts. |
| **Action Point Analysis** | AI-generated action items from calls. |

### Menu Visibility

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **Integration** | Show the integration menu. |
| **Knowledge Base** | Show the knowledge base menu. |
| **Docs & Media** | Show the documents and media menu. |
| **Social Planner** | Show the social-media post-scheduling menu. |
| **Phone Numbers** | Show the phone numbers menu. |
| **Schedule Meeting** | Show the schedule-a-meeting menu. |
| **Pricing Information** | Show the pricing / cost breakdown to the customer. |
| **MCP Access** | Let the customer mint their own MCP token. |
| **New Solutions Marketplace** | Show the app-marketplace "New Solutions" menu. |

<Note>
Turning on **Docs & Media** (or **Knowledge Base**) reveals a **Default storage (MB)** field — _"Applied to new subscribers; overridable per customer."_
</Note>

#### Knowledge Base Processing (revealed by Knowledge Base)

Enabling **Knowledge Base** expands a **Knowledge Base Processing** sub-group:

| Field | What it controls |
|-------|------------------|
| **KB Processing Enabled** | Allow knowledge-base ingestion for this customer. |
| **Auto-Embedding** | Automatically embed new KB assets (consumes credits). |
| **KB Ingestion Tier** | `Essential`, `Moderate`, or `Premium` — _"Drives parser quality for ingested documents."_ Higher tiers cost more per embedding. |

### AI & Automation

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **AI Gateway** | Lets the customer generate their own AI Gateway API keys. Gated — shows _"Disabled at partner level — enable the AI Gateway in Partner Settings first"_ until enabled at the partner level. |
| **AI Memory (Mind)** | Persistent per-customer / per-agent memory bank. Requires partner-level AI Memory. See [AI Memory](/partner-portal/ai-memory). |

### API Access

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **API Access Enabled** | Allow the customer to use the public API. |
| **API Keys Menu** | Show the API Keys menu — requires **API Access Enabled** first. |

### Team Management

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **Team Members** | Let the customer invite team members. |

<Note>
Turning on **Team Members** reveals a **Max team members** field — _"Seat cap applied to new subscribers; overridable per customer."_ Leave it blank for no cap.
</Note>

### Servers & Infrastructure

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **Server Terminal (BYO)** | Show the bring-your-own VPS terminal. |
| **Managed VPS Purchase** | Let the customer buy managed VPS servers via the portal. |

### AI Credits

| Toggle | What it controls |
|--------|------------------|
| **AI Credits Enabled** | Enable the AI-credit billing system for the customer. |

When **AI Credits Enabled** is on, additional fields appear:

| Field | What it controls |
|-------|------------------|
| **Initial AI Credits** | One-time credit grant when the customer first onboards with this plan. |
| **Low Credit Notifications** → **Enable Notifications** | Send whitelabel email alerts when the customer's AI credits run low. |
| **Low Credit Threshold** | The customer receives an email when their AI credit balance reaches this threshold or below. |

## Tips

<Tip>
  If you add a feature to a plan and want existing subscribers to have it, update each customer individually via [Customer Management → Features & Add-ons](/partner-portal/customers/features-addons-and-menu-visibility). Editing the plan alone won't back-fill existing accounts.
</Tip>

<Info>
  The **Advanced AI Analytics**, **AI Gateway**, and **AI Memory (Mind)** toggles only take effect if you've enabled those features at the **partner level** first. A gated toggle shows a _"Disabled at partner level…"_ note until then.
</Info>
