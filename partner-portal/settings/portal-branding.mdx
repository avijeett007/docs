## What Portal Branding controls

The **Portal Branding** section in Whitelabel Settings lets you apply your own visual identity to the customer portal. Three things live here:

| Setting | What it affects |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | The main brand image shown in the portal header and login page |
| **Favicon** | The icon that appears in the browser tab, bookmarks, and Safari previews |
| **Brand colours** | Primary and accent colours applied across buttons, links, and UI highlights |

## Where to find it

1. Open **Settings** in the Partner Portal sidebar.
2. Click **Whitelabel**.
3. Scroll to the **Portal Branding** section and expand it.

## Uploading a logo

1. Click **Upload Logo** (or drag and drop).
2. Use an SVG or PNG with a transparent background for best results.
3. Save the section.

Your logo appears in the customer portal header and on the login/signup pages.

## Uploading a favicon

1. Click **Upload Favicon** (or drag and drop).
2. Use a square image — PNG or ICO, at least 32×32 px. SVG is also accepted.
3. Save the section.

Your favicon appears in the **browser tab**, the **address bar**, iOS and Android **home screen shortcuts**, and **Safari's reading list and bookmarks**.

<Tip>
Favicons render at 16×16 or 32×32 px in most browsers. Keep the design simple — a single initial or small icon reads better than a detailed logo at that size.
</Tip>

### How the favicon works

Your favicon is **server-rendered** — it's baked directly into the HTML of every portal page on first load. This means:

- It shows correctly on the very **first page view**, before any JavaScript runs.
- It appears in **Safari** (which reads icons at page-load time and doesn't update them dynamically).
- It shows in **bookmarks, home screen shortcuts**, and browser history.
- It applies across all white-label subdomains and custom domains.

No extra setup is needed once you've uploaded your favicon and saved.

## Setting brand colours

Use the colour pickers to set:

- **Primary colour** — used for buttons, links, and key UI elements.
- **Accent colour** — used for highlights and secondary elements (if configurable on your plan).

Enter a hex code directly or use the picker. Click **Save** when done.

Changes take effect immediately for all portal visitors.

## Previewing your branding

After saving, open your portal URL (your subdomain or custom domain) in a browser. Check:

- The **browser tab** shows your favicon (not the Knotie logo).
- The **portal header** shows your logo.
- **Buttons and links** use your primary colour.

If you don't see your changes, do a hard refresh (`Ctrl+Shift+R` / `Cmd+Shift+R`) to bypass the browser cache.

## Troubleshooting

**Favicon shows as the Knotie logo after uploading**
- Confirm you clicked **Save** in the Portal Branding section.
- Do a hard refresh (`Ctrl+Shift+R` / `Cmd+Shift+R`). Browsers aggressively cache favicons.
- In Safari, clear the cache under **Develop → Empty Caches** (enable the Develop menu first under Safari → Settings → Advanced).
- If the issue persists more than a few minutes after saving, contact support.

**Favicon doesn't appear on iOS home screen**
- When a visitor adds your portal to their home screen, iOS reads the `apple-touch-icon`. This is set automatically from your uploaded favicon.
- Ensure your image is at least 180×180 px for best quality on high-resolution screens.

**Logo appears blurry**
- Use an SVG for the sharpest result at any resolution. If using PNG, export at 2× or 3× the display size.
