## What Integrations & Approvals mode is

**Integrations & Approvals** is a dashboard mode designed for partners whose product is:

- Connected apps (OAuth integrations, API key connections)
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) access
- Phone number approvals

In this mode the customer portal replaces the standard voice-agent menus with an Integrations-first layout. Customers land on the **Integrations** page instead of the voice dashboard.

<Info>
This is the fourth dashboard mode, alongside **Standard**, **Agency + Infra**, and **Infrastructure**. It is stored as `dashboardModeKey = 'integrations'` and selected separately from the two Boolean flags used by the other three modes.
</Info>

---

## Customer portal layout in this mode

| Section | Menu entries |
|---------|--------------|
| **Core** | Integrations (`/whitelabel/integration`) |
| **Management** | Only the features you have switched on: VPS, AI Gateway, My Minds, the media studios |
| **Settings & Security** | Settings, Billing & Payments, MCP Access, Approvals |

Voice-agent menus (AI Agents, Knowledge Base, Phone Numbers, etc.) are hidden for customers in this mode.

## How to enable Integrations & Approvals mode

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Settings → White-Label Portal**.
2. Scroll to (or click) **Platform Configuration** in the Section Navigation Rail.
3. Click the **Integrations & Approvals** mode card.
   - The selected card shows a check-mark (✓) and a blue border.
   - The change is saved immediately.
4. Customers created after this point will start in Integrations & Approvals mode.

## Access control

Customers must have **Integration** turned on for them (via **Features & Add-ons**) to access the Integrations page. This is enforced server-side on all six integration routes — a customer without the feature flag gets a **403** even if they know the URL.

If **Integration** is off for a customer in this mode, they land on the standard dashboard instead.

<Note>
Switching a partner to Integrations & Approvals mode hides the voice menus in the sidebar but does **not** revoke `showAiAgents` or `showKnowledgeBase`. A customer can still reach those pages by typing the URL directly. To fully restrict access, turn those features off per customer in **Features & Add-ons**.
</Note>

## The v2 Integrations dashboard

Customers in this mode see a dedicated dashboard body with:

- Where to start (getting-connected quick-links)
- MCP access card
- Approvals card
- Billing & credits summary

The dashboard does not show a count of connected apps or pending approvals — those numbers are shown on the Integrations and Approvals pages themselves, not the dashboard.

---

## Existing customers

Switching to Integrations & Approvals mode affects **new** customers only. Existing customers keep their current menu settings. To update existing customers:

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Find the customer and click **Manage**.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.
4. Adjust menu visibility as needed.
5. Save.
