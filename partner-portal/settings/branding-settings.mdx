## What Branding Settings controls

Branding Settings affects how your **customer-facing portal** looks.

The Settings page shows:

- Portal Title
- Portal Slogan
- Portal URL (subdomain or custom domain)
- Logo
- Primary Color
- Secondary Color
- Font Family

## Where to find it

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Branding Settings**.
3. Click **Update Branding**.

## Copy your portal URL

In **Partner Information** or **Branding Settings**, the portal displays your customer portal URL.

Use **Copy URL** to copy it.

If you haven’t configured subdomain/custom domain yet, it will prompt you to configure it in Whitelabel settings.

## How this relates to Whitelabel

Branding Settings controls the look and feel.

For domain setup and portal mode selection, use:

- Whitelabel Subdomain
- Custom Domain
- Portal Modes
