## What Subscription & Billing controls

The **Subscription & Billing** section shows your current plan and billing status.

It can also provide a link to manage payments in Stripe (if available).

## Where to find it

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Subscription & Billing**.

## What you can see

Typical fields include:

- Plan type (example: Free Forever, Starter, Pro, Enterprise)
- Subscription status (ACTIVE / INACTIVE / CANCELLED / CANCELLING)
- Current billing period dates (monthly or annual)
- Payment method (brand, last four digits, expiry) when available
- Upcoming invoice amount when available

## Refresh subscription data

Use the refresh icon to reload the subscription state.

## Manage payment details

If the section shows a **Manage Payment** action, it opens Stripe’s customer portal in a new tab.

## Cancellation

If your plan supports cancellation, the portal may show a cancellation workflow.
