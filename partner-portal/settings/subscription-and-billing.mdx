## What Subscription & Billing controls

The **Plans & Billing** page shows your current plan, your credit auto-recharge settings, and your customer expansion limits.

Billing in Knotie is built around credits and auto-recharge, so this page focuses on those rather than on traditional billing details like billing-period dates, saved-card digits, or upcoming invoices.

## Where to find it

1. Partner Portal → **Settings** → **Plans & Billing** in the sidebar

You'll land on `/partner/settings/billing`.

## What you can see

### Platform Subscription

- **Plan Type** — e.g., Free Forever, Starter, Pro, Ultimate Scaleup Agency
- **Status** — ACTIVE / INACTIVE / CANCELLED / CANCELLING
- **Business Name**
- **Email**
- **Member Since** date

### Customer Expansion Plan

Shows whether your plan includes expanded customer limits:

- "Free tier only — Included in your plan" (on higher tiers)
- Or an upgrade prompt to unlock more capacity

### Credit Auto-Recharge

A major feature for preventing service interruptions:

- **Saved Payment Method** — "No card saved. Buy credits with the 'Save card and enable auto-recharge' checkbox to add one."
- **Auto-Recharge** toggle — disabled until a card is saved
- **Threshold** (credits) — minimum 100 credits = $1.00
- **Amount** (USD) — minimum $20
- **Note:** "The threshold is a soft floor."
- **Recent auto-recharges** — history list

> **Soft floor behavior:** Auto-recharge fires whenever a business operation needs more credits than the current balance, even if you're above the configured threshold.

## Refresh subscription data

Use the **Refresh subscription data** button to reload the current plan state from Stripe.

## Manage payment details

To add or update a payment method:

- Click **Buy credits** to open the credits page.
- Check **"Save card and enable auto-recharge"** during your purchase to save a card on file.

## Cancellation

To cancel or change your plan, contact Knotie support and they'll take care of it for you.
