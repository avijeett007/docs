## Open Integration Pricing

In the Partner Portal, open **Settings → Integration Pricing**.

Use the switch at the top of the page to turn integration charging on or off for the partner account.

![Integration Pricing showing the base rate, margin, and per-app prices](/images/screenshots/integration-pricing-production-2026-09-14.png)

## Set the default price

The page shows the platform base rate and a **Margin multiplier**. Production currently displays a base rate of **0.03 credits per call**. The calculated customer price and margin update as you change the multiplier.

Review the calculated price before saving.

## Set prices for individual apps

Use **Per-app pricing** when an app should not use the default margin.

- **Per-call price** sets the credits charged each time an agent uses that app.
- **Unlock fee** sets a one-time charge before the customer can connect the app.
- **Effective price** shows the resulting per-call charge.

Leave an app on **Use margin** or **Free** to keep the default shown by the page.

## Choose which experiences charge

Under **Per experience**, choose **Inherit**, **On**, or **Off** for each listed experience. **Inherit** follows the partner-wide switch.

## Override one customer

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers**.
2. Select **Manage** for the customer.
3. Open **Features & Add-ons**.
4. Find **Charge for integrations** under **AI Credit Management**.
5. Choose **Inherit partner setting**, **On**, or **Off**.
6. Save the customer.

The customer setting takes priority over the experience and partner defaults. Charges apply only while **AI Credits Enabled** is on for that customer.

## Apps for your customers

**Apps for your customers** sets one list of apps for all your customers, so you don't have to choose apps for each customer one by one.

- **New customers** get this list automatically.
- **Existing customers** get it only when you hand it to them (see below).
- A customer you chose apps for yourself keeps their own list.

Changing the list updates everyone who follows it straight away. Customers still need the **Integration** menu turned on to see their apps.

![Apps for your customers, with the Tick the free apps button and the Free and Standard app groups](/images/screenshots/integration-pricing-apps-for-customers-2026-09-29.png)

### Choose the apps

1. Open **Settings → Integration Pricing** and find **Apps for your customers**.
2. Tick the apps you want. Apps are grouped as **Free**, **Standard**, and **Premium**. To tick every free app at once, select **Tick the free apps**.
3. Select **Save** at the bottom of the page.

If you're on the Free Forever plan, you can only offer free apps.

### Add new apps automatically

Turn on **Automatically add new apps as we release them** to add each new app to your list on the day it's released. This only ever adds apps — anything you've unticked stays off.

### Give the list to existing customers

![The auto-add option and the Existing customers section with the Give them this list button](/images/screenshots/integration-pricing-give-list-2026-09-29.png)

**Existing customers** shows how many customers already follow your list, and how many have no apps of their own.

Select **Give them this list** to hand your list to the customers who have no apps of their own. From then on they follow your list.

<Warning>
**Give them this list** uses the apps ticked on screen right now. Tick the apps you want before you select it.
</Warning>

To give one customer a different set of apps, choose them in that customer's **Edit Apps**. They'll then keep their own list instead of following yours.
