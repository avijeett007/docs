## What Customer Credit Plans are for

**Customer Credit Plans** allow you to create credit packages that customers can purchase directly from their whitelabel portal. This enables self-service credit purchasing without manual intervention.

Use this when:
- You want customers to buy credits on-demand
- You offer multiple pricing tiers
- You use AI Credit-based billing

## Where to find it

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Customer Credit Plans** section.
3. Or navigate to **Settings → Customer Credit Plans** (if separate page).

## Prerequisites

Before creating credit plans, you need:

1. **Not on Free Forever plan** – Credit plans require a paid plan (Starter, Pro, or Enterprise)
2. **Stripe Connect configured** – Payment processing requires Stripe
   - Connect Stripe account
   - Complete onboarding
   - Enable charges

If prerequisites aren't met, you'll see an upgrade prompt or Stripe setup instructions.

## Create a credit plan

### Basic credit plan

1. Click **Create Credit Plan**.
2. Fill in the plan details:
   - **Name** – Display name (e.g., "Starter Pack", "Pro Bundle")
   - **Credits** – Number of credits in the package (e.g., 1000, 5000, 10000)
   - **Price** – Dollar amount customers pay (e.g., $49, $199, $899)
   - **Description** – Optional details about the plan
3. Toggle **Active** to make it visible to customers.
4. Toggle **Popular** to highlight it (only one plan can be popular).
5. Click **Create**.

The plan is now available for customers to purchase in their portal.

![Create Credit Plan Setup](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-221530.png)

### Plan pricing strategy

When setting prices, consider:

**Cost-plus pricing**
- Calculate your actual credit costs
- Add your desired margin
- Set competitive pricing

**Example:**
- 1000 credits cost you $30
- Add 60% margin = $48
- Round to $49 for clean pricing

**Tiered discounting**
- Small package: $0.05 per credit
- Medium package: $0.04 per credit (20% discount)
- Large package: $0.03 per credit (40% discount)

## Manage existing plans

### Edit a credit plan

1. Find the plan in the Credit Plans list.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update any fields (name, credits, price, description).
4. Save changes.

**Note:** Editing a plan doesn't affect credits already purchased by customers.

### Activate or deactivate a plan

**To deactivate:**
1. Toggle the **Active** switch to OFF.
2. The plan disappears from customer portals immediately.

**To reactivate:**
1. Toggle the **Active** switch to ON.
2. The plan becomes visible again.

**Why deactivate instead of delete:**
- Preserve purchase history
- Seasonal offerings (e.g., holiday discounts)
- A/B testing different price points

![Credit Plans Management List](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-221530.png)

### Mark a plan as Popular

Highlight your best-value plan to drive sales:

1. Toggle **Popular** to ON for one plan.
2. The plan displays with a "Popular" badge in the customer portal.
3. Only one plan can be marked as Popular at a time.

**Best practice:** Mark your most purchased or highest-margin plan as Popular.

### Delete a credit plan

1. Click the **Delete** button (trash icon) on a plan.
2. Confirm deletion.

**Warning:** Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. However, existing purchases remain in customer history.

**When to delete:**
- Plan was created by mistake
- Permanently discontinuing an offering
- Cleaning up test plans

## How customers purchase credits

### Customer view

In the whitelabel customer portal:

1. Customer navigates to **Credits** or **Billing**.
2. Sees available credit plans with:
   - Plan name
   - Credit amount
   - Price
   - Description
   - "Popular" badge (if applicable)
3. Clicks **Purchase** on desired plan.
4. Completes Stripe checkout.
5. Credits are automatically added to their account.

![Customer Portal - Credit Plans View](/Screenshot2025-12-19at12.21.22AM.png)

### Payment flow

1. **Customer selects plan** – Clicks Purchase button
2. **Stripe checkout opens** – Secure payment form
3. **Payment processed** – Stripe handles transaction
4. **Credits added** – Automatically credited to account
5. **Confirmation email** – Customer receives receipt (if email configured)

### Failed purchases

If a payment fails:
- Customer sees error message
- No credits are added
- Customer can retry purchase
- You can view failed attempts in Stripe dashboard

## Credit plan analytics

### Track plan performance

Monitor which plans customers purchase:

1. Go to **Billing & Invoices** or **Analytics**.
2. View credit purchase metrics:
   - Most purchased plan
   - Total revenue per plan
   - Average purchase value
   - Purchase frequency

Use this data to:
- Optimize pricing
- Retire underperforming plans
- Create targeted promotions

## How credit plans work

### Credit delivery

When a customer completes a purchase:

1. **Payment validated** – Stripe confirms successful charge
2. **Credits added** – Exact amount from plan added to customer balance
3. **Transaction logged** – Recorded in customer's credit history
4. **Balance updated** – New balance reflects immediately in portal

### Refunds and adjustments

If you need to refund a credit purchase:

1. **Refund in Stripe** – Process refund through Stripe dashboard
2. **Manually deduct credits** – Use the customer's Credits tab to deduct the amount
3. **Add note** – Document the refund reason in the transaction note

**Automatic refunds** are not currently supported - you must manually adjust credits.

## Best practices

### Plan structure

- **Offer 3-5 plans** – Not too many, not too few (analysis paralysis vs limited choice)
- **Use psychological pricing** – $49 instead of $50, $99 instead of $100
- **Clear differentiation** – Each plan should offer clear value differences
- **Bundle discounts** – Larger packages should have better per-credit pricing

### Naming conventions

Good names:
- "Starter Pack" - 1,000 credits
- "Pro Bundle" - 5,000 credits
- "Enterprise Package" - 10,000 credits

Avoid:
- Generic names ("Plan 1", "Option B")
- Confusing names ("Super Mega Ultra Plan")
- Overly long names

### Pricing psychology

- **Anchor with highest price** – Show most expensive first to make others seem reasonable
- **Popular badge** – Nudge customers toward your preferred option
- **Limited offers** – Create urgency with seasonal or promotional plans

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot create credit plans
- **Error: "Upgrade required"** – You're on Free Forever plan, upgrade needed
- **Error: "Stripe not connected"** – Complete Stripe Connect setup first
- **Plans not saving** – Check all required fields are filled

### Customers can't see plans
- Verify plans are toggled **Active**
- Confirm customer has portal access enabled
- Check whitelabel portal is configured
- Ensure customer's email domain is verified

### Purchases not completing
- Check Stripe account status (charges enabled)
- Verify webhook configuration
- Review Stripe logs for errors
- Confirm credit plan IDs match in database

### Credits not added after purchase
- Check transaction in customer's credit history
- Verify webhook received from Stripe
- Manual adjustment may be needed
- Contact support if persistent
