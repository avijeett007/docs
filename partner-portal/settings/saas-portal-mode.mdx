## What SaaS Portal Mode is for

**SaaS Portal Mode** transforms your whitelabel portal into an enterprise-grade funnel experience with:

- Automated prospect onboarding
- Self-service customer signup
- Built-in AI Receptionist landing pages
- Free trial capabilities
- Advanced conversion tracking

This mode is ideal for agencies running a "productized" SaaS offering instead of traditional done-for-you services.

## Portal modes explained

Knotie AI Pro offers three portal modes:

### Basic Mode (Default)
- Traditional customer management portal
- Manual customer onboarding
- Simple, straightforward interface
- Available on all plans

### Professional Mode
- Enhanced portal with premium branding
- Advanced customization options
- Professional theme templates
- Available on Starter and above

### SaaS Mode (Enterprise)
- Full funnel automation
- Self-service signup flows
- AI Receptionist landing pages
- Prospect management system
- Available on Starter, Pro, Enterprise, Ultimate tiers

![Portal Mode Selection](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-221026.png)

## Prerequisites

To use SaaS Portal Mode, you need:

1. **Paid plan** – Starter, Pro, Enterprise, or Ultimate tier
2. **Whitelabel portal configured** – Subdomain or custom domain set up
3. **Stripe Connect** – For processing customer payments (optional but recommended)
4. **Manual enablement** – Special offers may provide SaaS mode access on lower tiers

## Enable SaaS Portal Mode

### Step 1: Select SaaS mode

1. Go to **Settings → Whitelabel**.
2. Find the **Portal Mode** section.
3. Click on **SaaS Portal**.
4. If you don't have access, you'll see an upgrade prompt.

### Step 2: Configure SaaS settings

Once SaaS mode is selected, the **SaaS Portal Configuration** section appears:

1. **Character Name** (Optional)
   - Add a character name for personalized branding (e.g., "Sarah", "Alex")
   - If left empty, uses your business name
   - This appears in the AI Receptionist onboarding flow

2. **Free Trial Enabled**
   - Toggle ON to show "Start Free Trial" button on landing page
   - Allows prospects to try before buying
   - Default: Enabled

3. **SaaS Onboarding Enabled**
   - Toggle ON to enable the 10-step onboarding flow
   - Prospects fill out business info, connect knowledge base, complete payment, and then deploy the agent
   - Step 10 deploys the AI agent — only after Step 9 (Pricing & Payment) has cleared

4. **Free AI Credits**
   - Set initial credits for new signups (e.g., 50, 100, 500)
   - Helps prospects test without payment
   - Default: 50 credits

5. **Pricing Model**
   - **Subscription** – Monthly/annual plans
   - **Pay-as-you-go** – Usage-based billing
   - **Fixed Price** – One-time setup fee

6. **Pay-as-you-go Rate**
   - Set rate per minute if using pay-as-you-go model
   - Example: $0.10 per minute
   - Only applies if pricing model is "Pay-as-you-go"

![SaaS Portal Configuration Panel](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-221234.png)

### Step 3: Configure agent tier

Choose which AI agent tier prospects get:

- **Essentials** – Basic AI capabilities
- **Professional** – Enhanced features
- **Enterprise** – Full capabilities

This determines what's available to customers who sign up through your SaaS portal.

### Step 4: Save configuration

1. Scroll to the bottom.
2. Click **Save Settings**.
3. SaaS mode is now active on your whitelabel portal.

### 10-step onboarding flow

> The 10-step onboarding flow is available **only in SaaS portal mode**.

Prospects who click "Start Free Trial" go through a guided onboarding:

1. **Business Info** – Company name, website
   ![Step 1: Business Info](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-194032.png)
2. **Website Verification** – Validate business website
   ![Step 2: Website Verification](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-200344.png)
3. **Contact Details** – Email, phone
   ![Step 3: Contact Details](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-194646.png)
4. **Service Categories** – What the business offers
   ![Step 4: Service Categories](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-194758.png)
5. **Knowledge Base** – Upload FAQs, documents
   ![Step 5: Knowledge Base](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-194858.png)
6. **Greeting Setup** – Configure AI greeting message
   ![Step 6: Greeting Setup](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-194933.png)
7. **Information Collection** – What data to collect from callers
   ![Step 7: Information Collection](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-195016.png)
8. **Communication Settings** – Email/SMS preferences
   ![Step 8: Communication Settings](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-195043.png)
9. **Pricing & Payment** – Review the plan summary and complete payment via Stripe Checkout (subscription, fixed price, or pay-as-you-go). Free / pay-as-you-go prospects are forwarded straight through.
   ![Step 9: Review and Pricing](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-195117.png)
   ![Step 9: Pricing Plan](/images/screenshots/screenshot-2026-03-19-195145.png)
10. **Deploy Agent** – Final activation. The page checks that payment has cleared, then provisions the phone number, trains the AI, configures integrations, and enables AI Credits.

**At Step 3:** Prospect converts to Customer (moves from Prospects tab to Customers tab)

**At Step 9:** Customer credential is created in `suspended` state. Stripe Checkout opens in the partner's Connect account. After successful payment the credential is flipped to `active` and the prospect is advanced to Step 10. Free Forever and pay-as-you-go portals skip the payment leg.

**At Step 10:** The Deploy Agent screen runs a server-side payment check (`GET /api/whitelabel/check-onboarding-payment`). If the credential is still suspended (payment cancelled or pending), the user is bounced back to Step 9. Otherwise the **Deploy** button becomes available and one click provisions the AI Receptionist end-to-end.

#### Payment gate — what each pricing model does at Step 9

| Pricing model | Step 9 behavior | When Step 10 deploy unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription** | Opens a recurring Stripe Checkout session via partner's Connect account | After webhook confirms the subscription is active |
| **Fixed Price (one-time)** | Opens a one-off Stripe Checkout (mode: `payment`) | After `POST /api/whitelabel/verify-customer-price-payment` activates the credential |
| **Fixed Price (recurring — month/year)** | Opens a subscription Stripe Checkout with the partner's interval | Same as above — verification flips credential to active |
| **Pay-as-you-go** | Skips checkout — prospect is moved straight to Step 10 | Immediately on arrival |
| **Free / no model configured** | Skips checkout — prospect is moved straight to Step 10 | Immediately on arrival |

> If the payment-success page errors or the prospect closes the browser before verification finishes, the Step 10 gate will redirect them back to Step 9 so they can retry without losing onboarding data.

## Auto-deploy configuration

When SaaS onboarding is enabled, auto-deploy activates AI agents automatically:

### How auto-deploy works

1. **Prospect completes Step 9** – Pricing & Payment cleared (or skipped for free / pay-as-you-go)
2. **Step 10 gate runs** – `/api/whitelabel/check-onboarding-payment` confirms the credential is `active`
3. **Prospect clicks Deploy** – `/api/whitelabel/save-onboarding-details` runs the full provision
4. **System validates** – Checks for credits, phone number, agent configuration
5. **Agent deployed** – AI Receptionist is activated
6. **Customer notified** – Deployment notification email sent to the partner; setup email sent to the customer (if configured)
7. **Ready for calls** – Agent starts handling calls immediately

### Auto-deploy requirements

For auto-deploy to work, ensure:
- **Free AI Credits configured** – Customers need credits to use the agent
- **Default agent selected** – Set which agent template to deploy
- **Phone number assignment** – Configure number provisioning (optional)

## Pricing model configuration

### Subscription pricing

For subscription-based SaaS:

1. Set **Pricing Model** to "Subscription".
2. Configure subscription plans in **Subscription Plans** section.
3. Add monthly/annual tiers (Basic, Pro, Enterprise).
4. Enable Stripe for recurring billing.

<Info>
**Step 9 plan display:** All plans with **Active** toggled ON are shown to prospects at Step 9 (Summary & Deploy). See [Subscription Plans](/partner-portal/settings/subscription-plans) for visibility rules and the constraint that applies when you're on subscription billing.
</Info>

### Pay-as-you-go pricing

For usage-based billing:

1. Set **Pricing Model** to "Pay-as-you-go".
2. Set **Pay-as-you-go Rate** (e.g., $0.10 per minute).
3. Customers are charged based on actual usage.
4. Requires AI Credits system enabled.

### Fixed price

For one-time setup fees or simple recurring access:

1. Set **Pricing Model** to "Fixed Price".
2. Fill in the three Fixed Price fields:
   - **Fixed Price** – Amount the customer pays (e.g., `499`)
   - **Fixed Price Currency** – Three-letter ISO code (default `USD`)
   - **Fixed Price Period** – `one_time`, `month`, or `year`
3. Customers checkout at Step 9 via Stripe Checkout running on your Connect account:
   - `one_time` → Stripe `payment` mode, single charge
   - `month` / `year` → Stripe `subscription` mode with that interval
4. After payment succeeds, the customer credential is activated and the prospect lands on Step 10 to deploy.

> The partner must have **Stripe Connect onboarding completed** and **charges enabled** for the Fixed Price checkout to be created. Missing Stripe setup returns `STRIPE_SETUP_INCOMPLETE` and blocks the customer at Step 9.

## Troubleshooting

### SaaS mode not available
- **Upgrade required** – You're on Free Forever plan, upgrade to Starter or above
- **Contact support** – Request manual SaaS mode enablement

### Landing page not showing
- **Portal mode not saved** – Verify SaaS mode is selected and saved
- **DNS not configured** – Check custom domain is verified
- **Cache issue** – Clear browser cache and try in incognito mode

### Onboarding flow not working
- **SaaS Onboarding disabled** – Toggle "SaaS Onboarding Enabled" to ON
- **Missing configuration** – Ensure all SaaS settings are filled in
- **Free credits not set** – Configure Free AI Credits amount

### Customer stuck on Step 9 (payment loop)
- **Stripe Connect not configured** – Finish Stripe Connect onboarding under Settings → Stripe Connect, then retry
- **Charges not enabled** – Stripe is still reviewing the account; checkout cannot be created
- **Fixed Price missing** – With pricing model set to `fixedprice` but no `fixedPrice` value, the checkout call returns `No fixed price configured`
- **Payment cancelled** – The cancel URL drops the prospect back at Step 9; their data is preserved

### Customer paid but Step 10 sends them back to Step 9
- **Verification not run** – The `payment-success` page calls `/api/whitelabel/verify-customer-price-payment`; check the network tab for non-2xx responses
- **Stripe session not yet `paid`** – Webhooks may lag a few seconds; refresh after 30s
- **Wrong partner context** – The session is retrieved on the partner's Connect account; mismatched `partnerId` returns a 500

### Auto-deploy failing
- **Insufficient credits** – Increase Free AI Credits allocation
- **No agent selected** – Configure default agent template
- **Integration missing** – Check provider API keys are configured

## Best practices

- **Test the full funnel** – Go through onboarding as a prospect before going live
- **Set appropriate free credits** – Enough to test but not too much to abuse
- **Monitor drop-off points** – Improve steps where prospects abandon
- **A/B test pricing** – Try different models and rates
- **Provide clear value propositions** – Explain benefits on landing page
- **Optimize for mobile** – Many prospects will sign up on mobile devices
