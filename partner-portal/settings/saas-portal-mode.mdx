## Choose SaaS portal mode

1. Open **Settings → Whitelabel**.
2. Find **Portal Mode**.
3. Select **SaaS Portal**.
4. Review the portal theme and language, then save.

SaaS portal mode is designed for a customer-facing signup and onboarding experience. If the option is unavailable, use the upgrade action shown in the portal or contact support.

## Configure AI Receptionist

AI Receptionist settings now have their own page:

1. Open **One-Click Experiences**.
2. Find **AI Receptionist**.
3. Select **Settings**.

From there, you can configure the free-trial action, automatic setup, starting credits, agent tier, pricing, business lookup limits, and landing page style.

See [AI Receptionist settings](/partner-portal/experiences/ai-receptionist-settings) for the full steps.

## Review before publishing

1. Save your changes.
2. Use **View live** to open the customer-facing page.
3. Check the branding, pricing, signup actions, and legal links.
4. Complete a test signup before sharing the page with customers.

<Tip>
The settings page shows current rates and available options. Review those values each time you change the offer instead of relying on an older saved price list.
</Tip>
