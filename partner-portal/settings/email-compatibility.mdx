## Overview

All platform emails sent to your customers and team members — portal invites, welcome messages, password resets, credit alerts, and team invitations — now use a **table-based layout with 100% inline styles**. This ensures correct rendering across every major email client, including Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and mobile apps.

## What changed

Previously, some platform emails used `<div>` layouts with embedded CSS `<style>` blocks. Gmail strips `<style>` blocks from email `<head>` sections entirely, which caused those emails to appear as unstyled white text on a white background. Recipients needed to click **"View entire message"** to see any content — meaning most branded emails went unread or appeared broken.

The following email types were updated:

| Email | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Customer portal invite** | Partner invites a customer to their portal |
| **Welcome email** | Customer completes portal signup |
| **Partner team invite** | Partner adds a team member |
| **Customer team invite** | Customer invites a team member to their sub-account |
| **Marketing welcome** | New partner completes registration |
| **Customer team password reset** | Team member requests a password reset |
| **Waitlist confirmation** | New sign-up joins the waitlist |
| **Insufficient telephony credit** | Customer's telephony credit runs low |

## What partners see

No configuration change is needed. All fixes are applied automatically at the platform level.

Your emails will now:

- Render fully in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and iOS/Android mail apps on first open — no extra clicks required
- Show a real **inbox preview line** (preheader text) instead of a blank body in Gmail's inbox list
- Display the correct branded layout including logos, colours, and buttons

## Inbox preview text

Each email now includes a hidden preheader — a short summary line that appears next to the subject in the inbox before a recipient opens the message. This improves open rates by giving recipients a meaningful preview instead of blank space.

## Troubleshooting

**Emails still look plain or unstyled**
- Check that your custom SMTP or Domain Email Service is correctly configured under **Settings → Email Settings**. Delivery issues (bounces, spam filtering, or SMTP errors) can cause the platform to fall back to a minimal format.
- If you are using a custom SMTP server, verify your SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records are set correctly. Misconfigured DNS records can cause Gmail to flag your mail and suppress images or formatting as a security precaution.

**Emails land in spam**
- Authenticate your sending domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). See [Email settings](/partner-portal/settings/email-settings) for setup guidance.
- Avoid spam trigger words in your custom email branding fields.
