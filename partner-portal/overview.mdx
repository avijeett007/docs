## What the Partner Portal is

The **Partner Portal** is your main dashboard for managing your agency account. From here you can:

- Onboard and manage customers
- Create and configure AI voice agents
- Monitor usage and billing
- Set up white-label branding for your customer-facing portal
- Manage your team members
- Access marketing resources

## Navigation

The sidebar is your primary navigation. It groups functionality into these main areas:

| Section | What you’ll find there |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Customers** | Customer list, onboarding, features, credits, billing |
| **AI Agents** | Agent management, voices, knowledge bases, phone numbers |
| **AI Usage** | Call analytics, cost tracking, provider-level breakdowns |
| **Billing** | Invoices, subscription, credit top-ups |
| **Marketing** | Ready-to-use videos, posts, demo tools |
| **Settings** | Integration keys, email, branding, Stripe Connect, whitelabel |
| **Team** | Invite and manage team members |

## Theme-aware design

The Partner Portal renders using your white-label theme. All pages — including Marketing, Settings, Analytics, and Whitelabel configuration — respect the CSS design tokens configured in **Settings → Whitelabel**. If you have a custom brand, screenshots you take will reflect those colors.
