## What the Partner Portal is

The **Partner Portal** is where you run Knotie AI Pro as an agency: onboard customers, connect AI agent providers, manage phone numbers, track usage, and handle billing.

If you’re new, your goal is simple:

1. Get your own account set up.
2. Onboard your first customer.
3. Connect an agent provider (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, GHL, or N8N).
4. Assign a phone number and test calls.
5. Track usage and keep credits topped up.

## Sidebar reference

The sidebar is divided into sections. Items with a **Premium Feature** badge require a paid plan upgrade. Items shown as buttons (not links) indicate features that open a panel or modal rather than navigating to a new page.

![Partner Portal sidebar reference](/images/partner-sidebar-reference.png)

## What each sidebar item is for

**CUSTOMERS**
- **Customers** — Manage active customers, create accounts, control portal access. Includes **Prospects** tab for eligible plans (Enterprise).

**AI PLATFORM**
- **AI Agents** *(Premium Feature, button)* — Opens the AI agent management panel.
- **Phone Numbers** *(button)* — Import or manage phone numbers, assign to customers and agents.
- **AI Gateway NEW** *(link)* — OpenAI-compatible API keys. Create partner-owned keys, manage model access, budgets, and customer self-service keys.
- **Agent Analytics** *(Premium Feature, link)* — Call counts, duration, cost breakdown per agent.

**INFRASTRUCTURE**
- **VPS Servers** *(button)* — Provision and manage virtual private servers for customers.

**GROW**
- **One-Click Experiences** *(link)* — Launch prebuilt experiences and growth-focused workflows. Availability depends on plan level.

**ACCOUNT**
- **Tutorials** — Video tutorials plus the in-app floating video help.
- **Team Management** *(Premium Feature, link)* — Invite teammates, manage roles, and stay within your plan's team limit.
- **Settings**
  - **Profile Settings** — Business info, contact details, integration tokens.
  - **Partner API Keys** — Manage API keys for programmatic access.
  - **Email Domain Management** *(Premium Feature)* — Configure custom email domains.
  - **Plans & Billing** — View and manage your plan and billing.
  - **White-Label Portal** *(Premium Feature)* — Subdomain and custom domain configuration.

## Pricing Calculator

The **Pricing Calculator** helps you estimate monthly costs before onboarding a customer. Access it by clicking the **Pricing Calculator** button on the Dashboard.

### How the calculator works

1. **Select minutes per month** – Choose the expected call volume for the customer.
2. **Review cost breakdown** – The calculator shows:
   - AI provider costs (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, etc.)
   - Email notification costs
   - SMS notification costs
   - Platform fees
3. **Compare providers** – See side-by-side cost comparisons across different AI providers to choose the most cost-effective option for your use case.

### Why use the Pricing Calculator

- **Accurate quoting** – Give customers realistic pricing before they commit.
- **Margin planning** – Understand your costs to set profitable markups.
- **Provider selection** – Compare costs across providers to optimize your stack.

## A simple “first week” setup checklist

1. Complete **Signup** and enable **Passkeys** and **MFA**.
2. Add your first customer using **Customer List**.
3. Set up portal access (subdomain or custom domain).
4. Import or create an AI agent.
5. Add/assign a phone number.
6. Check **Agent Analytics** and **Credits**.

## Tips

- If you see a feature marked **Locked** or “Upgrade required,” it usually means your current plan doesn’t include it.
- Use the floating video help in the Partner Portal for page-specific walkthroughs.
