## Overview

**Daily Challenges** are milestone tasks published by the Knotie team inside your partner dashboard. Completing a challenge earns **reward credits** deposited directly to your account balance, and can optionally advance your **Partner Level** — a progression metric (0–10) that Knotie uses to power targeted marketing campaigns and future feature unlocks.

## How challenges work

1. A challenge card appears in your dashboard with a title, description, and ordered list of steps.
2. Complete each step — steps can include watching a video, connecting a feature, onboarding a customer, or similar actions.
3. On completion, **reward credits** are deposited to your account balance automatically.
4. If the challenge carries a **Grants Level** value, your partner level is promoted to at least that level.

## Partner levels

Each partner account has an associated level (0–10). Levels only ever increase — the platform never decrements a level once set.

**Factors that determine your level:**

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Feature adoption | Connecting a voice provider, onboarding customers, configuring white-label, etc. |
| Completed challenges | Challenges with a **Grants Level** promote you to at least that level |

**What your level affects:**

- Tags in Knotie's internal partner CRM used for targeted email campaigns and support prioritisation.
- Access to level-gated promotions and announcements sent directly by Knotie.
- (Future) Unlocked features surfaced automatically on the dashboard.

### Approximate level milestones

| Level | Typical milestone |
|-------|------------------|
| 0 | Account created |
| 1 | Onboarding wizard completed |
| 2 | First customer onboarded |
| 3 | AI agent + phone number connected |
| 4 | White-label portal configured |
| 5+ | Advanced adoption (Stripe Connect, metered billing, team seats) |

<Note>
  Exact thresholds are managed by the Knotie platform and may change over time. Contact [support@knotie-ai.pro](mailto:support@knotie-ai.pro) if you believe your level is incorrect.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can my level decrease?**
No. Partner levels only increase — the platform never decrements a level once set.

**How often do new challenges appear?**
Challenges may be scheduled for specific dates or run indefinitely. Check the challenge card in your dashboard for any scheduled date indicator.

**Where do earned credits appear?**
Credits are deposited to your account balance and visible in the **Credits** section of the Partner Portal.

**Can I complete a challenge more than once?**
No. Each challenge is completed once per partner account.

## For admins: challenge configuration

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the admin Daily Challenges Manager showing a challenge creation or edit form with the "Grants Level" field._

Challenge metadata is managed from the Knotie internal admin panel and is not editable by partners. When creating or editing a challenge, admins configure:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Title | Challenge display name |
| Description | What the partner needs to accomplish |
| Steps | Ordered task list (each step requires `id`, `title`, and `type`) |
| Reward Credits | Credits deposited on completion (default 50) |
| Requires Proof | Whether the partner must submit proof before credit is granted |
| Scheduled Date | Optional date the challenge becomes active |
| **Grants Level** | Optional integer 0–10: completing this challenge promotes the partner to at least this level |

If **Grants Level** is left blank, the challenge still records a `challenge-{id}-complete` tag against the partner in the internal CRM but does not change their numeric level.

### Admin API

**Create a challenge:**
```http
POST /api/admin/challenges
```

**Update a challenge:**
```http
PUT /api/admin/challenges/:challengeId
```

Both endpoints accept a `grantsLevel` field in the JSON body:

```json
{
  "title": "Connect your first voice agent",
  "steps": [
    { "id": "step-1", "title": "Go to AI Agents", "type": "navigate" },
    { "id": "step-2", "title": "Import a Retell or VAPI agent", "type": "action" }
  ],
  "rewardCredits": 50,
  "grantsLevel": 3
}
```

`grantsLevel` must be a non-negative integer between **0 and 10**, or `null` to remove a previously set grant.

**Error responses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 400 | `grantsLevel` is outside the valid range (0–10) or is not an integer |
| 401 | Admin authentication required |
