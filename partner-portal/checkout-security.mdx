## Overview

All Stripe Checkout sessions created on your behalf — for experience packages, AI Business Audit, credit top-ups, and Resell AI Gateway subscriptions — include security controls that prevent payment redirect flows from landing your customers on any domain you do not own.

This page explains what those controls are and what they mean for you as a partner and for your customers.

---

## Redirect pinning

After a customer completes (or cancels) a Stripe Checkout, they are redirected back to a URL configured by the platform. Knotie AI Pro enforces that this redirect URL is always on **your own partner domain**.

### What this means

- A customer who pays through your AI Business Audit checkout lands back on your domain (`your-agency.com/audit/thank-you/…`)
- A customer who buys an Experience package lands on your domain (e.g., `your-agency.com/websites?checkout=success`)
- A customer who tops up credits is returned to your whitelabel dashboard (`your-agency.com/whitelabel/dashboard?payment=success`)

No post-payment page ever redirects to an external URL, even if one is supplied in the checkout request.

### Your custom domain must be verified

The redirect target is derived from your **verified** custom domain. An unverified custom domain is treated the same as no custom domain — the fallback is your subdomain (`yoursubdomain.knotie-ai.pro`).

This protects your customers: if your custom domain is not yet serving the portal, directing a paying customer there would strand them on a broken page.

To verify your custom domain: **Partner Portal → Settings → Whitelabel Settings → Custom Domain**.

## Email links from lead capture

When a prospect submits an AI Business Audit form or books through an Experience landing page, they receive a confirmation email with a link to the audit running page or booking confirmation.

This link is derived from your partner record's verified domain — it does not use the visitor's browser host. This means:

- The link in the email is always on your own domain
- Your DKIM/SPF-aligned sender identity and your domain match — the email looks (and is) fully legitimate

## Credit purchase amount validation

When purchasing AI Credits through the whitelabel customer portal or via the API:

- `creditAmount` must be a **whole number**
- Minimum: **1 credit**
- Maximum: **1,000,000 credits**

Requests outside this range return an HTTP `400` with a descriptive error message:

```
creditAmount must be a number between 1 and 1000000
```

This prevents NaN or out-of-range values from reaching Stripe.

## Affected flows

| Flow | Checkout route | Redirect protected |
|------|---------------|--------------------|
| AI Business Audit checkout | `/api/whitelabel/audit/[prospectId]/checkout/[packageId]` | ✅ Pinned to partner domain |
| Experience package checkout | `/api/whitelabel/experience-checkout/[alias]` | ✅ Pinned to partner domain |
| AI Credit top-up | `/api/whitelabel/purchase-credits` | ✅ Pinned to partner domain |
| Resell AI Gateway subscription | `/api/whitelabel/resell-ai-gateway/checkout` | ✅ Pinned to partner domain |
| AI Business Audit lead email | `/api/whitelabel/experience-leads/[alias]` | ✅ Uses partner record domain |

## What to do if redirects land on the wrong domain

1. **Check your custom domain verification status** — go to **Settings → Whitelabel Settings → Custom Domain** and confirm the domain shows as verified.
2. **Check your subdomain** — if no custom domain is configured, redirects fall back to `yoursubdomain.knotie-ai.pro`. Ensure your subdomain is set in **Settings → Whitelabel Settings**.
3. If neither is set, the redirect falls back to the platform root (`knotie-ai.pro`). Contact support to review your partner account setup.
