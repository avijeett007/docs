## What you can do

The VPS section of the Partner Portal lets you:

1. **Purchase VPS servers** for your customers (6 plan tiers, $6.98–$75.57/mo)
2. **Create App Packages** — bundle apps like OpenClaw with a VPS plan and set your own price
3. **Control visibility** — choose which plans your customers can see

---

## App Packages

In **VPS Settings**, scroll to **App Packages** to bundle apps with a VPS plan and sell them as a ready-to-go offering.

![VPS Settings — App Packages section](/images/screenshots/vps-settings-app-packages-annotated.png)

### Create a package

1. Click **New Package**
2. Give the package a name (e.g. "AI Starter Stack")
3. Select which apps to include — OpenClaw is automatically configured with a Knotie API key for each customer
4. Set your price for the bundle
5. Click **Save**

When a customer purchases a package, the platform automatically provisions the apps and configures OpenClaw with their gateway API key — no manual setup needed.

---

## VPS Settings

In **VPS Settings** you can configure how VPS purchases work for you and your customers:

**Enable VPS for Customers** — toggle on to let customers purchase VPS from their whitelabel portal

**Customer Margin (%)** — markup added to your partner cost before showing the price to customers. Example: Cloud VPS 10 costs you $6.98/mo, at 20% margin customers see $8.38/mo. Can be overridden per customer.

**Display Currency** — choose the currency shown to customers (USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, CAD, SGD, INR). Your billing from Knotie is always in USD.

**Plan Visibility** — choose which server plans customers can see. All 6 plans are shown by default.

---

## OpenClaw Auto-Deploy

When OpenClaw is included in a package (or deployed via the server panel), the platform **automatically generates a Knotie API key** for each customer and configures OpenClaw with it. Keys are managed in the customer's gateway settings — no manual configuration required.
