## What Cost Tracking is

The **Cost Tracking** section of Mission Control (navigate to **Mission Control → Costs**) gives you a real-time view of your platform's operating costs — what you spend to deliver the service — and how those costs relate to the credits you charge partners and customers.

Access requires admin login with MFA.

## Domains covered

Cost Tracking is organised into five views:

### Overview

A cross-domain summary showing:

- Total platform COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) vs credits charged, with margin
- Per-domain breakdown in a table (Media Studio, Director LLM, AI Gateway, VPS)
- Stacked time-series chart of costs over time
- Top partners across all domains
- Data freshness indicator

### Media Studio

Generation costs for the Generative Media Studio:

- **COGS vs credits charged** — quoted generation cost (`cogsUsd`) versus partner and customer credits
- **By provider / model / modality / status / surface** — filter spend by provider, model type (image, video, audio), generation status, and where the job came from (portal, MCP, agent)
- **Refunds and net** — refunded credit amount subtracted from totals
- **Refusal reasons** — breakdown of why jobs were blocked (likeness, copyright, content policy)
- **Top partners and customers by spend**
- **Paginated job drill-down** with CSV export

<Note>
Media Studio COGS figures are **quoted cost** (what the provider quoted at submission time), not reconciled actuals. Figures are labelled accordingly.
</Note>

### Director LLM

Internal platform LLM usage — every call the platform makes using its own AI keys (film agent director, storyboard assistant, script breakdown, shot composer, brand kit, persona planning, super-analytics, and others):

- **Tokens and USD** — prompt tokens, completion tokens, and estimated cost per feature
- **By feature / model / provider / partner** — filter by which platform feature drove the spend
- **Cost source** — whether the cost was derived from the provider's response header, the model registry, or a static fallback (labelled "est." when a fallback is used)

<Note>
This meter tracks LLM calls made with the **platform's own keys** (e.g. `LITELLM_MASTER_KEY`). Customer AI Gateway usage appears in the AI Gateway view, not here.
</Note>

### AI Gateway

Vendor spend for customer-facing AI Gateway keys:

- **Vendor spend vs credits charged** — what was paid to the AI provider vs what was billed to partners and customers
- **Margin** — difference between credits charged and vendor cost
- **Tokens** — by model, provider, and source
- **Top partners / customers / keys by spend**
- **Spend drill-down** — per-request detail with model, provider, token counts, latency, partner, and customer
- CSV export

### VPS

VPS fleet costs:

- **Fleet overview** — active instances, total vendor monthly run-rate, platform and partner margin
- **By plan / region / interval / status**
- **Monthly charges** — billing events over time
- **Lifecycle time series** — provisioning, termination, and renewal events
- **Top partners and customers by VPS spend**
- **Instance drill-down** — per-instance detail
- Coupon and discount tracking

<Note>
VPS vendor cost is based on the Contabo catalog snapshot at provisioning time, not prorated actuals.
</Note>

## Filtering and date ranges

Every domain view shares:

- **Date range presets** — Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, custom range
- **Partner search** — combobox that searches partners by name or ID
- **Customer search** — combobox that searches customers by name or ID
- **URL-persisted filters** — share a filtered view with a teammate via a direct link

## CSV export

Most drill-down tables have a **Download CSV** button. The export respects the current date range and filter selection.

## Partner detail shortcut

On the partner detail page in Mission Control, a **Costs** button opens the Cost Tracking view pre-filtered to that partner across all domains.

## Access and rate limiting

- Requires admin session with MFA (Mission Control login).
- Cost API routes are protected by a dedicated 120 requests/minute rate limit bucket.
- All query results are bounded to prevent runaway queries.

## Honesty labels

The dashboard surfaces several estimates and approximations. Where a figure is not exact, it carries an explicit label:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Quoted COGS | Provider's quoted cost at job submission; not reconciled against actual invoice |
| est. | USD cost estimated from the model registry or a static fallback (not from the provider's response) |
| Run-rate | Monthly cost extrapolated from the current fleet, not a prorated invoice amount |
