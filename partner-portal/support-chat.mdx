## Ask Katie

Select the floating **Ask Katie** button in the lower-right corner of the public site or Partner Portal. Katie can answer product questions and help you find settings.

Type a question and send it. If a microphone button is available in your browser, you can also dictate the question and review the text before sending.

![Katie support chat open in the Partner Portal](/images/screenshots/katie-chat-open-2026-07-03.png)

## Open partner support

Select **Get Support** at the top of the Partner Portal. The support panel currently offers:

- **Contact Support**
- **Support Keyword**
- a link to the Discord community
- email at [support@knotie-ai.pro](mailto:support@knotie-ai.pro)

Use email when you cannot complete a request in the portal. Include the page you were using, what you expected, what happened, and the approximate time. Do not include passwords or private keys.

## Support keyword

Use the **Support Keyword** tab when the support team needs to confirm that you own the account. Follow the code and identity-check instructions shown in the panel.

## If a support request fails

Keep a copy of your message and send it to [support@knotie-ai.pro](mailto:support@knotie-ai.pro). If Katie creates a support ticket, save the reference shown in the chat. A confirmation email is only expected when the chat says one was sent.
