## Overview

The **Campaign Manager** lets you run outbound calling campaigns using your AI voice agents. You upload a list of prospects, choose which agent calls them, assign a number pool, and the platform dials automatically — tracking outcomes, honouring Do Not Call lists, and letting you pause, resume, or cancel at any time.

## Where to find it

Partner Portal → **Campaigns** in the sidebar.

## Key concepts

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Campaign** | A named outbound calling run with an assigned agent, number pool, and prospect list |
| **Prospect** | A contact record (name + phone number) the campaign will call |
| **DNC list** | Do Not Call — numbers that are blocked from being dialled |
| **Number pool** | A group of phone numbers the campaign rotates through for caller-ID variety |
| **Call outcome** | The result of each call attempt (answered, voicemail, no answer, etc.) |

## Create a campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Campaign**.
3. Fill in the campaign details:
   - **Name** — a label for this campaign
   - **AI Agent** — the voice agent that will make the calls (choose from your imported agents)
   - **Number Pool** — the pool of caller-ID numbers to rotate through
   - **Schedule** — calling window (days and hours the campaign is allowed to dial)
4. Click **Create Campaign**.

The campaign is created in a **Draft** state. Add prospects before starting it.

## Upload prospects

Prospects are the contacts your campaign will call. You can upload them as a CSV file.

1. Open the campaign.
2. Click **Upload Prospects**.
3. Download the CSV template if you need the correct column format.
4. Fill in your prospect list and upload the file.
5. The platform validates each row — invalid rows are flagged and skipped.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the prospect upload modal with the template download link and file picker._

### Required CSV columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `phone` | Phone number in E.164 format (e.g. `+14155552671`) |
| `name` | Contact's name (shown in the call log) |

Additional columns you include are passed through to the agent as context variables.

### Export prospects

To download the current prospect list (including call outcomes), open the campaign and click **Export Prospects**. The file downloads as a CSV.

## Start a campaign

Once you have prospects uploaded:

1. Open the campaign.
2. Click **Start Campaign**.
3. Confirm the start.

The campaign moves to **Active** and begins dialling prospects within the configured schedule window.

## Pause and resume

You can pause an active campaign at any time without losing progress.

- Click **Pause** on the campaign — the dialler stops immediately.
- Click **Resume** to continue from where it left off.

Prospects that were already called are not redialled.

## Cancel a campaign

Click **Cancel Campaign** to stop the campaign permanently. Cancelled campaigns cannot be restarted; you would need to create a new campaign.

## View call outcomes

Open the campaign and click the **Calls** tab to see a log of every call attempt:

- Prospect name and number
- Call status (answered, voicemail, no answer, failed, etc.)
- Duration
- Agent used
- Timestamp

## Campaign statistics

The campaign detail page shows a live stats summary:

- **Total prospects** — how many contacts are in the list
- **Called** — how many have been attempted
- **Pending** — remaining to call
- **Answered rate** — percentage of calls that connected

Click **Estimate** to get a projected completion time based on your schedule and the number of remaining prospects.

## Do Not Call (DNC) list

The DNC list is a blocklist of phone numbers that should never be dialled. Any prospect whose number appears on the DNC list is automatically skipped.

### Manage the DNC list

1. Partner Portal → **Campaigns** → **DNC List**.
2. To add a number manually, click **+ Add Number**.
3. To add numbers in bulk, click **Upload DNC** and upload a CSV with one number per row.
4. To remove a number, find it in the list and click **Remove**.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the DNC list page showing the upload button and a few example entries._

### When a number is on the DNC list

If a prospect's number is on the DNC list at the time the campaign tries to dial it, the call is skipped and the prospect's status is set to `dnc`. The number is never called, even if the campaign is restarted.

## Whitelabel / customer campaigns

If you have a whitelabel portal, your customers can access their own campaign endpoints under the whitelabel API. Campaign creation, prospect management, and call-outcome tracking are all available via the whitelabel route.
