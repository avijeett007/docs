---
title: "Email (any mailbox)"
description: "Connect your own domain email so AI agents can read, organize, draft, and send messages."
---

**Email (any mailbox)** connects an email account from almost any provider — for example Hostinger, Namecheap, GoDaddy/Titan, or cPanel — so your AI agents can work with it. You choose exactly what each agent is allowed to do, from just reading email to sending it.

It's marked **Beta** and is one of the free apps you can offer every customer.

<Note>
Using Gmail or Microsoft 365? Connect the **Gmail** or **Outlook** app instead. Email (any mailbox) is for other providers and custom domain email.
</Note>

## Make it available to a customer

As a partner, add **Email (any mailbox)** to the customer's apps:

- For one customer: **Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons → Edit Apps**. See [Choose customer integrations](/partner-portal/customers/integration-apps-selection).
- For all customers: add it to your list under [Apps for your customers](/partner-portal/settings/integration-pricing#apps-for-your-customers).

The customer then connects their mailbox from the **Integration** page in their portal.

## What you need to connect

Your email provider can give you these details:

- **Incoming mail (IMAP)** server and port — often `imap.yourdomain.com`, port 993
- **Outgoing mail (SMTP)** server and port — often `smtp.yourdomain.com`, port 587 or 465
- The mailbox **username** (usually the full email address) and **password**

If your mailbox uses two-step sign-in, create an **app password** with your provider and use that instead of your normal password.

## Connect the mailbox

1. In the customer portal, open **Integration** and find **Email (any mailbox)**.
2. Enter a name for the mailbox, the email address, the server details, and the sign-in details.
3. Test the connection, fix anything it reports, and save.
4. Choose which agents can use the mailbox and what each one is allowed to do.

## Choose what each agent can do

| Access | The agent can |
|---|---|
| **Read** | Read and search email |
| **Organise** | Everything above, plus mark, flag, and move email, and move it to Trash |
| **Draft** | Everything above, plus write drafts |
| **Send approval** | Everything above, plus prepare email that waits for your approval before it's sent |
| **Send** | Everything above, plus send email straight away |

Give each agent the lowest level it needs. You can change it at any time.

Agents can never permanently delete email unless you also turn on **Allow permanent delete**. See [Let agents delete email](/agents/mailbox-delete-tools).

## Approve email before it's sent

With **Send approval**, the agent's email is held until you approve or reject it. This is the safer choice for most agents. Only choose **Send** for an agent you trust to email people without review.

## If the connection fails

- Check the server names and ports with your email provider.
- Try the full email address as the username.
- If two-step sign-in is on, use an app password.
- Check that your provider allows IMAP and SMTP connections — some turn them off by default.

## If an agent can't use the mailbox

- Check the agent is assigned to the same customer as the mailbox.
- Check the agent's access level allows the action — for example, **Read** can't send email.
