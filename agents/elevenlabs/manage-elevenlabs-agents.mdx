## What ElevenLabs agents are in Knotie AI

ElevenLabs is a supported voice AI provider.

In the Partner Portal you can:

- **Import Agents** from your ElevenLabs account
- **Create New Agents** (when available)
- Assign agents to customers
- Configure webhooks for analytics

## Where to find it

1. Open **Manage AI Agents**.
2. Click **Manage ElevenLabs Agents**.

## Import agents

1. Click **Import Agents**.
2. Complete the import flow.
3. After import, the portal may refresh the list automatically.

## Create a new agent

1. Click **Create New Agent**.
2. Configure the agent.
3. Save.

## Assign to a customer

1. Open the agent actions.
2. Select the customer.
3. Set profit multiplier (markup).
4. Save.

If you see “No customers found,” onboard a customer first.

## Troubleshooting

- **Import doesn’t work**
  - Confirm your ElevenLabs API key is valid.
  - Check Settings for provider keys.
- **Upgrade prompt**
  - ElevenLabs may be a premium feature depending on plan.
