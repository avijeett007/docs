## Open the agent setup

1. In the Partner Portal, open **AI Agents → Retell Agents**.
2. Click **Create New Agent**.
3. In **Setup**, choose the customer who will own the agent.

![Create New Agent window showing the four setup sections and customer selector](/images/screenshots/retell-create-production-2026-09-12.png)

## Complete the agent details

Move through the sections at the top of the window:

- **Basic Settings** — enter the agent name and instructions, choose a voice and language, and set the opening message.
- **Advanced Settings** — adjust voice, conversation, call, audio, outbound, and webhook options when needed. The defaults are suitable for many agents.
- **Function Calls** — connect customer tools when this section is available for your account.

<Warning>
The production portal currently marks Function Calls as **Work in Progress**. Avoid relying on it for a live customer until that notice is removed.
</Warning>

Review the selected customer and required fields, then click **Create Agent**.

## After creation

Find the new agent on the Retell Agents page. From there, you can open its details, change the customer assignment, or manage its webhook.
