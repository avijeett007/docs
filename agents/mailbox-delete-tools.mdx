description: "Allow AI agents to move email to Trash, or delete it permanently, with safety limits."
---

Agents connected to an [Email (any mailbox)](/agents/agent-mailbox) account can delete email in two ways:

| Action | What happens | Can it be undone? |
|---|---|---|
| **Move to Trash** | The email goes to the Trash folder | Yes — restore it from Trash |
| **Delete permanently** | The email is deleted for good, including emptying Trash or Junk | **No** |

## Move to Trash

Any agent with **Organise** access or higher can move email to Trash. Nothing else needs to be turned on.

## Allow permanent delete

Permanent delete is off for every agent until you turn it on.

1. In the customer portal, open **Integration** and find **Email (any mailbox)**.
2. Open the access settings for the agent.
3. Make sure its access is **Organise** or higher.
4. Expand **Advanced** and turn on **Allow permanent delete**.
5. Confirm when asked.

The agent is then labelled **Can delete for good**.

<Warning>
Turning **Allow permanent delete** off again only stops future deletes. Email that was already deleted can't be recovered.
</Warning>

## Safety limits

- An agent can permanently delete up to 25 emails in one go, and can empty Trash or Junk in small batches.
- Each mailbox has a daily limit on permanent deletes. If an agent reaches it, nothing more is deleted until the limit resets.
- Moving email to Trash doesn't count toward the limit, because it can be undone.
