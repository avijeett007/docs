## What the AI Receptionist is

The **AI Receptionist** is a production-ready, hosted voice agent built on LiveKit Agents. It answers inbound phone calls, introduces itself by your persona name, fetches business context from a guaranteed-deployed knowledge base, and handles caller requests using configurable tools.

Unlike generic Knova agents, the AI Receptionist is provisioned end-to-end through the **Experiences** flow: onboarding steps guide you through persona naming, voice selection, pricing configuration, and auto-deploy — resulting in a live phone number your callers can reach immediately.

<Info>
The AI Receptionist runs on the **Knova** agent backend (LiveKit Agents runtime). A Knova agent is created and deployed automatically; you do not configure it separately in the Knova Agents page.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

Before enabling the AI Receptionist:

1. **Partner Portal access** — you must be logged into your partner account.
2. **Knotie Credits** on the customer's account (5–10 credits/min depending on the agent tier selected; see [Agent tiers](#agent-tiers)).
3. A **phone number** — either imported from your own Twilio/Telnyx account, or one auto-provisioned during setup.
4. Your **business profile** completed (address, phone, hours) — the AI Receptionist's business facts are built deterministically from this profile at deploy time.

## Enable the AI Receptionist

1. In the Partner Portal sidebar, go to **Experiences**.
2. Find the **AI Receptionist** card and click **Enable**.
3. Complete the onboarding steps (see [Onboarding steps](#onboarding-steps) below).

## Onboarding steps

The AI Receptionist onboarding is a guided multi-step flow. Each step is server-rendered for fast load times.

| Step | What you configure |
|------|--------------------|
| 1 | Business profile — name, address, phone, hours |
| 2 | Persona name (the character name your receptionist introduces itself as) |
| 3 | Voice — pick from a curated shortlist of up to 5 voices per tier (audition one at a time with **Hear** / **Try another** / **Browse all**) |
| 4 | Agent tier — Essentials, Moderate, or Premium (see [Agent tiers](#agent-tiers)) |
| 5 | Knowledge base — website URL for crawl + business profile doc (auto-created) |
| 6 | Customer pricing — set a profit multiplier (1.0–5.0×) if you charge your customers for Voice AI usage |
| 7 | Phone number — auto-provisioned or imported |
| 8 | Summary and deploy |
| 9 | Post-deploy confirmation and live test call |

<Note>
Steps 7–9 are heavier on the client side. Steps 1–6 are fully server-rendered and load in under 200 ms.
</Note>

## Persona-first branding

The AI Receptionist is branded with your **persona name** everywhere customers see it — onboarding flow headings, storefront hero, portal shell, authentication pages, and customer emails.

Your legal business name appears only in terms of service, privacy policy, payment pages, and invoices.

A single helper (`brandName.ts`) controls which name is shown in each context; you do not need to configure this manually.

## Agent tiers

The AI Receptionist runs on LiveKit Inference. Three tiers are available:

| Tier | LLM | STT | TTS | Credits/min |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-------------|
| **Essentials** | Gemma 4 31B | AssemblyAI | Inworld TTS 1.5 Mini | 5 |
| **Moderate** | Gemini 3.5 Flash | Nova-3 | Inworld TTS 2 (expressive mode) | 7 |
| **Premium** | GPT-5.1 | Flux | Cartesia Sonic 3.5 | 10 |

<Info>
**Zero Data Retention** — platform tiers (Essentials, Moderate, Premium) route all LLM, STT, and TTS calls through **LiveKit Inference** exclusively. Your call audio and transcripts never touch a third-party vendor's storage. BYOA agents use the partner's own API keys and are not subject to this policy.
</Info>

All tiers include:
- Per-tier fallback chains (the agent stays live if a provider has an outage)
- Sticky fast TTS failover (1 retry × 4 s, then pinned for the remainder of the call)
- Duration cap, turn cap, and per-caller inbound frequency limit to protect against call abuse

## Voice AI workflow patterns

The AI Receptionist supports four LiveKit workflow modes:

| Mode | When to use |
|------|-------------|
| **Solo agent** | Single-agent inbound handling — the default for most receptionists |
| **Front desk** | Routes caller to the right specialist agent based on intent |
| **Step by step** | Guides a caller through a structured flow (e.g., appointment booking) |
| **Concierge** | Silent background consults while the main agent speaks |

The builder sets the workflow mode automatically based on your business profile and the tools you enable. You can override this in the agent configuration.

## Knowledge base safety net

At deploy time, the platform guarantees that the agent has at least one knowledge base entry before going live:

1. **Business facts** — a document built deterministically from your business profile (address, phone, hours, services) is attached to the agent.
2. **Website crawl** — if you provide a URL (Step 5), the platform crawls and indexes it. Requires `STANDARD_APIFY_TOKEN` on Connect Hub; without it, the business facts document is still attached.

If your AI Receptionist went live before this safety net existed (i.e., before this update), you can repair it without redeploying:

```
POST /api/auto-deploy/refresh-knowledge
{
  "partnerId": "<your partner ID>",
  "customerId": "<customer ID>"
}
```
Include the `x-internal-api-key` header. The response includes `kbSafetyNet: "created"` if a knowledge base was created, or `kbSafetyNet: "exists"` if one was already attached.

## Typing / thinking sounds

The AI Receptionist can play a subtle filler sound while the model is generating a response. This is **off by default**.

To enable:
1. Open the agent's modal (Configure → Agent modal).
2. Under **Advanced**, toggle **Thinking sounds** on.

<Note>
Thinking sounds are not available on the **Premium** tier.
</Note>

## Curated voices

In Step 3 of onboarding, the voice picker shows a curated shortlist of up to **5 voices** in the order your partner has configured. You can:

- Click **Hear** to audition the currently highlighted voice.
- Click **Try another** to cycle to the next voice in the shortlist.
- Click **Browse all** to see the full voice catalogue.

Each voice is specific to your agent's tier — voices that require a higher tier than the one you selected are not shown.

## Partner customer pricing (Voice AI multiplier)

Partners can charge their Voice AI customers a custom rate per minute by setting a **profit multiplier** in Step 6:

- Range: **1.0× – 5.0×**
- The multiplier applies to the base credit rate for the agent's tier.
- Example: Moderate tier (7 credits/min) at 1.5× = **11 credits/min** (rounded up to whole credits).
- The preview in Step 6 shows the approximate credit cost per minute at each tier for your multiplier.

If no multiplier is set (default), customers are billed at your platform's standard rate.

Committed partners (on a special pricing plan) are priced from their committed base rate before the multiplier is applied.

The customer-facing voice pricing endpoint (`GET /api/whitelabel/voice-pricing`) returns the display rate ("≈ N credits/min") for each tier chip in the customer portal. Under legacy (no multiplier) pricing, this endpoint no longer reveals your underlying cost rate to customers.

## Call limits and abuse protection

Each AI Receptionist deployment includes configurable call limits to protect against runaway costs:

| Limit | What it controls |
|-------|-----------------|
| Duration cap | Maximum call length before the agent gracefully ends the call |
| Turn cap | Maximum back-and-forth exchanges per call |
| Per-caller inbound frequency | Rate limit on how often a single caller ID can call |

These limits can be tightened at the agent level. They cannot be loosened beyond the platform defaults.

Withheld caller IDs and rotating callers are automatically rate-limited by the platform.

## Site-wide partner voice agent

Partners can designate a single Knova agent as the **site-wide partner voice agent**. This agent is surfaced publicly on the Voice AI landing page and is accessible via a landing token.

To configure:
1. Go to **Settings → Platform Configuration**.
2. Under **Voice AI**, select the agent to use as the site-wide agent.
3. Save. The landing page token is generated automatically.

## Voice Agent Studio

The **Voice Agent Studio** is a node-graph builder for multi-agent orchestration, available in both partner and customer portals.

Key capabilities:
- **Node graph** — visually wire agents as nodes in an orchestration graph.
- **AI copilot** — describe what you want in plain language; the copilot proposes a graph.
- **Per-persona tool picker** — assign specific tools to each agent in the graph.
- **Import existing agent** — add an already-deployed agent as a node in a team.
- **Stacked side panel** — edit node properties without leaving the graph view.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Agent answers but gives no business info | Knowledge base not attached at deploy | Run `POST /api/auto-deploy/refresh-knowledge` (see above) |
| Caller hears silence after greeting | TTS failover triggered; call recovers within 4 s | Check LiveKit Inference quota/plan; Premium audio may need Cartesia API key |
| Agent uses business name instead of persona name | Pre-update deployment | Redeploy the agent from the Experience settings page |
| Step 6 shows all tiers (no curated shortlist) | Custom-domain branding route not emitting `curatedVoiceKeys` | Update to the latest app build (fixed in post-launch release) |
| Wide-screen layout overflows | Pre-update build | Update to latest app build (fixed in post-launch release) |
