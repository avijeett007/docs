## Explore the homepage

The homepage introduces the products you can sell under your own brand. Use the interactive product display or scroll through the story to see examples of the platform, customer journeys, integrations, and partner tools.

![Knotie AI Pro homepage with the product explorer and partner sign-up actions](/images/screenshots/landing-page-production-2026-09-12.png)

## Compare ways to sell AI

In **How do you want to sell AI?**, choose a path to explore common offers such as voice agents, chat agents, AI gateways, CRM services, or VPS reselling. Each option explains the offer before you estimate revenue.

## Use the revenue playground

Open the [Revenue Playground](https://knotie-ai.pro/#revenue-playground), choose an offer, and move the sliders to match your expected customer count, usage, and selling price. The results update as you adjust the inputs.

Treat the totals as planning estimates. Your actual costs and margin depend on usage, the services you enable, and the price you charge customers.

## Start free

The [Start Free section](https://knotie-ai.pro/#start-free) explains the current entry offer and links to account creation. Review the plan details shown there before signing up because availability and included features can change.
