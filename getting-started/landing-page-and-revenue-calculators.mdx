## Overview

The Knotie AI Pro homepage (`knotie-ai.pro`) received a full hero-section revamp. The new design replaces the previous centered headline layout with an interactive split view:

- **Left panel** — value proposition, headline, and calls to action
- **Right panel** — an animated 3D globe showing every Knotie service as a pinned tile

Clicking any globe tile opens a **Service Drawer** with a live revenue calculator for that service.

Below the hero, the existing decision tree section guides visitors through three paths for selling AI, each with its own calculator.

## Service drawer

Clicking any tile on the globe slides in a **Service Drawer** from the right edge of the screen. The drawer:

1. Shows the service name, billing model, and a short description
2. Exposes sliders for every revenue input relevant to that service
3. Computes and displays live results as you move each slider
4. Closes via the **×** button, the Escape key, or clicking the overlay

The drawer locks page scroll while open.

### Example: Voice AI drawer

| Slider | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Number of customers | 1 – 200 | 25 |
| Avg. minutes / customer / month | 50 – 3 000 | 600 |
| Your resale price | 12 ¢/min – 45 ¢/min | 20 ¢/min |

**Displayed results**

| Metric | Example at defaults |
|---|---|
| Total minutes / month | 15 000 min |
| Monthly revenue | $3 000 |
| Wholesale (10 ¢/min) | $1 500 |
| **Annual profit** | **$18 000** |

## "How do you want to sell AI?" — decision tree

Below the globe hero, the **decision tree** section guides visitors through three top-level paths:

### Path 01 — AI as Agency Services

You build, configure, and deliver AI for clients on a project-based or retainer model. Four sub-options:

| Sub-path | Revenue model |
|---|---|
| Voice receptionist setup | Setup fee + monthly managed retainer |
| Chat & support deployment | Setup fee + monthly management |
| Custom AI workflows | Per-project fee + hosting retainer |
| CRM setup & ops | Setup fee + monthly retainer (CRM is $0 in your plan) |

### Path 02 — AI as SaaS

You resell AI products under your own brand on a recurring subscription. Five sub-options:

| Product | Billing | Wholesale cost |
|---|---|---|
| Voice AI | Per-minute | 10 ¢/min |
| Chat AI | Per-message | $3 / 1 000 messages |
| API Gateway | Per-token | Pass-through with margin |
| CRM-as-a-Service | Subscription | $0 (included in partner plan) |
| VPS Reselling | Per-server | ~$6/server |

### Path 03 — One-Click Experiences

Ready-made AI products you can enable, white-label, and sell immediately. Each comes with its own landing page, onboarding flow, and agent deployment. Available experiences include AI Receptionist, AI Support, SDR agent, Hermes agent, and Business Audit.

## Revenue calculators

Every leaf node in the decision tree opens an inline revenue calculator. All calculators follow the same pattern: adjustable sliders → live computed results table.

**Common outputs across all calculators**

- Monthly revenue (or project revenue)
- Your wholesale cost (what Knotie charges you)
- Annual profit estimate

**VPS Reselling example**

| Slider | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Number of servers | 1 – 200 | 20 |
| Your resale price | $7 – $30 / server | $12 |

| Metric | Example at defaults |
|---|---|
| Monthly revenue | $240 |
| Wholesale ($6/server) | $120 |
| **Annual profit** | **$1 440** |

Calculators are illustrative estimates. Actual margins depend on your plan, usage, and retail pricing.

---

## Navigation change

As part of this revamp, the top navigation bar changed from `position: sticky` to `position: fixed`. This means the nav bar stays visible at the top of the viewport while scrolling, rather than leaving the top of the document.
