## What is progressive onboarding?

When you sign up for Knotie AI Pro, the platform guides you through a short first-login flow that personalises your partner portal experience:

1. **Welcome video** — a brief orientation to what the platform can do
2. **Track picker** — select the capabilities you plan to use most
3. **Configured sidebar** — your portal navigation is immediately focused on what you chose

This flow is designed to surface the right features for your agency instead of overwhelming you with everything at once.

## Step 1 — Welcome video

The first thing you see after logging in is a **Welcome Video Modal**. Watch the short orientation video to get an overview of the platform, then click **Continue** to move to the track picker.

<Tip>
You can skip the video if you've seen it before or want to go straight to setup. The "Skip" link is in the bottom-left of the modal.
</Tip>

---

## Step 2 — Track picker

The track picker shows **preference tracks** — abstract categories that represent how you plan to use Knotie AI Pro. Select every track that fits your agency.

Examples of tracks you may see:

| Track | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Voice AI** | Inbound / outbound calling, voice agents |
| **Chat & Messaging** | Webchat, SMS, CRM bots |
| **VPS & Apps** | Self-hosted apps, Hermes/OpenClaw agents, server management |
| **Outreach & CRM** | GHL integration, campaign automation, deal management |
| **Agency Growth** | White-label, customer billing, revenue calculators |

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the track picker screen showing the multi-select track chips, at least 2 selected (highlighted), and the "Set up my portal" confirm button._

You can select multiple tracks. The more specific your selection, the tighter the initial sidebar will be — you can always add or unlock more features later.

### How tracks shape your sidebar

After you confirm your selection, the sidebar is populated with the features that match your chosen tracks and are approved for new partners. Features outside your tracks are hidden by default — they do not appear until you unlock them through challenges or request them from your account manager.

<Note>
Selecting a track does not restrict you permanently. You can unlock any feature from the level system challenges or by asking Knotie support. The picker is a starting point, not a ceiling.
</Note>

## Existing partners — redesign introduction

If you joined Knotie AI Pro before the progressive onboarding system launched, you will see a brief **Redesign Introduction** screen the first time you log in after the update. This one-time screen explains what changed in the portal layout.

Existing partners always have access to the **full set of active features** — the curated new-partner picker does not apply. Your sidebar shows everything you were using before, plus any features that have been unlocked since you joined.

## Returning to the picker

The track picker is a first-login experience. After you complete it, your preferences are saved and the picker does not show again automatically.

To change your track preferences after onboarding, contact Knotie support or use the level system challenges to unlock additional features one by one.

---

## How tracks connect to the level system

Your selected tracks influence which **challenges** appear in your challenge queue. If you picked the **VPS & Apps** track, for example, your early challenges will guide you through deploying your first VPS app. If you picked **Voice AI**, your challenges will walk through creating a voice agent and assigning a phone number.

This means the level system adapts to the type of agency you're building — not just a generic checklist.

See [Partner Level System](/partner-portal/level-system) for the full breakdown of levels, challenges, and rewards.
