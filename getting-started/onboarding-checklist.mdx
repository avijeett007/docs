## Overview

When you log into the Partner Portal for the first time, you’ll see a **Getting Started** onboarding checklist.

It’s designed to help you get from “new account” to “customer-ready” in a single session.

## What you’ll get when you finish

When you complete all steps, Knotie AI unlocks a **completion reward** (shown inside the checklist).

## The 6 onboarding steps (in order)

1. **Customize your Pod**
   Go to **Settings → Whitelabel** and set up your portal branding.

2. **Onboard your first customer**
   Go to **Customers** and use **Quick Onboard**.

3. **Import an agent**
   Go to **AI Agents** and create or import an agent (Retell or VAPI).

4. **Review analytics**
   Go to **AI Usage** to confirm usage and make sure data is flowing.

5. **Complete whitelabeling (email)**
   Go to **Settings → Email Domain** and configure your email domain.

6. **Set up payments**
   Go to **Settings** and connect **Stripe** (recommended when you’re ready to charge customers).

## Tips

- If you get stuck, click the floating **tutorial video** button (bottom-right). It opens a short walkthrough for the page you’re on.
- You can complete onboarding gradually—your progress is saved.
