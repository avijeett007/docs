## Overview

Knotie AI is built for partners and agencies.

Plans are designed around how many customers you manage, how many agents you run, and which automation + whitelabel features you need.

## Plan options

### Free Forever

Best for trying Knotie AI Pro and validating your workflow.

Highlights:

- Up to 2 customers
- Unlimited Knova agents
- Up to 2 Retell agents (free tier)
- Subdomain whitelabel portal
- Passkey + MFA
- **100 MB** customer media storage

### Solo Agency Owner

Best for a small agency that wants to run multiple customers with full provider access.

Highlights:

- Up to 20 customers
- All AI providers supported (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, GHL)
- Custom domain whitelabel
- Team management (up to 2 members)
- Monthly Knotie credits
- **1 GB** customer media storage pool

### Ultimate Scaleup Agency

Best for agencies scaling to a large customer base.

Highlights:

- Unlimited customers
- Higher concurrency limits
- Priority support + business manager
- SaaS mode (turn your agency into a SaaS)
- **5 GB** customer media storage pool

## Monthly vs yearly billing

On the pricing page you can toggle **Monthly** or **Yearly**.

- Yearly billing shows an estimated monthly equivalent.
- If a yearly discount is available, it will be shown next to the toggle.

## “Detailed pricing” view

If you click **See complete pricing breakdown**, Knotie AI Pro opens the detailed view with the full feature list.

## Feature highlights

- Customer onboarding and portal access controls
- Whitelabel portal setup (subdomain or custom domain)
- AI agents (Retell and VAPI, plus other providers on paid plans)
- Integrations and automation
- Team members and customer portal permissions (paid plans)
- Customer media library (Docs & Media) with plan-based storage quotas
- Social posting calendar (Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok)

## Storage quotas

Storage for the customer media library is allocated per partner plan and divided across customers.

| Plan | Total partner pool |
|---|---|
| Free Forever | 100 MB |
| Solo Agency / Pro | 1 GB |
| Ultimate Scaleup Agency | 5 GB |

You control how much of that pool each customer gets in **Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons → Allocated Storage**. A customer hitting their individual cap gets an upload error; the overall pool cap blocks uploads from all customers until storage is freed.

See [Docs & Media library](/customer-management/media-library) for the full customer-facing guide.
