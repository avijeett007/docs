## Pricing page layout

The pricing page at **knotie-ai.pro/pricing** has three sections:

1. **Plan cards** — headline features for each tier, with a subscribe or sign-up button.
2. **Detailed comparison table** — full feature-by-feature breakdown across all tiers (expand via _See complete pricing breakdown_).
3. **FAQ** — answers to common questions about billing, switching plans, and enterprise options.

### Monthly vs yearly billing

Use the **Monthly / Yearly** toggle at the top of the plan cards section to switch between billing intervals.

- Yearly billing shows an estimated per-month cost.
- The full yearly charge is billed at signup.
- You can switch between monthly and yearly at any time from your subscription settings.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I switch plans anytime?**
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your subscription settings. Changes take effect immediately and are prorated.

**What payment methods are accepted?**
All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) via Stripe.

**Is there a setup fee?**
No. You only pay your subscription. Migration assistance on paid plans is free.

**What's included in Free Forever?**
Up to 2 customers, unlimited Knova agents, subdomain white-label portal, passkey + MFA authentication, and community support.

**Do you offer custom enterprise plans?**
Yes. For agencies with unique requirements, custom plans are available with dedicated support, custom integrations, and volume discounts. Contact sales from the pricing page.

**What happens if I exceed my customer limit?**
You receive a notification as you approach the limit and can upgrade in one click. There is no service interruption — live customer sessions are not cut off.
