## Overview

The Knotie AI Pro homepage (`knotie-ai.pro`) leads visitors to a **Start Free** section that replaces the traditional pricing-tier wall with a **free-first strategy**: get partners onto Free Forever first, then help them grow and monetise customers from day one.

This page documents that public-facing section — what prospects see before they create an account, and the tools available to you as a partner for sharing and converting your own prospects.

## Free Forever card

At the centre of the Start Free section is the **Free Forever card** — a clear statement of what's included at no charge, so prospects know exactly what they're committing to:

| What's included | Notes |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 customers | Expand with Stage add-ons |
| Unlimited Knova agents | Knotie's native voice AI agent |
| Up to 2 Retell agents | Free-tier Retell integration |
| Up to 2 N8N chat agents | Automation / chat pipeline |
| Passkey + MFA security | Passwordless login built in |
| Subdomain whitelabel portal | Your brand, your subdomain |

### Card verification — compliance note

Sign-up requires a payment card for **identity and anti-bot verification only**.

<Warning>
  Your card is **never charged** during sign-up or the Free Forever plan. No trial ends in a surprise charge — there is no auto-upgrade.
</Warning>

This guarantee is enforced in the product code; the card-copy rule is locked by an automated test. If you ever see a charge you didn't authorise, contact [support@knotie-ai.pro](mailto:support@knotie-ai.pro) immediately.

## Partner Journey — Level System rail

Below the Free Forever card, the **Partner Journey** rail shows a gamified growth path (L1–L6) so prospects can see what their agency journey looks like before they sign up.

| Level | Name | What it represents |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | First Steps | Account created, portal explored |
| L2 | Builder | First agent created and assigned to a customer |
| L3 | Launcher | First customer live with a working AI agent |
| L4 | Monetiser | First paid customer billed through the platform |
| L5 | Scaler | Multiple customers active; white-label branding live |
| L6 | Partner Pro | Full agency stack — reselling AI, credits, and apps |

Each level is linked to in-product challenges that unlock features, credits, and rewards as you complete them. The rail on the landing page is illustrative; once you log in the challenges adapt to your chosen tracks.

<Tip>
  Share the landing page with prospects to show them the growth path — it makes the value proposition concrete before they commit to a sign-up.
</Tip>

See [Partner Level System](/partner-portal/level-system) for the full in-product breakdown of XP, challenges, and rewards.

## Pay-as-you-grow model

The Free-first strategy is built around customer-expansion billing rather than upfront plan selection:

1. **Sign up free** — no plan wall, no trial expiry pressure.
2. **Onboard your first 2 customers** — fully functional on Free Forever.
3. **Add a Stage** — subscribe to an optional monthly add-on when you need more customer slots or advanced features. Cancel any time.
4. **Grow into credits** — as your customers use AI agents, you buy credit bundles. Revenue follows your customer success, not your plan level.

See [Stage Subscriptions](/partner-portal/settings/stage-subscriptions) and [Credits](/partner-portal/credits) for billing details.

## Rollback (for self-hosters)

If you self-host Knotie AI Pro and need to fall back to the v1 landing page while the v2 rolls out:

```bash
# In your build environment — set before running `npm run build`
NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_LANDING_V2=false
```

Then redeploy. The v1 split-globe hero is restored. No database change required.

## How to sign up from the landing page

1. Visit **knotie-ai.pro** and scroll to the **Start Free** section (or click **Start for free** in the header).
2. Review the Free Forever card to confirm what's included.
3. Click **Start for Free** and fill in your business name, email, and phone number.
4. Add a card for verification — you will **not** be charged.
5. Click **Create Account** to complete sign-up.
6. Follow the [Onboarding Checklist](/getting-started/onboarding-checklist) to get your first customer live.
