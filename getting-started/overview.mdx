## Who this is for

These docs are for partners and agencies managing one or more customers on Knotie AI Pro.

## What you'll learn

- How to create and secure your account
- How to onboard customers and set up their portal access
- How to configure whitelabel and custom domains
- How to connect and manage AI agents
- How to use the partner APIs

## Your two portals

- **Your partner portal** — where you manage everything: customers, agents, settings, and billing.
- **Your customer's portal** — what your customers see when they log in under your brand.
