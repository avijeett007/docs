## Who this documentation is for

These docs are written for partners and agencies who manage one or more customers on Knotie AI.

## What you’ll learn

- How to create and secure your account
- How to onboard customers and configure their portal access
- How to set up whitelabel and custom domains
- How to work with agents (Retell and VAPI)
- How to use partner-facing APIs (MCP)

## Portal types

- Customer portal: used by your customers.
- Whitelabel portal: used by partners/agencies to support customers and manage portal settings.
