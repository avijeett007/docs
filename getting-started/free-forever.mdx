## Overview

Knotie AI Pro offers a **Free Forever tier** so you can explore the platform risk-free. Every new account starts on Free Forever and automatically receives a **7-day premium trial** that unlocks all features — no auto-upgrade, no charge after the trial ends.

## 7-day premium trial

When you sign up, you're offered a 7-day trial of all premium features:

| What's included | Notes |
|----------------|-------|
| All AI voice providers (VAPI, Retell, LiveKit, Ultravox) | Access every provider during the trial |
| Unlimited customers and agents | No tier limits during trial |
| Advanced integrations and analytics | GHL, N8N, and all integrations |
| Priority support | |

**How the trial works:**
- The first signup screen is a Business Name / Email / Phone modal with no card entry. The Stripe card step (if required for verification) happens on a subsequent screen — not captured in the live `Create Account` modal.
- After 7 days, your account reverts to the Free Forever plan automatically.
- There is no auto-upgrade and no charge unless you choose to upgrade.

## Free Forever (after trial)

After the trial ends, you remain on the Free Forever plan with these limits:

**Customer & Agent Management**

- Up to 2 customers
- Unlimited Knova agents
- Up to 2 Retell agents (free tier)
- Up to 2 N8N chat agents

**Security & Branding**

- Passkey + MFA authentication
- Subdomain whitelabel portal

**Performance**

- Concurrency limit: 5 (Knova)

<Note>
  Some add-ons, such as phone numbers, may incur separate charges even on the Free Forever plan.
</Note>

## Expanding your customer limit with Stages

If you need more than 2 customers but aren't ready to move to a full paid plan, you can subscribe to a **Stage** — an optional monthly add-on that increases your customer slot count and can unlock additional agent types or One-Click Experiences.

Stages are billed monthly through Stripe. You can subscribe, upgrade, or cancel at any time from **Settings → Stage Subscriptions**.

See [Stage subscriptions](/partner-portal/settings/stage-subscriptions) for full details.

## When to upgrade

Consider upgrading to a paid plan when you need:

- More than 2 customers
- More than 2 Retell agents
- Premium provider integrations (VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, GHL)
- Custom domain whitelabel branding
- Team member access
- Higher concurrency limits and advanced scaling features

## How to get started

1. Visit **knotie-ai.pro** and click **Start for free**.
2. Fill in your business name, email, and phone number.
3. Click **Create Account** — no plan selection required.
4. On the next screen, click **Start 7-Day Free Trial** and complete the Stripe card step.
5. Log in to your Partner Portal and follow the onboarding checklist.

See [How to sign up](/account/signup) for the detailed signup walkthrough.
