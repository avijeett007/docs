## Overview

Knotie AI offers a **Free Forever tier** so you can explore the platform and validate your workflow risk-free before committing to a paid plan.

It's designed for partners who want to test:

- Customer onboarding
- Whitelabel portal setup
- Basic agent management

## What’s included

The Free Forever tier includes essential features with clearly defined limits:

**Customer & Agent Management**

- Up to 2 customers
- Unlimited Knova agents
- Up to 2 Retell agents (free tier)
- Up to 2 N8N chat agents

**Security & Branding**

- Passkey + MFA authentication
- Subdomain whitelabel portal

**Performance**

- Concurrency limit: 5 (Knova)

**Note:** Some add-ons, such as phone numbers, may incur separate charges.

## When to upgrade

Consider upgrading to a paid plan when you need:

- More than 2 customers
- More than 2 Retell agents
- Premium provider integrations (VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, GHL)
- Custom domain whitelabel branding
- Team member access
- Higher concurrency limits and advanced scaling features
