## Overview

Knotie AI offers a Free Forever tier so you can start using the platform and validate your workflow before upgrading.

It’s built for partners who want to test:

- Customer onboarding
- Whitelabel portal setup
- Basic agent management

## What’s included

Free Forever includes essential features, with clear limits:

- Up to **2 customers**
- **Unlimited Knova agents**
- Up to **2 Retell agents** (free tier)
- Up to **2 N8N chat agents**
- Passkey + MFA authentication
- **Subdomain** whitelabel portal
- Concurrency limit: **5** (Knova)

Some add-ons (like phone numbers) may have separate charges.

## When to upgrade

Upgrade when you need any of the following:

- More than 2 customers
- More than 2 Retell agents
- Premium providers (VAPI / Ultravox / ElevenLabs / GHL)
- Custom domain whitelabel
- Team members
- Higher concurrency and scale features

## How to start on Free Forever

1. Open the Knotie AI pricing section.
2. Select **Free Forever**.
3. Complete the signup form.
4. Log in to the Partner Portal and follow the onboarding checklist.
