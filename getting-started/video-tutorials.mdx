## Overview

Knotie AI includes a **floating video button** inside the Partner Portal.

You’ll usually see it in the **bottom-right corner** of the screen.

## What it does

- Opens a short tutorial video for the page you’re currently viewing
- Helps you complete onboarding steps faster
- Keeps you moving without needing to search for help articles

## What to expect

When you click the button:

- A video player opens
- You can mute/unmute
- You can close it and continue working immediately

## If you don’t see the video button

- Some pages may not have a video assigned yet.
- If you’re not logged in, the Partner Portal help overlay won’t load.
