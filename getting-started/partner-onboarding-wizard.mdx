## Overview

When you log in as a new partner for the first time, Knotie AI Pro opens the **Onboarding Wizard** — a five-step interactive popup that replaces the previous welcome video modal. The wizard takes about 60 seconds and personalizes your dashboard and recommended agent templates.

## Starting the wizard

On the welcome screen you have two options:

- **Tune it** — launches the five-step profiling wizard.
- **Watch video** — plays the embedded product tour. You can still go through the wizard afterward.

## The five steps

### Step 1 — Sectors

**WHO YOU SERVE** — Select **at least 2** industries from 23 options that best describe your customers' businesses.

| Available sectors | |
|---|---|
| Real estate | Healthcare |
| Home services | Legal |
| Auto dealerships | Restaurants |
| Fitness & wellness | E-commerce |
| B2B SaaS | Finance |
| Insurance | Education |
| Travel & hospitality | Logistics |
| Marketing agencies | Recruiting |
| Nonprofits | Government |
| Manufacturing | Beauty & salons |
| Dental practices | Solar & energy |
| Med spas | |

### Step 2 — Products

**WHAT YOU'LL SELL** — Select **at least 1** AI product type you plan to offer your customers.

| Available products | |
|---|---|
| AI voice agents | Chat widgets |
| SMS receptionist | Email autoresponders |
| Inbound qualification | Outbound dialers |
| Meeting summarizer | Knowledge bots |
| Compliance audit | Hermes content |
| API gateway | White-label resell |
| Custom training | Pricing & quotes |
| Form intake | Recommendation engine |

### Step 3 — Comfort level

Choose the option that best describes your AI experience level. This adjusts the guidance style shown throughout the platform:

| Option | Best for |
|--------|---------|
| **Explorer** (New) | First time selling AI — shows templates first |
| **Operator** (Mid) | Shipped a few projects — surfaces configuration dials |
| **Builder** (Tech) | Writes code — exposes API endpoints and logs |
| **Agency lead** (Team) | Multi-client agency — focuses on margins and team seats |

### Step 4 — Goal (TARGET)

Set your monthly revenue target with the slider ($500 – $50,000, default $5,000). Knotie uses this to display a live deals counter on your dashboard calibrated to your goal.

### Step 5 — Ready

A summary of your selections is displayed, along with a personalized "first challenge" suggestion based on your comfort level and chosen products. Click **Finish** to save your profile and enter the portal.

## Closing the wizard without finishing

If you press **✕** or **Escape**, the wizard is dismissed without saving your selections. The wizard is marked as seen and will **not** appear again on future logins. Your dashboard will use default settings.

If you complete all five steps, your selections are saved to your partner profile and the wizard won't reappear.

## What happens after completion

Your answers are persisted to your partner account and used to:

- Display a live deal counter on the dashboard calibrated to your revenue goal.
- Show a personalized suggested first challenge on the "Ready" step.
- Store selections in your browser (`localStorage`) for fast access on next load.

## API reference

The wizard calls this endpoint on completion. Third-party integrations or admin scripts can also call it directly.

```http
POST /api/partner/onboarding/save
Authorization: Bearer <partner_jwt>
Content-Type: application/json
```

**Request body:**
```json
{
  "sectors": ["realestate", "healthcare"],
  "products": ["voice", "chat"],
  "comfort": "operator",
  "agencyName": "Acme AI Agency",
  "goal": 10000
}
```

**`comfort` accepted values:** `explorer`, `operator`, `builder`, `agency`

**`goal` range:** 500–50,000 (integer, increments of 500)

**Response (200):**
```json
{ "success": true }
```

**Error responses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 400 | Invalid onboarding profile (failed schema validation) |
| 401 | Missing or invalid `Authorization: Bearer` header |
| 413 | Request body exceeds 4 KB |
| 429 | Rate limit exceeded |

<Note>
  This endpoint requires an explicit `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header.
  Cookie-based auth is intentionally not accepted for this mutation to prevent CSRF attacks.
</Note>
