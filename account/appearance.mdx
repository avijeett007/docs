## Overview

The Knotie AI Pro Partner Portal supports **light mode** and **dark mode**. You can switch themes at any time using the theme toggle in the navigation bar.

**As of July 2026, the default theme is light mode.** If you had previously selected dark mode, you may notice the portal switched to light on your next login — this was a one-time reset to align all accounts with the new light-first default. You can switch back to dark mode immediately using the toggle described below.

## How to switch themes

1. Look for the **sun/moon icon** (☀️ / 🌙) in the top navigation bar.
2. Click it to toggle between light and dark mode.
3. Your preference is saved in your browser and will be remembered on your next visit.

## What changes between themes

| Element | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| Background | White / light grey | Dark grey / near-black |
| Text | Dark (high contrast) | Light (high contrast) |
| Modals and panels | White | Dark |
| Sidebar | Light | Dark |

Both themes meet accessibility contrast standards. If you notice any readability issues in either mode, contact support.

## Frequently asked questions

**My portal switched to light mode on its own — is something wrong?**

No. In July 2026 the platform migrated to a light-first default. All accounts received a one-time reset to light mode regardless of their previous preference. Your new preference is saved from this point forward — switch back to dark if you prefer it.

**My preference isn't being remembered.**

Theme preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. If you clear browser data or use private/incognito browsing, the portal will revert to the default (light). Re-apply your preferred theme after clearing storage.

**Will my customers see the theme I set?**

No. Each user controls their own theme. Your theme preference does not affect what your customers see in their portal.
