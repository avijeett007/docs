## Overview

Card verification helps protect Knotie AI from automated signups and abuse.

Knotie AI is a bootstrapped SaaS (we’re not funded by big investors). We keep pricing competitive by keeping costs tight.

Unfortunately, bots and fake signups can create real costs (email sending, phone provisioning, infrastructure usage). Card verification is one of the ways we reduce that risk while still offering Free Forever access.

## What happens during verification

- You’ll be asked to add a payment method.
- A small verification charge may appear temporarily.
- The charge is typically **reversed automatically** (timing depends on your bank).

## Why we do this

- Reduce automated abuse and spam accounts
- Protect platform reliability for real users
- Keep the Free Forever plan sustainable
