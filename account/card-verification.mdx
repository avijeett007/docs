## Overview

Card verification helps protect Knotie AI from automated signups and abuse. It’s used as a bot-protection step.

## What happens during verification

- You’ll be asked to add a payment method.
- A small verification charge may appear temporarily, depending on your payment provider.

## Troubleshooting
