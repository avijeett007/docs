## Overview

Knotie AI Pro publishes two sets of legal documents:

1. **Platform Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** — govern the relationship between SONTI LTD and partners (you, the agency or reseller) who access knotie-ai.pro.
2. **White-label portal Terms of Service & Privacy Policy** — displayed to your customers inside the white-label portal, written entirely in your brand's voice (no Knotie or SONTI references).

Both were updated to **v2.0, effective 18 August 2026**.

## Where to find the documents

| Document | Location |
|----------|----------|
| Platform Terms of Service | Footer of knotie-ai.pro → **Terms** |
| Platform Privacy Policy | Footer of knotie-ai.pro → **Privacy** |
| White-label portal Terms | Footer of your white-label portal → **Terms** |
| White-label portal Privacy | Footer of your white-label portal → **Privacy** |

## What's new in v2.0

### Platform Terms

- **Provider-neutral language** — Knotie routes generation tasks across multiple third-party AI model and infrastructure providers based on price, quality, and availability. The platform does not name specific providers in the Terms; a subprocessor list is available on request.
- **Content you provide** — You grant Knotie a licence to process your inputs to deliver the service. You warrant that you have rights to submit any content, including no one else's image, likeness, or voice without their consent, and no minors' likenesses. API and MCP submission is itself the warranty.
- **What you create** — You own your outputs and have a full commercial licence to use them. Knotie does not train on your content (except customer-requested character/persona training, which remains private to your account).
- **Permitted use and no onward resale** — Partners resell only through official Knotie portals and tools. Wrapping, proxying, or building competing platforms is not permitted. Partners must flow these restrictions down to their own customers.
- **Liability cap** — 12 months of fees paid (or £100), with UK consumer statutory rights preserved.
- **Prepaid credits** — Non-refundable except for failed or blocked generation work.

### White-label portal Terms

- The portal Terms and Privacy Policy are written entirely in the partner's brand voice — your business name, support email, and address appear throughout.
- No Knotie AI or SONTI references appear anywhere in the white-label portal legal pages.
- Branding is interpolated automatically from your [Whitelabel settings](/partner-portal/settings/whitelabel-settings).

## White-label legal page branding

The white-label portal Terms and Privacy Policy automatically pick up:

- **Business name** — from your Whitelabel Settings → Business Name
- **Support email** — from your Whitelabel Settings → Support Email
- **Address** — from your Whitelabel Settings → Business Address

No manual editing is required. If you update your business name in Settings, the legal pages update automatically.

## Note on legal review

The documents were drafted defensively for a pre-launch context. They have not yet been reviewed by a solicitor. Partners with specific compliance requirements (GDPR, CCPA, regulated industries) should seek independent legal advice before relying on these documents for their own customers.
