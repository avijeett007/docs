## Overview

Resetting your partner password is a two-step process:

1. **Request** — you submit your email address. Nothing changes on your account yet.
2. **Confirm** — you click the link in the email, enter a new password, and the change
   takes effect.

Your old password continues to work until you complete step 2. If you request a reset and
then decide not to change your password, simply ignore the email.

## Step 1 — Request a reset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the login page">
    Go to `https://knotie-ai.pro/partner/login` (or your white-label domain's partner
    login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    Below the sign-in button, click **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address registered to your partner account and click **Send reset
    link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    You will receive an email with a reset link. The link is valid for **15 minutes** and
    can be used **once**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The same response is shown regardless of whether the email address belongs to an active,
  suspended, or unknown account. This is intentional — it prevents email enumeration.
</Note>

---

## Step 2 — Set a new password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the reset link">
    Click the link in the email. It opens the **Set new password** page. The token in the
    link is in the URL fragment (`#`) only, so it never appears in server logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new password">
    Choose a password that is at least 8 characters. If it is too short, the link is
    **not** spent — you can correct it and try again on the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Set password**. Your password is updated and you are redirected to the login
    page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your new password. If MFA is enabled on your account, you will be asked
    for your code as normal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The reset link does **not** sign you in automatically. You must sign in after setting
  the new password. This ensures MFA and other security checks still apply.
</Warning>

## Link behaviour

| Scenario | What happens |
|---|---|
| Link is used once | Marked as used; a second click shows "no longer valid" |
| Link expires (>15 min) | "No longer valid" — request a new one |
| Wrong password entered | Link is preserved; correct the password and resubmit |
| Account suspended | Link is refused; contact support |
| Email address changed since link was sent | Link is refused; request a new one |

---

## Rate limits

To prevent abuse, the reset request is limited to:
- **5 requests per email address** per 15 minutes.
- **20 requests per IP address** per 15 minutes.

If you hit a limit, the response still shows the standard message (no 429 is revealed for
the per-email limit). Wait 15 minutes and try again.

---

## If password login is disabled

Some partner accounts or platforms have password login turned off in favour of passkeys or
magic sign-in links. If this is the case, the reset endpoint returns a "feature not
available" message. See [Passwordless sign-in](/account/passwordless-login) for how to
sign in without a password.

---

## Need help?

If you cannot access the reset email (e.g. the address is out of date), contact support
at **support@knotie-ai.pro** or use the **Get Support** link in the portal sidebar.
