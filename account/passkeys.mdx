## Overview

Passkeys let you sign in securely without a password, using your device’s authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or a security key).

## When to use passkeys

- You want the simplest sign-in experience.
- You want strong protection against phishing.

## How to set up

1. Open your account security settings.
2. Choose **Add passkey**.
3. Follow your device prompt to save the passkey.
