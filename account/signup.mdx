## Overview

This guide walks you through creating your Knotie AI partner account.

You sign up from the **Knotie AI Pro website** (knotie-ai.pro). Click any "Get started" button and a signup form opens right on the page.

<Note>
  The standalone Pricing page has been removed. You no longer choose a plan before creating your account. Instead, every new signup starts with a **Free Forever** account and is offered a **7-day free trial** of premium features immediately after registration.
</Note>

## Steps

1. Open **knotie-ai.pro** and click **Start for free** (or any "Get started" button).
2. A **Create Account** modal opens. Fill in:
   - **Business name**
   - **Email address**
   - **Phone number** (used for WhatsApp updates on exclusive offers)
3. (Optional) Enter a **coupon code** if you have one. The coupon is validated instantly — a green check and description appear when the code is accepted.
4. Click **Create Account**.
5. After the account is created, you'll see the **7-Day Free Trial** confirmation showing a "Free Forever — 7-day premium trial" summary and **$0 today**. It lists every premium feature you unlock for the trial.
6. Click **Start 7-Day Free Trial**. You'll be taken to a secure payment page to add a card for verification — **no charge is made today**.
7. After adding your card, use **[Partner Login](/partner/login)** to access your dashboard.

## What the 7-day trial includes

During the trial you have full access to all premium features:

- All AI voice providers (VAPI, Retell, LiveKit, Ultravox)
- Unlimited customers and agents
- Advanced integrations and analytics
- Priority support

After 7 days your account automatically reverts to the **Free Forever** plan. There is no auto-upgrade and no charge unless you choose to upgrade.

## Google signup (if enabled)

If your platform has Google auth enabled, you'll see a **Continue with Google** button at the top of the signup modal.

After signing in with Google, the form shows your Google name and photo already filled in. You still need to provide:
- **Business name**
- **Phone number**

The same **7-day trial** confirmation follows for Google signups.

## What happens next

After your first login, Knotie AI may prompt you to set up a **passkey** ("Secure Your Account") before you reach the dashboard. You may also be asked to enable extra sign-in security (**MFA**) under Settings → Profile Settings.

## A note on the email field

You don't need to worry about extra spaces in your email address. If your phone keyboard or browser autofill adds a trailing space, the form removes it for you automatically.

## Troubleshooting

- **Signup modal doesn't appear** — try disabling browser extensions that block pop-ups or modals.
- **No confirmation email** — check your spam folder and verify the email address you entered.
- **Coupon code not accepted** — the code is entered in uppercase automatically. If the error persists, the code may be expired or already used.
- **Card declined** — use a valid card for the $0 verification. Prepaid cards may be rejected.
- **For issues with Google signup** — ensure your browser is signed into a Google account and that pop-ups are allowed.
