## Overview

This guide walks you through creating your Knotie AI partner account.

Signup is a modal on the main website — there is no standalone `/partner/signup` page. You start from the **Knotie AI Pro website** (knotie-ai.pro), not from the partner portal.

## Steps

1. Open **knotie-ai.pro** and click **Start for free** (or any "Get started" button).
2. A signup modal opens. Fill in:
   - **Business name**
   - **Email address**
   - **Phone number**
3. (Optional) Enter a **coupon code** if you have one. If it unlocks a special offer, you'll see a confirmation in the form.
4. Click **Create Account**.
5. If you selected a paid plan, you'll be redirected to payment to finish setup.
6. After signup, use **[Partner Login](/partner/login)** to access your dashboard.

## Google signup (if enabled)

If your account has Google auth enabled, you'll see a **Continue with Google** button inside the signup modal.

If you choose Google signup, you may still be asked for:
- Business name
- Phone number

## What happens next

After your first login, Knotie AI may prompt you to set up a **passkey** ("Secure Your Account" modal) before you reach the dashboard. You may also be asked to enable **MFA** under Settings → Security.

## Email field behaviour

The **Email address** field automatically strips any whitespace (spaces, tabs, or non-breaking spaces) as you type. This handles trailing spaces that mobile keyboards and browser autofill often append after completing an email address. You do not need to trim the field yourself — the form handles it silently.

## Troubleshooting

- If you don't receive a confirmation email, check your spam folder and verify the email address is correct. The form automatically removes any trailing or leading spaces from your email address, so the address stored in the system will not have extra spaces.
- If the signup modal doesn't appear, try disabling browser extensions that block pop-ups.
- For issues with Google signup, ensure your browser is signed into a Google account.
