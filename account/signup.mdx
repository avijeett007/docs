## Overview

This guide walks you through creating your Knotie AI partner account.

You can start signup from the pricing section (recommended), or from any **Get started / Create account** button.

## Steps

1. Open the Knotie AI website and click **Get started**.
2. Pick a plan (optional but recommended). If you choose a plan, the signup form may say **Continue with [plan name]**.
3. In the signup form, enter:
   - Business name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
4. (Optional) Enter a **coupon code**. If the coupon unlocks a special offer, you’ll see a confirmation in the form.
5. Click **Create Account**.
6. If you selected a paid plan, you’ll be redirected to payment to finish setup.
7. After signup, use **Partner Login** to access your dashboard.

## Google signup (if enabled)

If your account has Google auth enabled, you’ll see a **Continue with Google** option in the signup modal.

If you choose Google signup, you may still be asked for:

- Business name
- Phone number

## What happens next

After your first login, Knotie AI may prompt you to:

- Set up a **passkey** (recommended)
- Enable **MFA** (recommended, especially if you connect Stripe)
