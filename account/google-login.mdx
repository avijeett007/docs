## Overview

Google login makes it easier to access the Partner Portal without remembering a password.

Note: Google login may be enabled or disabled depending on your account configuration.

## Steps

1. Go to **Partner Login**.
2. Click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account you want to use.
4. You’ll be redirected back to the Partner Portal automatically.

## Good to know

- Use the same email consistently to avoid creating multiple accounts.
- If you don’t see Google login, it may not be enabled on your environment.
