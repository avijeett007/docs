## Overview

Google login lets you access the Partner Portal without a separate password. The same button handles both **first-time signup** and **returning logins**.

<Note>
  Google login may be enabled or disabled depending on your platform configuration. If you don't see the button, use email and password instead.
</Note>

## Returning login (existing account)

1. Go to **Partner Login** (knotie-ai.pro/partner/login).
2. Click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose your Google account.
4. You're redirected back to the Partner Portal automatically.

Use the same Google account email you signed up with to avoid creating a duplicate account.

## First-time signup with Google

1. Open **knotie-ai.pro** and click **Start for free**.
2. Inside the signup modal, click **Continue with Google**.
3. Choose your Google account. The modal updates to show a **"Continuing with Google"** panel with your Google profile photo and email.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the signup modal in "Continuing with Google" state — showing the user's Google profile photo, name, email, and the Business Name + Phone Number fields below._

4. Fill in:
   - **Business name** (required)
   - **Phone number** (required)
5. (Optional) Enter a **coupon code**.
6. Click **Complete Google Signup**.
7. The modal transitions to the **7-Day Free Trial** confirmation view — click **Start 7-Day Free Trial** to add your card in Stripe and activate the trial.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Continue with Google" button not visible** — Google auth may not be enabled on your platform. Contact your platform operator.
- **Wrong account selected** — click "Switch account" on the Google account picker. Make sure you use the same email for every login.
- **Redirect loops after Google login** — clear cookies for knotie-ai.pro and try again.
- **Missing profile photo** — the modal fetches your Google profile at signup time. If the image fails to load, a placeholder icon is shown. This doesn't affect the signup itself.
