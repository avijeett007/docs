## Overview

This guide walks you through logging into the **Knotie AI Pro Partner Portal** as a new partner. By the end, you'll be on your dashboard with full access to the onboarding checklist.

**Time to complete:** ~5 minutes for first login (password change + optional MFA).

---

## Step 1 — Navigate to Partner Login

Go to the partner login page:

```
https://knotie-ai.pro/partner/login
```

![Partner Login page](/images/screenshots/login-page-2026-06-09.png)

**What you'll see:**
- Email and password fields
- Sign In button
- "Continue with Google" option
- "Login with Passkey" option
- "Forgot password?" link

---

## Step 2 — Enter Credentials

Enter the email address and temporary password provided in your welcome email.

- **Email:** your partner email (e.g. `partner@example.com`)
- **Password:** the temporary password from the welcome email

Click **Sign In**.

> **First login only:** If this is your first time logging in, you'll be prompted to change your password immediately after this step.

---

## Step 3 — Set a New Password (First Login Only)

If this is your first login, you'll see the **Change Password** screen:

![Partner Login page (also used as visual reference for the first-time login flow until a dedicated Change Password screenshot is captured)](/images/screenshots/login-page-2026-06-09.png)

**Fill in all three fields:**
- **Current Password** — enter your temporary password
- **New Password** — create a strong password (min. 8 characters, mixed case, numbers)
- **Confirm New Password** — re-enter your new password

Click **Change Password** to continue.

---

## Step 4 — Secure Your Account / Passkey Setup

After your first password change, you'll see the **"Secure Your Account"** modal offering passkey setup, followed by the **Welcome** screen.

**Choose one of:**
- **Set Up Later** — skip passkey setup and go to the dashboard *(you can enable it later under Settings → Profile Settings)*
- **Create a Passkey** — follow the browser prompt to register a passkey

<Note>
  The passkey prompt appears *before* the extra sign-in security (MFA) setup. Both are available — a passkey is the newer option and works across all your devices. Your password, MFA, and passkey controls all live under **Settings → Profile Settings**.
</Note>

You can enable **MFA** (a code from an authenticator app) separately any time from **Settings → Profile Settings**.

## Step 5 — Choose How You Start

After the security prompt, you land on your dashboard. A small selector lets you switch how you want to work, and a **Watch Tutorial** button is always available:

- **Manual** — the standard dashboard (this is where you'll spend most of your time).
- **Nexus** — a preview of a new chat-first way to run your whole agency from one window. Choosing it opens the preview below.
- **Watch Tutorial** — play a guided tour of the portal.

If you pick **Nexus**, you'll see a preview screen. It's an early look — nothing here touches your real data yet, and you can return to **Manual** at any time.

<Frame>

</Frame>

For everyday use, stay on **Manual**.

## Step 6 — Partner Dashboard & Your Onboarding Path

You're now on the **Partner Dashboard**. Your onboarding is laid out as a series of **levels** — you complete short challenges to move from one level to the next.

![Partner Dashboard onboarding — Level 0 progress bar and the "Make the Platform Yours" challenge with its steps](/images/screenshots/login-step-6-dashboard-onboarding.png)

### Your first challenge

You start at **Level 0**, with your first challenge — **Make the Platform Yours** — front and center. It walks you through a few quick steps to get set up:

- **Take a Quick Platform Tour** — a short overview of how the dashboard fits together.
- **See Why Your Customer Portal Matters** — the value of your white-label portal.
- **Follow the Portal Setup Guide** — set up your portal.
- **Create the Right Experience for Every Customer** — tailor what customers see.
- **Choose Your Default Customer Experience** — pick the default portal experience for new customers.

As you finish challenges, the progress bar fills and you unlock the next level.

### What's coming up next

Later levels guide you through the rest of your setup:

- **Onboard Your First Customer**
- **Launch Your First AI Agent**
- **Connect Stripe and Start Getting Paid**

> **Progress is saved automatically** — you can pick up where you left off across multiple sessions.

### Quick Actions (Top of Dashboard)

- **Pricing Calculator** — estimate pricing for new customers
- **Onboard New Customer** — start the customer onboarding flow

### Account Overview (Top-Right)

- **Knotie Credits** — your current balance (5,000 credits on signup)
- **Telephony** — call spend to date
- **Auto-recharge** — toggle auto-recharge settings
- **Quick Buy Credits** — purchase more credits
- **Storage** — portal storage used vs. allocated

## Troubleshooting

**"Invalid credentials" on login**
- Check your email spelling — use the address from your welcome email
- Your temporary password expires after first use — must be changed immediately
- If you forgot your password, use **"Forgot password?"** to reset

**MFA setup failing**
- Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
- Save the backup codes in a secure location
- You can skip and enable MFA later from **Settings → Profile Settings**

**Dashboard shows "Setting up your command center"**
- This is a loading screen — wait a few seconds
- If it persists more than 30s, refresh the page

**Onboarding steps not saving progress**
- Progress saves automatically after each step
- If a step doesn't save, try completing it again or refresh the dashboard
