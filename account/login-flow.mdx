## Overview

This guide walks you through logging into the **Knotie AI Pro Partner Portal** as a new partner. By the end, you'll be on your dashboard with full access to the onboarding checklist.

**Time to complete:** ~5 minutes for first login (password change + optional MFA).

---

## Step 1 — Navigate to Partner Login

Go to the partner login page:

```
https://knotie-ai.pro/partner/login
```

![Partner Login page](/images/screenshots/login-page-2026-06-09.png)

**What you'll see:**
- Email and password fields
- Sign In button (primary CTA)
- "Continue with Google" option (SSO)
- "Login with Passkey" option (FIDO2/WebAuthn)
- "Forgot password?" link

---

## Step 2 — Enter Credentials

Enter the email address and temporary password provided in your welcome email.

- **Email:** your partner email (e.g. `veda@kno2gether.com`)
- **Password:** the temporary password from the welcome email

Click **Sign In**.

> **First login only:** If this is your first time logging in, you'll be prompted to change your password immediately after this step.

---

## Step 3 — Set a New Password (First Login Only)

If this is your first login, you'll see the **Change Password** screen:

![Partner Login page (also used as visual reference for the first-time login flow until a dedicated Change Password screenshot is captured)](/images/screenshots/login-page-2026-06-09.png)

**Fill in all three fields:**
- **Current Password** — enter your temporary password
- **New Password** — create a strong password (min. 8 characters, mixed case, numbers)
- **Confirm New Password** — re-enter your new password

Click **Change Password** to continue.

---

## Step 4 — Secure Your Account / Passkey Setup

After your first password change, you'll see the **"Secure Your Account"** modal offering passkey setup, followed by the **Welcome** screen.

**Choose one of:**
- **Set Up Later** — skip passkey setup and go to the dashboard *(you can enable it later under Settings → Security)*
- **Create a Passkey** — follow the browser prompt to register a passkey (FIDO2/WebAuthn)

<Note>
  The passkey prompt appears *before* the traditional MFA setup. Both are available — passkey is the newer option and works across all your devices via your OS's credential manager.
</Note>

You can enable **MFA** (TOTP authenticator app) separately any time from **Settings → Security**.

## Step 5 — Welcome Screen

After the security prompt, you'll see a welcome/video overview:

![Welcome screen with Manual, Nexus New, and Watch Tutorial buttons](/images/screenshots/login-step-5-welcome-screen-annotated.png)

**Your options:**
- **Manual** — skip the walkthrough and go straight to the dashboard *(recommended for returning users)*
- **Nexus New** — explore the Nexus feature (new feature onboarding)
- **Watch Tutorial** — play an interactive guided tour of the portal

To go directly to the dashboard, click **Manual**.

---

## Step 6 — Partner Dashboard & 6-Step Onboarding Checklist

You're now on the **Partner Dashboard**:

![Partner Dashboard with 6-step onboarding checklist](/images/screenshots/login-step-6-dashboard-onboarding-annotated.png)

### Getting Started Widget

The **Getting Started (0/6)** widget is your onboarding guide. Complete all 6 steps to unlock **100 Knotie Credits** as a reward.

| # | Step | What to do |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | **Customize Your Pod** | Set up your whitelabel portal with subdomain or custom domain |
| 2 | **Onboard Your First Customer** | Create your first customer account via **Quick Onboard** |
| 3 | **Import an Agent** | Create or import an AI agent (Retell, VAPI, Ultravox, ElevenLabs, GHL) |
| 4 | **Review Analytics** | Confirm your customer has accessed their portal |
| 5 | **Complete Whitelabeling** | Configure email settings (SMTP or SES domain) |
| 6 | **Setup Your Payments** | Integrate your Stripe Connect account |

> **Progress is saved automatically** — you can complete the checklist across multiple sessions.
> The checklist reappears every 7 days if you haven't completed it.

### Quick Actions (Top of Dashboard)

- **Pricing Calculator** — estimate pricing for new customers
- **Onboard New Customer** — start the customer onboarding flow

### Account Overview (Top-Right)

- **Knotie Credits** — your current balance (5,000 credits on signup)
- **Telephony** — call spend to date
- **Auto-recharge** — toggle auto-recharge settings
- **Quick Buy Credits** — purchase more credits
- **Storage** — portal storage used vs. allocated

## Troubleshooting

**"Invalid credentials" on login**
- Check your email spelling — use the address from your welcome email
- Your temporary password expires after first use — must be changed immediately
- If you forgot your password, use **"Forgot password?"** to reset

**MFA setup failing**
- Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
- Save the backup codes in a secure location
- You can skip and enable MFA later from **Settings → Security**

**Dashboard shows "Setting up your command center"**
- This is a loading screen — wait a few seconds
- If it persists more than 30s, refresh the page

**Onboarding steps not saving progress**
- Progress saves automatically after each step
- If a step doesn't save, try completing it again or refresh the dashboard
