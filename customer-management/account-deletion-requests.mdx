## Overview

Customers can request that their account or personal data be deleted directly from their portal. The request is sent to you (the partner) by email — **nothing is deleted automatically**. You review the request and take action using the account controls you already have in the customer management panel.

This feature is available on the **Account Settings** page inside the customer portal, at the bottom of the page below Team Management.

## What a customer can request

- **Delete my account** — A request to remove their entire account, including all associated data.
- **Delete some of my data** — A request to remove specific personal data while keeping their account.

Both requests are declared in the **Data safety** section of the AgentAction Google Play listing, so the platform is obligated to honour them.

## How the request flow works

1. The customer opens **Account Settings** in their portal.
2. They scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. They select **Request account deletion** or **Request data deletion**.
4. A confirmation prompt appears. They must confirm before the request is sent.

## What you receive as the partner

When a customer submits a deletion request, you receive an email at the address configured in your email settings. The email includes:

- The customer's name and email address.
- The type of request (account deletion or data deletion).
- The date and time of the request.

<Note>
If the email notification fails to send (for example, due to a temporary SMTP issue), the request is still logged and the customer sees a success confirmation. The email failure is recorded in your platform logs.
</Note>

## What you do next

Deletion requests do not take effect automatically. You review the email, decide what to do, and then act using your existing customer controls:

1. Open **Customers → Active Customers** in your Partner Portal.
2. Find the customer.
3. Select **Manage** and use the available options:
   - **Block Access** to immediately suspend the account while you prepare.
   - **Delete** under Customer Portal Access to remove their portal login.

For more on these actions, see [Customer portal access](/partner-portal/customers/customer-portal-access-actions).

## Rate limit

Customers can submit up to **5 deletion requests per hour**. This prevents accidental or repeated submissions from flooding your inbox.

## Partner visibility

Currently, the only record of a deletion request is the email you receive. There is no in-portal list of pending requests. Future updates may add a request queue visible inside the Partner Portal.
