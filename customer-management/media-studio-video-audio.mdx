## Overview

Several video generation models in the Media Studio produce videos **with audio by default** — and their credit prices already reflect the audio-on rate. The platform now explicitly requests audio from the provider for these models, so the audio you pay for is reliably the audio you receive.

## Which models include audio

| Model | Audio included | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| veo-3.1 | Yes | Google Veo — priced at audio-on rate |
| veo-3.1-fast | Yes | Faster variant — priced at audio-on rate |
| kling-2.6-pro | Yes | Kling Pro — priced at audio-on rate |

Other models in the catalogue do not include audio and are priced lower accordingly.

## What changed

Previously, whether audio appeared in the output depended on the provider's own default setting — a vendor default that could change at any time without any change to Knotie AI. If the vendor had ever silently switched to audio-off, customers would have continued paying audio-on rates while receiving silent video.

The platform now **explicitly sends `generate_audio: true`** to the provider for these three models whenever a video generation job is created. This means:

- The audio you pay for is the audio you receive.
- The output will always include an audio track when the model supports it.
- A future vendor default change will not silently affect your generated videos.

## Generating a video with audio

No extra steps are needed. When a customer selects veo-3.1, veo-3.1-fast, or kling-2.6-pro in the Media Studio, audio is requested automatically.

1. Open the **Media Studio** in the customer portal.
2. Enter a video prompt and select one of the audio-enabled models from the model picker.
3. Click **Generate** — the delivered file will include an audio track.

## Credit costs

Credit costs for audio-on models are unchanged — the pricing already accounted for audio-on generation. No credit adjustment is needed.

For credit rates per model tier, see [Credits and AI credit management](/partner-portal/customers/ai-credit-management).

## Common questions

**Will older video jobs be regenerated?**
No. This fix applies to new generation jobs only. Existing completed jobs are unaffected.

**What if I don't want audio in the output?**
The models listed above are configured and priced for audio-on generation. If you need audio-off output, raise a request with support — audio-off pricing is different and must be quoted separately.

**Why is the audio track silent on some jobs?**
If a delivered video has no audio despite using an audio-enabled model, the most likely causes are: the prompt contained no audible scene elements, or the job was created before this fix was deployed. Regenerate the job to apply the fix.
