## Overview

Customers whose accounts have **Project Management** enabled now see an **Hours remaining** indicator and a **Buy hours** button directly in the portal sidebar. Previously, customers had to navigate to **Billing → Hours** to check their balance or purchase more time — a tab most users never opened.

The sidebar widget shows the same live balance as the Hours page, so a purchase made anywhere updates both views instantly.

## Where it appears

| Sidebar type | Location |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard sidebar** | Inside the credits card, below "Add Credits" |
| **Classic sidebar** | Small card below the AI-credit widget |
| **Collapsed rail (both)** | A clock icon whose tooltip shows the balance |

The widget is only visible when **Project Management** is switched on for that customer's account. Customers without Project Management see no change.

## Buying hours from the sidebar

1. The customer opens the portal (the white-label URL their partner gave them).
2. In the sidebar, they see **Hours remaining: X h Y min** (or the equivalent in their portal language).
3. They click **Buy hours** to open the purchase window.
4. They choose a package and complete checkout.
5. The sidebar balance updates immediately after the purchase confirms.

## When "Buy hours" is hidden

The **Buy hours** button is not shown in every context:

- **Impersonation sessions** — When a partner views the portal as a customer for support purposes, the button is hidden to prevent accidental purchases.
- **Embedded portal** — When the portal is loaded inside an iframe or embedded integration (`canPurchase: false`), the button is hidden. The hours balance still shows.

In both cases the hours balance is still visible; only the purchase action is restricted.

## Prerequisites for customers to see this

Partners need to have configured the following before customers see the sidebar widget:

1. **Project Management enabled** for the customer — toggle it on under **Customers → [Customer] → Manage → Features & Add-ons**.
2. **At least one active Customer Hours Package** created — see [Customer hours packages](/partner-portal/settings/customer-hours-packages).
3. **Stripe Connect configured** — required so customers can complete purchases.

If any of these is missing, the sidebar widget is hidden for that customer.
