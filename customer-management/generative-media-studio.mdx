## What the Generative Media Studio does

The **Generative Media Studio** is the video creation centre inside every customer portal. Partners enable it per-customer via the Features & Add-ons panel. Customers use it to:

- Generate short video clips from prompts using AI models (Seedance, Veo, and others).
- Automatically produce **multiple aspect-ratio versions** of every clip (landscape 16:9, portrait 9:16, square 1:1) in a single derive job.
- **Burn styled captions** directly into a clip — synced to a voiceover, with font, colour, and positioning options.
- Organise assets across projects and keep a searchable library.

## Prerequisites

1. The partner must enable the **Generative Media Studio** toggle in **Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons**.
2. KnotieManager (the media-processing service) must be running and reachable. Partners self-hosting on a VPS provision this via the **Auto-Install** flow.
3. The VPS running KnotieManager must have **ffmpeg** installed _and_ compiled with the `drawtext` filter (required for caption burning). The app logs a warning at startup if `drawtext` is absent.

## Generating a clip

1. Open the customer portal and navigate to **Media Studio**.
2. Choose a **Recipe** (a preset that defines prompt structure, model, style, and shot count).
3. Fill in the recipe fields and click **Generate**.
4. The job enters the queue. A progress indicator appears in the **Library** tab.
5. When the job completes, the clip appears in your library with a stable direct URL.

## Derive jobs — multi-format exports

After generating a clip, the studio automatically creates **derived versions** for the most common ad sizes. Each derive job produces up to four aspect-ratio re-frames from the source clip:

| Format | Dimensions | Common use |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape | 16:9 (1920 × 1080) | YouTube, web, desktop |
| Portrait | 9:16 (1080 × 1920) | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Shorts |
| Square | 1:1 (1080 × 1080) | Instagram feed, Facebook |
| Vertical wide | 4:5 (1080 × 1350) | Instagram feed (tall) |

Derived clips appear as siblings of the source in the library, labelled by their format. They share the same title and tags as the original.

## Caption burning

The studio can render styled captions directly onto a video — the text is **composited into the video pixels**, not an overlay — so it looks the same everywhere the clip is shared.

### How it works

1. Generate (or upload) a clip.
2. Add a **voiceover** to the clip from the Captions panel.
3. Select a caption **style** (see table below).
4. Click **Burn captions**. The platform sends the clip and voiceover to the caption engine.
5. A new captioned version appears in your library alongside the original.

### Caption styles

| Style | Description |
|---|---|
| Default | White text, black drop-shadow, centred lower-third |
| Bold highlight | Thick white text, yellow word highlight on the current cue |
| Minimal | Small light-grey text, lower-third, no shadow |

## Project filing — organising assets

Every generated or derived asset can be filed into one or more **Projects**, letting customers group clips by campaign, client, or content series.

- From the Library, select an asset and choose **File to project**.
- An asset can belong to multiple projects at once. Removing it from one project does not delete it.
- The **Projects** tab shows a gallery view filtered by project.

## Capacity and queue management

The studio processes all video jobs on a shared media-runner service. To protect system stability, the runner caps concurrent ffmpeg jobs and download jobs at fixed limits. When the runner is at capacity:

- Your job is automatically **queued** if there is room in the backlog.
- If the backlog itself is full, the main app leaves the job row in its current state and the **5-minute cron sweep re-submits it automatically** — no action needed.
- Jobs are never lost due to capacity. They cost latency, not data.

The partner portal's **KnotieManager status panel** shows current queue depth (lane: `ffmpeg` and lane: `ingest`) and memory usage. If the runner is consistently saturated, adding CPU/RAM on the VPS or provisioning a second runner node is the recommended fix.

## Common errors

| Error | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Job stays in `ingesting` status for >15 min | KnotieManager is unreachable or at capacity | Check KnotieManager logs; the cron sweep retries every 5 minutes |
| Caption burn fails with "drawtext unavailable" | ffmpeg on the VPS was not compiled with `drawtext` | Reinstall ffmpeg with full codec support (e.g. `ffmpeg-full` on Ubuntu) |
| Derive job shows `failed` | Source clip URL has expired or is unreachable | Re-generate the source clip, then trigger derive again |
| "Media runner is at capacity" shown to user | All ffmpeg slots and the queue are full | Wait ~1 minute and retry; or check VPS resources in the KnotieManager panel |
