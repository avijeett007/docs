## Overview

Motion Studio includes nine new template families in wave 2. Each template has a distinct visual style and takes a small amount of customer input — a product photo, a few words, or a short video clip. The portal wizard presents all available templates with preview thumbnails.

## Wave-2 template catalogue

### Product templates

| Template | Visual look | Customer input required |
|---|---|---|
| **product-hero-3d** | The customer's product appears as a 3D hero object placed in a generated world | 1 product photo |
| **product-orbit-3d** | Lifestyle objects orbit the product and burst on the beat drop | 1 product photo |
| **comic-panel-ad** | A comic page builds panel-by-panel on the beat; the product crashes in on the drop | 1 product photo (used as uploaded — not AI-processed) |

### Character templates

| Template | Visual look | Customer input required |
|---|---|---|
| **clay-character** | An original plasticine character performs in a miniature clay set at 12 fps | Story captions |
| **papercraft-character** | A cardboard toy theatre with cut-paper layers and torn caption strips | Story captions |

### Typographic and explainer templates

| Template | Visual look | Customer input required |
|---|---|---|
| **kinetic-3d-type** | Extruded 3D words move through generated worlds | Words (the text to extrude) |
| **mono-3d-explainer** | A grey 3D stage with one accent colour; features a phone counter, a calendar, and a prize wheel | Numbers and steps |

### Story templates

| Template | Visual look | Customer input required |
|---|---|---|
| **isometric-story** | Abstract figures clear one named hurdle per beat on a flat isometric track | Hurdle names (one per beat) |

### Footage templates

| Template | Visual look | Customer input required |
|---|---|---|
| **footage-overlay** | The customer's own video clip plays full-frame while icon tiles arc out of an anchor point | 1 video clip (trimmed, retimed, or cropped only — no AI processing applied to the clip) |

## Picking a template

1. Open **Media Studio → Motion Studio** in the portal.
2. The wizard shows available templates with preview thumbnails.
3. Select a template to see what inputs it needs.
4. Supply the required input (photo, words, captions, or video clip).
5. Review the credit estimate, then select **Make it**.

<Note>
Templates that use AI-generated plates (such as the 3D product and character templates) require the `ai-plate` module to be active. Ask your partner if a template is unavailable.
</Note>

## Footage overlay — video clip requirements

The footage-overlay template accepts a customer-supplied video clip. The clip must meet these technical requirements:

- Between 16 and 4096 pixels on each side
- Aspect ratio within a 4:1 ratio (e.g., standard 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 are all fine)

The clip is trimmed, retimed, or cropped as needed, but its content is not AI-processed or replaced.

## Character template content policy

Clay-character, papercraft-character, and comic-panel-ad use AI to generate stylised characters. To comply with the platform's content policy:

- Do not enter real names (famous people, public figures, or other individuals) in story captions or brief fields.
- The generation system automatically blocks prompts that appear to describe or imitate a real person.
- If a character prompt is rejected, rewrite it to describe a fictional character without referencing a real person's appearance.
