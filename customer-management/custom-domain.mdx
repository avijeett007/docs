## Overview

Custom domains let customers use their own brand URL for the portal.

## Before you start

- Confirm you have access to the domain’s DNS
- Decide whether you will use a subdomain (recommended)

## Setup steps

1. Add the domain in the customer settings.
2. Copy the required DNS records.
3. Add the records in the customer’s DNS provider.
4. Wait for DNS propagation.
5. Verify the domain in Knotie AI.
