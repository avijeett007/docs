## Overview

Motion Studio ships with a growing library of video templates. Each template controls the visual style, layout, and animation of your video. You choose a template when you create a new video job; the AI fills in content from your brief.

## Template families

### Wave-3 templates (new in November 2026)

Six new template families were added as part of the wave-3 release:

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Vintage Collage Doc** | Documentary-style brand stories; layered paper and photo cutouts |
| **App Showcase 3D** | Mobile or web app launches; 3-D phone with animated UI |
| **Floating UI Scene** | SaaS products; UI elements floating in a soft 3-D environment |
| **Surreal Kinetic Collage** | Bold, eye-catching social ads; mixed media, fast cuts |
| **Retro TV Reveal** | Nostalgic or retro brand moments; CRT-TV frame reveal |
| **Hand-Drawn 2D** | Warm, personal storytelling; animated pencil-and-brush illustration with a character |

## Choosing a template

1. Open **Media Studio → Home** or **Recipes**.
2. Select **New video** or choose a recipe that includes a video step.
3. Browse the template gallery. Hover over a card to see a preview clip.
4. Select a template and fill in your brief (business name, message, photos, music mood, and any template-specific fields).
5. Review the credit estimate, then select **Make it**.

<Note>
Some templates require specific inputs. For example, **Creator Recut** needs you to upload a source video clip (up to 90 seconds). Templates that include a **camera move** use a paid generation; the credit estimate covers the move.
</Note>

## Camera moves

Templates that include a paid camera move (a generated video plate with motion) now resume correctly when a job is restarted. If your video was interrupted mid-generation, the camera move is not re-generated and re-billed — the existing asset is reused.

## Audio quality

All Motion Studio videos go through an automatic audio quality pass:

- **Music**: Background music is trimmed to match your video length, so the track fits cleanly from start to finish.
- **SFX loudness**: Sound effects are normalised to a consistent loudness (−14 to −12 LUFS) with a true-peak ceiling to prevent clipping, even on louder output devices.
