## What this is

Whitelabeling helps partners provide a branded experience. This section is written to help partners/agencies understand the whitelabel portal and support customers.

## When to use a whitelabel subdomain

- You want a fast setup without DNS changes.
- You want customers to sign in at a branded URL.

## Setup steps

1. Choose a subdomain (for example, `portal.yourbrand.com` or `yourbrand.knotie-ai.pro`).
2. Configure the whitelabel settings for your customer.
3. Confirm the portal loads and customers can sign in.

## Support notes

If a customer cannot access the portal, confirm:

- The correct portal URL was shared
- Their account is active
- Portal access is enabled
