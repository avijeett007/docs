## What a whitelabel subdomain does

A whitelabel subdomain gives you a branded login link for your Customer Portal without needing any DNS work.

Most partners use a whitelabel subdomain first, then upgrade to a custom domain later.

## When a whitelabel subdomain is the best choice

- **Fast setup**: you want a working portal link in minutes.
- **No DNS access**: the customer can’t (or won’t) update domain records.
- **Pilot launches**: you want to test the portal with a small group first.

## Set up your whitelabel subdomain

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Whitelabel**.
3. Find the **Subdomain** section.
4. Enter the subdomain you want customers to use.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Open the portal link in a private/incognito window to confirm:
   - The portal loads.
   - The branding looks right.
   - Customers can sign in.

## Share the login link with customers

- Use the whitelabel portal URL as the “single source of truth” for logins.
- If you send portal invites, paste the same URL in the email so customers don’t bookmark an old link.

## Full white label portal setup

Beyond just the subdomain, you can customize the entire portal experience:

### Branding customization

1. Go to **Settings → Branding Settings**.
2. Configure:
   - **Portal title** – Your company name or product name
   - **Portal slogan** – Tagline or description
   - **Logo** – Upload your company logo (light and dark versions)
   - **Brand colors** – Primary color, accent colors
   - **Favicon** – Small icon that appears in browser tabs

### Custom domain (advanced)

For a fully branded experience, use a custom domain instead of a subdomain:

1. Go to **Settings → Custom Domain**.
2. Enter your domain (e.g., `portal.yourcompany.com`).
3. Add the required DNS records to your domain provider.
4. Verify the domain.
5. Wait for SSL certificate provisioning.

See [Custom Domain](/customer-management/custom-domain) for detailed instructions.

### Email branding

Ensure emails sent from the portal match your brand:

1. Configure **Email Settings** with your domain.
2. Set up **Email Domain Management** for custom sender addresses.
3. Test emails to confirm branding appears correctly.

## Support notes (common issues)

- **Portal opens but looks unbranded**
  - Double-check you saved the whitelabel settings.
  - Refresh the page (hard refresh) or test in an incognito window.
- **Customer says the link doesn't work**
  - Confirm they copied the full URL (no trailing spaces).
  - Confirm the customer is active and has portal access.
- **Customer can't sign in**
  - Make sure they're using the email address you invited.
