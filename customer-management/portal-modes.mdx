## Overview

Portal modes determine what your customers can see and do when they sign in.

## Common portal options

- Knowledge base
- Integrations
- Docs & media
- Schedule meeting
- API keys
- Pricing visibility

## How to change portal modes

1. Open the customer settings.
2. Find **Portal modes** (or equivalent).
3. Enable/disable the options you want.
4. Save changes.
