## What portal modes are

Portal modes control the overall layout and “style” of your Customer Portal experience.

In the Partner Portal you can choose from these modes:

- **Basic Portal**: a clean, professional portal with customizable themes.
- **Professional Portal**: an advanced layout with enhanced features (marked as **upcoming** in the UI).
- **SaaS Portal**: an enterprise funnel-style portal experience designed for conversion.

## Plan access (what you can select)

- **Free Forever**
  - Basic Portal
  - SaaS Portal _(see note below)_
- **Starter / Pro / Lifetime**
  - Basic Portal
  - Professional Portal (premium / upcoming)
- **Enterprise**
  - Basic Portal
  - Professional Portal
  - SaaS Portal

If a mode is not available for your plan, it shows as **Locked** in the selector.

<Note>
**Free Forever + SaaS Portal.** Free Forever partners can now select SaaS Portal mode. The portal shell and default funnel become available immediately, but individual experience landing pages (such as the AI Receptionist) only go live when a Stage subscription grants the relevant experience entitlement. Without an entitlement, the storefront renders a portal-disabled page — nothing is published. Customer credit plans and subscription plans inside the portal remain locked behind their own unlock flags. See [Stage subscriptions](/partner-portal/settings/stage-subscriptions) for how to grant experience access.
</Note>

## How to change portal mode

1. In the Partner Portal, open **Settings**.
2. Go to **White-Label Portal**.
3. Scroll to the **Portal Mode** selector in the left navigation panel.
4. Click the mode you want:
   - You'll see labels like **Premium**, **Upcoming**, or **Locked** depending on availability.
5. Click **Save**.

Tip: Use **Preview** (if available) to quickly sanity-check the portal before you share the link with customers.

## When to use each mode

- **Basic Portal**
  - Best for most partners.
  - Fast to configure.
  - Great for a simple branded login + portal experience.
- **Professional Portal (Coming Soon)**
  - Offers a premium layout and enhanced presentation.
  - Plan your workflow around this feature, but note that it is still in development.
- **SaaS Portal**
  - Best for teams that want a full funnel-like portal experience.
  - Designed for partners selling access as a SaaS-style offering.
  - Available to Free Forever partners — individual experience landing pages activate when granted via a Stage subscription.
