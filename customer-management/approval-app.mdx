## Overview

The approval app lets a customer approve or deny actions their AI agent wants to take — from their phone. Before the first approval can be sent, the customer must pair at least one phone with their portal.

## Step 1 — Download the app

The customer portal shows a **Get the approval app** panel the first time a customer visits the Approvals section (before any phone is paired).

1. Open the customer portal and go to **Approvals**.
2. On the **Get the approval app** panel, either:
   - Tap **Download for Android** to open the download page directly.
   - Scan the QR code with the phone's camera to open the same page.

<Note>
The Android app is available to everyone. iPhone access is through TestFlight — the customer can request an invitation from support.
</Note>

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the "Get the approval app" card showing the download button and QR code side by side._

## Step 2 — Pair a phone

Once the app is installed, the customer pairs their phone from the portal:

1. On the **Approvals** page, click **Pair a phone**.
2. A QR code appears on screen.
3. Open the approval app on the phone and scan the QR code.
4. The portal confirms: **Phone paired**.

The paired phone now appears in **Your paired devices**. Multiple phones can be paired.

## Troubleshooting

### "We cannot tell whether your phone paired"

If the portal cannot confirm the pairing status after several attempts, it shows:

> We cannot tell whether your phone paired.
> If the app says it is paired, close this — it will be in your list of phones. Otherwise start again.

If the phone app shows it is paired, close the dialog and refresh the **Your paired devices** list — the phone will appear there. If nothing is paired, tap **Close** and try again.

### The QR code expired

Pairing codes expire after a short time. Click **Close** and open the pairing dialog again to get a fresh code.

### The app is not available in my region

The Android build is available globally. For iPhone, contact support to request a TestFlight invitation.
