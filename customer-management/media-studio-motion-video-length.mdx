## Overview

When you create a motion video in Media Studio you can now pick the target length of your video. Longer videos cost more credits but give you more time to tell your story. Short videos are still the recommended choice for social content — they perform best and finish fastest.

## Available lengths

| Length | Relative price | Best for |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Short** (template default) | × 1 | Social posts, quick demos, reels |
| **30 seconds** | × 1.5 | Short-form ads, story formats |
| **1 minute** | × 2.5 | Explainers, product walkthroughs |
| **2 minutes** | × 4 | Brand films, detailed demos |
| **5 minutes** | × 8 | Long-form promos, training content |

Credit costs are calculated as `base motion price × length multiplier`. The credit quote shown in the wizard already reflects the length you selected.

<Note>
Not every template supports every length. The wizard shows only the lengths your chosen template can produce. If a template has only one length option the chips are hidden.
</Note>

<Warning>
Short videos look best. 15–30 seconds keeps attention on social and gives the sharpest result. Longer videos take longer to make and should be reserved for content that genuinely needs the extra time.
</Warning>

## How to choose a length

1. Open **Media Studio → Home** or **Recipes**.
2. Choose a motion graphics recipe.
3. Fill in your brief — describe the scene, upload assets, and set any other options.
4. Under the brief you will see **length chips** when the template supports multiple lengths. Select the length you want.
5. Review the credit estimate — the number updates when you change the length.
6. Select **Make it** to start the generation.

## Credit costs for partners

Partners see the credit cost for each length before scheduling a render. The cost is re-verified when the job starts: if the price changed between quote and start the job is cancelled and the credits are returned automatically.

Revision requests are priced at the same length multiplier as the original render.

## Revisions and re-renders

If you request a revision on a motion video the credit cost is the same multiplier as the length you originally chose. To change the length you need to start a new creation.
