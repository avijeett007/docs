## What you can create

Media Studio offers guided recipes for business images, ads, social posts, films, avatars, and product content. Each recipe shows an estimated credit cost before you begin.

![Branded Media Studio landing page with a prompt, recipe shortcuts, and Start creating actions](/images/screenshots/media-studio-public-production-2026-09-12.png)

## Start a creation

1. Open **Media Studio** from your portal, or click **Start creating** on the public studio page.
2. Log in if prompted.
3. Choose a recipe or describe what you want in plain language.
4. Choose the available style and format options.
5. Review the credit quote, then start the generation.

When the result is ready, use the available actions to download it, copy its link, or send it to a supported social channel.

<Note>
Credit amounts shown on the public page are estimates. Check the quote shown immediately before you create.
</Note>

## If you cannot open Media Studio

Ask your provider to confirm that Media Studio is enabled for both the portal and your account. The feature is Experimental and may change while it is being improved.
