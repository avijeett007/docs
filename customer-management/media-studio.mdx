## What Generative Media Studio is

The **Generative Media Studio** lets you create AI-generated images and videos directly from your portal — no separate accounts or tools needed. Everything runs under your partner's branded portal.

You can:
- Generate a still image from a text prompt
- Generate a short video from a text prompt
- Build a **cinematic pipeline** — a multi-shot sequence assembled into a single video
- Browse, manage, and pin your generated assets in the gallery

Generation costs credits. The exact cost depends on the model and output resolution or duration, and is shown before you confirm each job.

## Prerequisites

The **Media Studio** must be enabled by your partner for your account. If you don't see the Studio section in your portal navigation, contact your service provider.

## Generating an image

1. Go to **Media Studio** in the portal navigation.
2. Select the **Image** tab.
3. Choose a model from the model picker. The card shows the model's name, a brief description, supported resolutions, and the estimated credit cost per resolution tier.
4. Write your prompt in the text box. For best results:
   - Be descriptive about style, lighting, and composition
   - Avoid prompts that reference real people by name
5. Choose an output **resolution** (e.g., 512px, 1024px, 2048px). Higher resolutions cost more credits.
6. Click **Generate**.
7. Credits are deducted immediately. You are taken to the **Jobs** tab where your generation appears in the queue.

## Generating a video

1. Go to **Media Studio** and select the **Video** tab.
2. Choose a video model (e.g., Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, Kling).
3. Write your prompt describing the scene, motion, and style.
4. Choose the output **duration** (in seconds). Each model has minimum and maximum duration limits shown on the model card.
5. Click **Generate**.
6. Credits are deducted and the job enters the queue. Video generation typically takes longer than images; the status shows **Processing** until complete.

## Cinematic pipeline (multi-shot sequences)

A **cinematic pipeline** lets you assemble multiple shots into one continuous video.

1. Go to **Media Studio → Pipeline** tab.
2. Click **New Pipeline** and give it a name.
3. Add shots one at a time — each shot has its own prompt, model, and duration.
4. Reorder shots by dragging the shot cards.
5. Click **Assemble** when you're ready. The platform generates each shot in sequence and stitches them into a single output video.
6. Assembled pipelines appear in the gallery as a single video asset.

<Info>
  Each individual shot in a pipeline is billed separately at generation time. The assembly step does not cost additional credits.
</Info>

## Job status and the gallery

Every generation job goes through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Queued** | Job is waiting to be sent to the generation provider |
| **Processing** | Provider is actively generating the asset |
| **Completed** | Asset is ready and available in the gallery |
| **Failed** | Generation failed — credits are automatically refunded |

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the Jobs tab / gallery view — showing cards with status badges, thumbnails, and action buttons._

Completed jobs appear in the **Gallery** tab as media cards. You can:
- **Preview** the image or play the video inline
- **Download** the asset
- **Pin** the asset to prevent it from expiring
- **Delete** the asset (immediate, cannot be undone, credits are not refunded for completed jobs)

## Pinning assets

Unpinned generated assets are automatically deleted after **30 days**. To keep an asset permanently:

1. Find the asset in the gallery.
2. Click the **Pin** button (pin icon) on the asset card.

Pinned assets are marked with a pin badge and are excluded from the auto-expiry sweep. You can unpin an asset at any time, after which it will be subject to the 30-day expiry from its original creation date.

<Warning>
  Make sure to pin any assets you want to keep permanently before 30 days have elapsed. After deletion, assets cannot be recovered.
</Warning>

## Credit costs

Credit costs are shown on each model card and confirmed before you submit a job. Costs vary by:

- **Model** — higher-quality models cost more per generation
- **Resolution** (images) — 512px < 1024px < 2048px < 4096px
- **Duration** (videos) — longer clips cost more credits

If a job fails, your credits are fully and automatically refunded. No manual refund request is needed.
