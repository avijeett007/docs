## Overview

Media Studio combines a plain-language prompt with guided recipes for images, ads, social posts, product content, avatars, and films. The portal shows an estimated credit amount before you start.

![Media Studio home with prompt controls, recipe shortcuts, projects, and the agent connection](/images/screenshots/customer-media-studio-home-production-2026-09-14.png)

## Start a creation

1. Open **Media Studio → Home**.
2. Describe what you want to make, or choose a recipe.
3. Add a photo when the selected job needs one.
4. Choose the available format options.
5. Review the estimate, then select **Make it**.

<Note>
The home screen labels credit amounts as estimates. Review the final quote before starting a generation.
</Note>

## Browse the studio

- **Recipes** groups ready-made jobs by output and business goal.
- **Projects** keeps related creations together.
- **Storyboard** plans a multi-scene film and is marked **Experimental**.
- **Library** stores images, video, and audio. Tag an asset to refer to it by name later.
- **Characters** keeps reusable people, places, and products consistent across creations.
- **Showcase** displays completed work.

### Brand Kit status

The Library currently shows **Brand Kit — Coming soon**. Until it is available, upload your logo to the Library and tag it so you can refer to it by name.

![Library Brand Kit tab marked Coming soon](/images/screenshots/media-studio-brand-kit-coming-soon-production-2026-09-14.png)

## If you cannot open Media Studio

Ask your provider to confirm that Media Studio is enabled both for the partner portal and for your customer account. The experience is **Experimental** and still under active development.
