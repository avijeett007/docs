## What Media Studio is

**Media Studio** gives your customers a self-serve AI content production workspace inside their whitelabel portal. From a gallery of pre-built _recipes_ they pick a use case, answer a short questionnaire, and the platform handles the generation — quoting the credit cost upfront, producing the output, and saving it to their media library.

Customers never need to write prompts. Every recipe encodes the prompt engineering; the customer provides only the business-specific details (their brand, their product, their script).

## Enabling Media Studio for a customer

Media Studio has two gates:

1. **Partner master switch** — enabled at the partner level in the Partner Portal settings. Contact your Knotie partner onboarding team if you do not see the toggle.
2. **Per-customer toggle** — once the partner switch is on, navigate to **Customers → [customer name] → Features & Add-ons** and enable **Media Studio**. The feature appears in that customer's portal sidebar immediately.

Only customers with the per-customer toggle on will see Media Studio; other customers on the same partner account are unaffected.

## The recipe gallery

When a customer opens Media Studio they see the recipe gallery — a grid of generation use cases organized into categories:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Restoration** | Photo Restoration (upscale + repair old or damaged photos) |
| **Characters** | Character Sheet, Wardrobe Sheet, Feature Sheet, Product Sheet |
| **Cartoon & Animation** | 32-preset cartoon pack, Cartoon Series (consistent character videos) |
| **Motion & Video** | Motion posters, image-to-video, voice-seam narrated videos |
| **Social & Ads** | UGC Ads (when enabled by your partner) |
| **Storyboard** | Multi-panel storyboard _(Experimental)_ |
| **Persona / Influencer** | AI persona creation for influencer-style content |

Recipes marked **Coming Soon** show a preview still and cannot be submitted yet.

## Running a generation

1. Open the customer portal and navigate to **Media Studio**.
2. Click any available recipe card.
3. Fill in the recipe's questionnaire (e.g., upload a reference photo, type a script, choose a voice, pick a cartoon style).
4. Review the **credit estimate** shown before committing. The platform quotes the cost before charging — you can back out at this step.
5. Accept the estimate to start the generation.
6. The platform queues the job. Depending on the recipe, output appears in seconds (images) to several minutes (multi-step videos).
7. Finished assets are saved automatically to the customer's **Docs & Media** library with a stable public URL.

<Info>
Credit costs vary by recipe type and output length. Image recipes are cheaper than video recipes. Partners can adjust the profit multiplier per their resell margin in **Settings → Media Studio**. The customer always sees the final quoted price, not the underlying model cost.
</Info>

## Generated media expiry

By default, generated media is stored for **30 days** from creation. After that, the files are automatically removed to free storage. The public URL in the media library becomes invalid after expiry.

Partners can ask the Knotie team to adjust the retention window via the `MEDIA_EXPIRY_DAYS` configuration.

To keep an asset beyond the expiry window, the customer should download it or move it to long-term storage before the deadline.

## Photo Restoration

Photo Restoration is a one-step recipe: upload an old, damaged, or low-resolution photo and the platform returns a cleaned, upscaled version.

Steps:
1. Choose **Photo Restoration** from the recipe gallery.
2. Upload the source photo.
3. Accept the credit estimate.
4. The restored photo is saved to the media library.

No brand kit or reference character is required — the recipe works from the photo alone.

## Character and product sheets

Sheets are a structured way to teach the AI what your customer's characters, products, or people look like — so that every future generation uses the same visual identity.

Four sheet types:

| Sheet | What it captures |
|---|---|
| **Character Sheet** | Face, build, outfit variants for a recurring AI character |
| **Wardrobe Sheet** | Outfit options separated from the character's face |
| **Feature Sheet** | Specific visual features (hair, accessories, environment) |
| **Product Sheet** | A product in multiple poses, backgrounds, and angles |

After submitting a sheet, the platform trains a model (or caches reference embeddings) and the resulting character becomes available as a **choice gate** option in subsequent video and image recipes.

## Cartoon pack

The cartoon pack gives customers access to **32 preset cartoon styles** (e.g., flat vector, 3-D animation, watercolour sketch, retro comic). Customers pick a style and supply a reference photo or description; the platform renders the character in that style.

Cartoon Series uses the same style + character definition to produce a **multi-episode video** where the character stays consistent across clips — ideal for social content where a brand mascot must look the same every time.

## Motion videos and voice narration

Motion recipes animate a static image (photo or AI-generated still) into a short video clip. The **voice seam** option layers in an AI-narrated voiceover — customers pick a voice and type a script, and the system aligns the motion timing to the speech.

Director controls let the customer choose:

- **Camera move** — pan, zoom, dolly, static
- **Motion strength** — subtle to dramatic

## Storyboard _(Experimental)_

Storyboard lets customers lay out a multi-panel visual narrative — each panel is an AI-generated image keyed to a scene description. Panels can be reordered, regenerated individually, or exported as a PDF contact sheet.

<Warning>
Storyboard is **Experimental**. The panel navigation and export flow are functional but the feature is still under active development. Behaviour may change between releases.
</Warning>

## Persona and Influencer Studio

Persona Studio lets customers define an AI influencer identity — name, voice, visual style, personality traits — and generate consistent image and video content for that persona. The persona definition is stored and reused across sessions.

The Social Planner bridge lets customers push finished persona content directly to the **Social Posting** calendar from within Media Studio without leaving the page.

## Storage and quotas

Generated assets count toward the customer's allocated storage quota and the partner's overall pool. When a customer hits their individual cap, new generations fail with a quota error rather than silently overwriting old files.

Partners can review storage usage per customer under **Customers → [customer name] → Storage** (requires the storage admin feature to be enabled for the partner account).

See [Docs & Media library](/customer-management/media-library) for quota limits per plan.
