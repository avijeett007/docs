## Overview

Every AI generation run in Media Studio consumes **media credits**. Credits are separate from voice call credits and are managed by the partner for each customer.

Generation is **blocked** if a customer's credit balance reaches zero — no negative balances are allowed.

## Checking your balance

The **credits popover** in the storyboard canvas toolbar shows your current media credit balance in real time. It updates after each generation run.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the credits popover on the storyboard canvas — showing the balance, estimated cost per run, and a "Top up" or "Get more credits" button._

## How credits are charged

Each generation run shows a **quote** (the exact credit cost) before you confirm. The charge only happens when you click **Confirm** and the generation starts.

The quote accounts for:

- The type of content being generated (image, video, etc.)
- The quality or length settings for the run

## When you run out

When your balance is too low to cover a generation:

- The **Generate** button on the card is **disabled**.
- The card shows **"Needs N more credits"** where N is the shortfall.
- The credits popover shows a **top-up prompt** or a message to contact the portal owner.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of a Production column card showing the "Needs N more credits" disabled state — to help customers recognize the low-balance state._

## Topping up

Customers contact their partner (the portal owner) to top up media credits. Partners manage credit allocations per customer from the Partner Portal:

1. Go to **Customers**.
2. Click **Manage** next to the customer.
3. Open the **Credits & AI Credit Management** tab.
4. Adjust the customer's media credit allocation.

See [AI credit management](/partner-portal/customers/ai-credit-management) for full partner-side instructions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do unused credits roll over?**
Credit rollover depends on the plan your partner has configured. Check with your portal owner for details.

**Can I see a history of what I spent?**
Generation history is visible in the storyboard's Production column — each completed take shows the credits consumed.

**Why did my generation fail even though I had enough credits?**
Credits are reserved at the start of a generation. If the run fails part-way through, your credits are automatically refunded to your balance.
