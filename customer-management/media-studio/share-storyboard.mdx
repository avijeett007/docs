## Overview

Any storyboard can be shared with a single click. The share link gives read-only access to the storyboard without requiring the viewer to log in. Viewers who want to create their own storyboards are shown a branded sign-up prompt from your partner portal.

## How to share

1. Open the storyboard you want to share.
2. Click **Share** in the floating chrome at the top of the canvas.
3. The share panel opens and generates a unique link automatically.
4. Copy the link and send it to your collaborator or client.

## What the viewer sees

When someone opens a share link they see:

- A **read-only view** of the storyboard — cards, connections, annotations, and scene content.
- Your **partner portal branding** (logo, colours) — not the Knotie default.
- A **sign-up prompt** if they want to build their own storyboards — it links to your portal's signup page, not Knotie's.

The viewer **cannot** edit cards, trigger generation, or see internal data like credit costs, provider names, or run IDs.

## Privacy and indexing

Share links are **unlisted** — they are not published anywhere and are not indexed by search engines. Only people who have the exact link can view the storyboard. There is no public gallery or directory of shared storyboards.

## Revoking a share link

To stop sharing:

1. Open the storyboard.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Click **Revoke link** (or the equivalent toggle).

Once revoked, anyone who tries to open the old link sees an "not found" page.

<Note>
Revoking a share link is immediate. If you shared the link widely and then revoke it, existing viewers will lose access as soon as they refresh or return to the URL.
</Note>
