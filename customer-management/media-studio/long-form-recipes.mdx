## What are recipes?

**Recipes** are pre-built creative workflows that guide the generation process through a series of inputs (subject, style, duration, motion, etc.) and produce a finished image or video. They remove the guesswork of prompt engineering: you fill in a brief, the recipe produces a structured result.

Recipes are grouped by **audience** and **use case**:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| For your business | Brand promo clips, product showcases |
| Ads | UGC-style direct-response videos, product ad cuts |
| Social | Short-form clips for Instagram Reels / TikTok |
| Film | Cinematic shots, storyboard sequences |
| Avatars | Talking-head videos with AI presenter |
| Product | Product hero shots, 360° views |

## Quality tiers

Each recipe offers multiple **quality tiers**. Higher tiers use more capable engines and produce better results — at a higher credit cost.

| Tier | Engine family | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Everyday** | Standard models | Quick drafts, social clips, exploration |
| **Studio** | Enhanced models | Client-ready work, product ads |
| **Best** | Premium models | Hero shots, portfolio-quality output |
| **Long-form** | Seedance 2.5 (720p) | Extended narrative videos up to 30 s |

## Long-form videos (new in Phase 6)

The **Long-form** tier uses **Seedance 2.5 at 720p** and supports durations up to **30 seconds** — significantly longer than the 5–8 second clips available on other tiers.

Long-form is suited for:
- Narrative brand stories
- Extended product demonstrations
- Short-film style creative work

### Duration ladder by tier

The available durations are engine-specific — longer durations only appear for engines that support them.

| Tier | Durations available |
|---|---|
| Everyday | 3 s, 5 s, 8 s |
| Studio | 3 s, 5 s, 8 s |
| Best | 3 s, 5 s, 8 s |
| Long-form (Seedance 2.5) | 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, 20 s, 30 s |

## Storyboard and recipe runs

Recipes can also be run from inside the **Storyboard** canvas. A recipe run generates a full beat-by-beat story structure — each card on the board maps to a recipe step. This lets you build, edit, and re-generate individual shots in context.

For a full walkthrough of the Storyboard, see [Storyboard canvas](/customer-management/media-studio/storyboard).

## Common errors

| Error | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Needs N more credits" | Customer balance too low | Partner tops up credits; customer waits for refresh |
| Generation stuck on "Processing" | Provider delay | Wait; the studio will push an update when the provider responds. Long-form jobs can take several minutes. |
| Duration option not showing | Engine doesn't support that length at the selected tier | Switch to Long-form tier, or reduce duration |
| "Engine not available" | The selected engine is temporarily unavailable | Try a different tier or try again later |
