## What Media Studio is

**Media Studio** is the generative AI creation tool inside the customer portal. Customers use it to generate images and videos from text prompts, run multi-step creative recipes, and build visual storyboards — all without leaving the portal.

Media Studio is divided into three main areas:

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Browse curated recipe categories, see platform-made examples, and jump straight into creation |
| **Storyboard** | A full-screen visual canvas for scripting and generating a complete story shot-by-shot |
| **Recipes** | Pre-built creative workflows (photo-to-video, product ads, social clips, etc.) that chain generation steps together |

## Prerequisites

Before a customer can access Media Studio:

1. The partner must enable the **Media Studio** feature for that customer in **Features & Add-ons**.
2. The customer must have sufficient **AI credits** in their balance. The studio shows a live credit balance and disables generation cards when the balance is too low.
3. The partner's account must have credits available to back the generation (credits flow: partner pool → customer generation).

## Navigating the home page

The Media Studio home page has three sections:

### Compose bar
A text prompt field at the top of the page. Type a prompt and press enter to start a generation directly, or pick a recipe first to guide the output.

### Start with a recipe rail
Six category chips — **For your business**, **Ads**, **Social**, **Film**, **Avatars**, **Product** — each linked to a set of pre-built recipes. Clicking a chip opens a brief sheet where you fill in a few inputs and the recipe handles the rest.

### Made with Media Studio showcase
A curated wall of example outputs made with real recipes. Clicking a tile opens the recipe that made it so you can use the same workflow.

## Real-time job updates

As of Phase 6, the studio receives job status updates **as they happen** via a server push (SSE) connection. You no longer need to keep the tab open and wait for a manual refresh — the card updates from "Queued" → "Processing" → "Ready" automatically when your job completes.

If the real-time connection drops (network change, tab backgrounded for a long time), the studio falls back to polling every 10–15 seconds automatically.

## Credit costs

Media Studio charges AI credits on a **per-generation** basis. The exact cost depends on the modality (image vs. video), the quality tier chosen, the duration (for video), and the engine.

Every generation card shows a **live credit quote** before you confirm — you will always know the cost before committing.

If your balance is lower than the cost of a generation, that card is shown as disabled with a message like "Needs N more credits". Contact your partner to top up your balance.

For detailed pricing by tier and engine, see [Media Studio recipes and tiers](/customer-management/media-studio/long-form-recipes).

## Enabling Media Studio for a customer (partner action)

As a partner, to turn on Media Studio for one of your customers:

1. In your partner portal, open the customer's record.
2. Go to **Features & Add-ons**.
3. Toggle on **Media Studio**.
4. (Optional) Set a daily or monthly generation cap under **AI Credits** to control spend.

The customer sees the Media Studio section in their portal immediately after the toggle is saved.
