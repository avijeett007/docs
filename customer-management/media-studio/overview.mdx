## What the Generative Media Studio is

The **Generative Media Studio** is a full creative suite inside the customer portal. Customers can generate images and videos from text prompts using AI rendering engines, arrange shots into storyboards, and — with the Phase 7 upgrade — produce complete multi-scene films end-to-end using an AI Film Director.

The studio has two main creative paths:

| Path | Best for | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Recipes** | Single assets, branded images, short clips | Pick a recipe, fill in the brief, generate and flip takes |
| **Cinema mode** | Full films, multi-scene productions | Build a storyboard, assign elements, run the Film Agent or an external AI director |

Every generation deducts credits. Partners set credit plans per customer; the studio shows an upfront quote before any charge.

---

## Prerequisites

To enable the Generative Media Studio for a customer:

1. Open **Partner Portal → Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons**.
2. Toggle **Generative Media Studio** on.
3. Assign a credit plan that includes media-studio credits or use the partner's shared pool.

The Studio menu item appears in the customer portal immediately after enabling.

## Studio layout

When a customer opens the Studio, they see:

- **Compose bar** — a text prompt input at the top with a recipe shortcut picker
- **Hero Wall** — a four-column mosaic of muted recipe loops (sample outputs) to inspire generation
- **"Start with a recipe" rail** — category chips (For your business · Ads · Social · Film · Avatars · Product). One tap loads a recipe brief form.
- **"Made with Media Studio" showcase** — a curated wall of real outputs organised by recipe type

## Recipes

A **recipe** is a structured generation workflow. Each recipe has:

- A brief form with labelled inputs (subject, style, colour, etc.)
- A **quality tier** selector: Everyday · Studio · Best · Long-form
- An upfront **credit quote** before any generation starts
- A **take** system — generate multiple variations and flip between them, paying only for the takes you keep

### Quality tiers and duration

| Tier | Engine family | Max duration |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday | Standard | 5 s |
| Studio | Premium | 8–15 s |
| Best | Best-available | 8–15 s |
| Long-form | Seedance 2.5 / 720p | Up to 30 s |

## Credit billing

- Every generation shows a **quote** before the credit charge. Customers see exactly how many credits a take will cost.
- Blocked generations (content policy, copyright) are **refunded** automatically.
- Partners track usage in **Partner Portal → Credits → AI Credits** and in the customer billing panel.

For plan configuration see [Customer credit plans](/partner-portal/settings/customer-credit-plans).
