## What the Media Studio is for

The **Generative Media Studio** is a white-label AI creative suite built into the customer portal. Customers can generate videos and images using state-of-the-art AI models, guided by ready-made recipes that handle prompting and model selection for them.

Partners control access: Media Studio is off by default and enabled per customer through the Features & Add-ons panel (see [Features, add-ons, and menu visibility](/partner-portal/customers/features-addons-and-menu-visibility)).

## What's inside

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Use Cases** | Guided, outcome-first recipes — the recommended starting point for new customers |
| **Create** | Free-form generation: pick a model, write a prompt, hit generate |
| **Projects** | Group generated assets into named projects; browse by project in My Library |
| **My Library** | Every asset a customer has ever generated, searchable and filterable |
| **Characters** | (Coming soon) Consistent character creation across runs |
| **Director** | (Coming soon) Multi-scene sequencing |

## Model catalog

The studio ships with **17 AI models** across video and image generation. All prices are live-verified against the provider's published rates and enforced with a floor guard so a model is never underpriced.

| Category | Example models | Typical use |
|----------|----------------|-------------|
| Video — short clips | Seedance 2.5 (30s), Hailuo, MiniMax H3 | Animated ads, effects, bring-to-life |
| Video — effects | Seedance 2.0 (5s) | Quick transitions, micro-animations |
| Image — generation | Flux 2, Seedream, nano-banana-pro | Marketing images, stills |

Credit costs vary per model and output length. The **Use-Case Studio** shows a live credit quote before any generation starts (see [Use-Case Studio & Recipes](/customer-management/media-studio/use-case-studio)).

## Credits and billing

Media Studio generations are charged in **credits** — the same credit balance the customer uses for voice agents and AI calls.

- Credits are deducted **per step** at submission time (never after).
- Before a run starts, the platform checks whether the customer has enough balance to complete the full recipe chain. If not, the run is blocked.
- Refunds for provider-rejected steps are immediate and exactly-once — a partial failure never double-charges.

## Prerequisites

For a customer to access Media Studio in their portal:

1. The partner must enable the **Media Studio** feature toggle in that customer's **Features & Add-ons** panel.
2. The customer must have sufficient credits for their intended generation.
