## What Media Studio is

**Media Studio** is the creative production workspace available to your customers inside their white-label portal. Customers use it to build generative media projects — storyboards, images, videos, and more — powered by AI.

Partners enable Media Studio for individual customers from **Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons**.

## Key features

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Storyboard canvas](/customer-management/media-studio/storyboard) | Full-screen visual canvas to build and connect cards, scenes, and production notes |
| [Share a storyboard](/customer-management/media-studio/share-storyboard) | Generate an unlisted share link so others can view your storyboard without logging in |
| [Media credits](/customer-management/media-studio/media-credits) | Credit-based billing for AI generation runs — partners control each customer's allowance |

## Prerequisites

Before a customer can access Media Studio:

1. The partner must enable the **Media Studio** toggle in **Customers → Manage → Features & Add-ons**.
2. The customer must have sufficient **media credits** to generate content. Generation is blocked when balance is zero.
3. The customer must have an active portal account.

## Navigating to Media Studio

Customers find Media Studio in their portal sidebar. The exact menu label depends on the portal mode configured by the partner.
