## What the Use-Case Studio is for

The **Use-Case Studio** is the recommended entry point for customers new to AI media generation. Instead of picking a model and writing a prompt from scratch, customers choose an **outcome** (e.g. "make my product image move") and a guided wizard handles the rest.

Behind each use case is a **recipe** — a sequence of model steps with pre-written prompts, tested pricing, and real preview assets generated by running the recipe itself. Customers see exactly what to expect before paying.

| **Bring to Life** | 2 recipes | Animate a still image, make a product photo move |

## Running a recipe — step by step

### 1. Choose a recipe

Open the **Use Cases** tab and browse or search the card gallery. Click any card to enter the wizard.

> 🎬 **Video walkthrough:** _Record a short clip walking through the full Use-Case Studio flow — selecting a recipe, stepping through the wizard, confirming the quote, watching the run tracker, and viewing the completed asset in My Library._

### 2. Follow the wizard steps

The wizard is 3 steps. Each step asks for exactly what the recipe needs — typically:

- **Upload an image** (for bring-to-life or marketing recipes)
- **Describe the output** — a short text prompt for direction or style
- **Confirm and pay** — review the live credit quote and click Generate

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of wizard step 1 — the image upload prompt, with the "faces note" visible for video recipes that animate a still._

**Important — face and likeness note:** Video recipes that animate a still photo will note on the upload step that the same photo used as a still may be rejected by the animation step if it shows a recognisable face. This is a provider safety rule, not a platform error. If your customer's content involves a person, use a non-photoreal illustration or discuss with your account manager.

### 3. Review the credit quote

Before anything is generated, the wizard shows:

| Line item | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Per-step credit cost | Each recipe step, priced individually |
| Total credits | Sum across all steps |
| Your current balance | Whether you have enough to proceed |

If the balance is insufficient, the wizard prompts the customer to top up. The run does **not** start until balance is confirmed.

> 📷 **Screenshot needed:** _Add a screenshot of the wizard credit confirmation screen — per-step breakdown and the "Confirm & Generate" button._

### 4. Watch the run tracker

After confirming, the wizard hands off to the **run tracker** — a live progress view showing each recipe step as it runs. Each step shows:

- **Status**: queued → running → complete (or failed)
- **Progress bar**: determinate per step (not just a spinner)
- **Retry button**: if a step fails, click Retry to re-run only that step without re-charging successful steps

## Pricing model

Recipe prices are based on live-verified rates from the AI provider. The rate for each model is locked at the session start and shown on the quote screen before any charge occurs.

Key guardrails:
- **Pre-run balance check** — the whole chain is checked before step 1 runs; you will not run out of balance mid-recipe
- **Per-step charge at submission** — each step is charged when it starts, not when it finishes
- **Exact-once refunds** — if a provider rejects a step, the credit is refunded exactly once, immediately
