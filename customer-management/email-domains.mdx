## Overview

Email domain setup helps customers send emails from their own domain (rather than a generic sender).

## Setup steps

1. Add the email domain in the customer settings.
2. Copy the DNS records (SPF, DKIM, and any verification records).
3. Add records to the domain’s DNS.
4. Verify inside Knotie AI.

## Troubleshooting

- DKIM is the most common cause of verification issues. Confirm the record value is copied exactly.
