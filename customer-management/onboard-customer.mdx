## Overview

As a partner or agency, onboarding a customer is the first step to setting up their portal experience.

## What you’ll need

- Customer name and contact details
- Any domain/subdomain requirements
- Which portal mode they should use

## Steps

1. Open the customer onboarding flow.
2. Enter the customer’s details.
3. Review portal settings and features.
4. Save and confirm.
